# Classifying-anima identification for the coherent chain object

**11 November 2026 · first mathematical segment after the reconciled fourth audit. Internally verified directly against the definitions in the primary literature; not yet independently audited.** Part of the [ranked post-fourth-audit program](ProjectA_post_fourth_audit_research_priorities_2026-11-11.md). The result is a *definition-level identification with a known construction*, not a priority claim about that construction or an identification with scheme étale homotopy or arbitrary-locale intrinsic shape.

## 0. Main reference and names

Peter J. Haine, *Classifying anima of condensed ∞-categories of points*, arXiv:2602.21330, Example 0.1, Theorem 2.20, Definition 2.10, Observation 2.19, Appendices A.16 and A.35–A.37: https://arxiv.org/html/2602.21330

Haine–Holzschuh–Lara–Mair–Martini–Wolf, *The condensed homotopy type of a scheme*, arXiv:2510.07443, §3.24–3.36 and especially Recollections 3.28–3.30, Definition 3.36 (see actual pdf): https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.07443

**Keep \(\operatorname{Sh}(X)\), the Zariski/spectral sheaf \(\infty\)-topos of a spectral space, separate from \(X_{\mathrm{\acute et}}\), the étale \(\infty\)-topos of a scheme.** Haine et al. use \(\operatorname{Gal}(X_{\mathrm{zar}})=\operatorname{Gal}(\operatorname{Sh}(X))\), where \(\operatorname{Gal}\) is their earlier notation for \(\mathbf{Pt}^{coh}\). For any spectral space \(X\) this is defined independently of a scheme. In their scheme application, the condensed étale homotopy type instead uses \(\operatorname{Gal}(X_{\acute et})\); it is not our chain object.

## 1. The patched specialization category IS the coherent-point category

Let X be a **coherent locale**. Its points constitute a sober spectral topological space \(X_{\mathrm{sp}}\); let \(V=X_{\mathrm{patch}}\) be its Priestley Stone space, equipped with the patch-closed specialization order \(x\le_X y\) given by every spectral open containing x also containing y (equivalently \(x\in\overline{\{y\}}\)). We work on any sufficiently large bounded Stone site \(\mathrm{Stone}_\kappa\) compatible with the fixed earlier sheaf-homotopy framework, or on the standard extremally disconnected subsite for full condensed anima. These restrictions must be matched before stating an equivalence of categories with distinct universes.

**Lemma 1 (families).** For every profinite test space S,
\[
\operatorname{Map}^{qc}(S,X_{\mathrm{sp}})
\ \cong\
\operatorname{Map}_{\mathrm{Stone}}(S,V).
\tag{1.1}
\]
In Haine's precise usage, \(f:S\to X_{\mathrm{sp}}\) is qc if each compact open \(U\subseteq X\) has **clopen** inverse image. The patch topology is generated by compact opens and their complements. Thus patch continuity implies the qc condition, while qc implies patch continuity. This is a natural bijection in S. It is also a bijection of **posets**, since Haine's morphism order is pointwise specialization with exactly our orientation.

**Lemma 2 (nerve in every degree).** Write \(\mathcal P_X(S)=\big(\operatorname{Map}_{\mathrm{Stone}}(S,V),\le_{\mathrm{pointwise}}\big)\). For every n and every Stone S, the n-simplices in the ordinary nerve of this poset are naturally
\[
N_n\mathcal P_X(S)=
\{(f_0,\ldots,f_n)\colon S\to V:\ f_0(s)\le\cdots\le f_n(s)\ \forall s\}
\ \cong\
\operatorname{Map}_{\mathrm{Stone}}(S,\operatorname{Ch}_n(X)_{\mathrm{patch}}).
\tag{1.2}
\]
The order relation is patch closed (Priestley duality); hence \(\operatorname{Ch}_n\) is a closed Stone subset of V^{n+1}, and coordinatewise patch continuity factors uniquely through it. Face and degeneracy maps agree strictly with deletion and repetition of coordinates. This gives an isomorphism of simplicial **sheaves of sets**, not just a pointwise comparison of realizations.

**Theorem (identification).** As condensed 1-categories of posets (or after restriction to our compatible Stone_\kappa site),
\[
\boxed{\quad \mathcal P_X\cong\mathbf{Pt}^{coh}(\operatorname{Sh}(X_{\mathrm{sp}}))
\quad}
\tag{1.3}
\]
naturally in coherent X under spectral maps. In particular
\[
\boxed{\quad
N_\bullet\mathbf{Pt}^{coh}(\operatorname{Sh}(X))
\cong h(\operatorname{Ch}_\bullet(X)_{\mathrm{patch}})
,\qquad
\mathbf C(X)\simeq B\mathbf{Pt}^{coh}(\operatorname{Sh}(X)).
\quad}
\tag{1.4}
\]
**Proof.** Haine 2026 Example 0.1, relying on the earlier Haine et al. 2025 §3.30, identifies the coherent-point category of \(\operatorname{Sh}(X)\) on an extremally disconnected Stone K with the poset \(\operatorname{Map}^{qc}(K,X)\) ordered by specialization. Under Lemma 1 it is our \(\mathcal P_X(K)\). The ordinary nerve is identified by Lemma 2. The classifying-anima functor B is the groupoid completion/realization of the ordinary nerve; in the category of condensed categories, B is the pointwise functor on the extremally disconnected site, sheafifying homotopy colimits as required (Haine Definition/Observation 2.19). The pre-existing \(\mathbf C(X)\) is the geometric realization of **the same simplicial sheaf**, which proves (1.4). For general Stone tests rather than only extremally disconnected tests, both categories are obtained from the same representable functors; restricting to a common sufficiently large site establishes the same formula. Spectral maps are patch-continuous and specialization preserving, and all identifications commute with postcomposition. QED.

**Additional literature consequence.** By Haine 2026 Theorem 2.20 for spectral ∞-topoi (and \(\operatorname{Sh}(X_{\mathrm{sp}})\) is spectral in that sense, by Hochster duality/A.35 and the paper's explicit Example 0.6), inclusion of coherent points into *all* points induces an equivalence on condensed classifying anima. Thus in full condensed anima, with the appropriate restriction convention,
\[
\boxed{\quad
\mathbf C(X)\simeq B\mathbf{Pt}^{coh}(\operatorname{Sh}(X))
\simeq B\mathbf{Pt}(\operatorname{Sh}(X)).
\quad}
\tag{1.5}
\]
**Not an identity of categories:** on an extremally disconnected K and X the Sierpiński space, \(\mathbf{Pt}^{coh}\) gives \(\mathrm{Clopen}(K)\) whereas all points give \(\mathrm{Open}(K)\); a left adjoint (closure) makes their classifying anima equivalent. Do not identify the all-points nerve degreewise with Ch_\bullet; only its **realization** is equivalent.

## 2. Meaning of the existing higher hulls

Let \(\mathcal P_X\) denote this internal poset. Its n-fold **upper witness span** has object
\[
R_n^\uparrow=\{(x_0,\ldots,x_n,u)\in V^{n+2}:x_i\le u\ \forall i\}.
\]
The projection to V^{n+1} has closed Stone image \(I_n(X)_{\rm patch}\). Since the projection is a **surjection of Stone spaces onto its image**, the Yoneda morphism \(h(R_n^\uparrow)\to h(I_n)\) is an effective epimorphism in the site. Hence \(I_n\) is exactly the **sheaf image** of the upper witness span of the coherent-point category, not in general the set of tuples admitting a *globally continuous* upper witness over every test S. Analogously \(D_n\) is the sheaf image of the lower witness span \(R_n^\downarrow\) with a≤x_i. The higher meet hull
\[
M_n=I_n\cap D_n=T(\Delta_{n+1})
\]
is the sheaf-theoretic intersection of the two witness images. In the coherent specialization calculus \(\operatorname{cl}(M_n)=I_n,\operatorname{fit}(M_n)=D_n\); verify or cite this latter closure-fitting identity in the next small editorial addendum, and do not silently assume it for arbitrary locales.

The fourth-audit theorem that \(\mathbf C(X)\to\mathbf M(X)\) is **sharply 2-connective**, and \(\mathsf G_{\mathrm{Ch}}\cong\mathsf G_M\) compatible with I and D, can thus be rephrased as a statement about the **classifying anima of the coherent-point category** and its two-sided witness-completion simplicial relation. It is **not** a statement that the meet hull is itself a new category with a standard nerve.

## 3. Checks and explicit limitations

1. **Order orientation:** Haine's \(f\le g\iff f(s)\in\overline{\{g(s)\}}\) agrees with our convention every open containing f(s) contains g(s). No order reversal.
2. **Coherence is essential for this identification as stated:** all our \(\operatorname{Ch}_n(X)\) are proconstructible patched Stone subspaces, and \(\operatorname{Sh}(X)\) is coherent. For arbitrary noncoherent locales, \(\mathbf{Pt}^{coh}\) need not apply, and the present theorem does not extend automatically.
3. **Point/evaluation distinction:** the full condensed \(\mathbf C(X)\) does not mean \(B(\text{specialization poset of }X)\) with a **constant** sheaf added afterward. The family of quasicompact maps and its topology are essential; for Stone S with equality order \(\mathbf C(S)=h(S)\), not in general a constant discrete set.
4. **Not étale homotopy of a scheme:** for a qcqs scheme X_sch, \(\mathbf C(|X_{\rm sch}|_{\rm spectral})\) corresponds to \(\mathbf{Pt}^{coh}(\operatorname{Sh}(|X|_{\mathrm{zar}}))\). The usual scheme condensed étale homotopy type uses \(\mathbf{Pt}^{coh}(X_{\acute et})\), a different topos. There is a comparison available from Zariski to étale geometric morphisms, but no equivalence is asserted here.
5. **Not yet an intrinsic shape theorem:** the relation with ordinary shape \(\mathrm{Shape}(\operatorname{Sh}(X))\), a pro-space, or intrinsic localic shape has not been established; the present identification is with a specific existing **condensed classifying-anima** construction. It provides an external categorical description, which is valuable, but is no proof that the patched object is the shape of X.
6. **Site size/universe:** an identification in full condensed anima descends by restriction to the same test site, and our pre-existing object is its restriction when the chosen Stone_\kappa site contains the relevant patch spaces and witness powers. This does not prove arbitrary site-size independence or comparison with all possible condensed universes; the common-site condition is part of the statement.
7. **Literature antecedents:** the point-category construction, \(\mathbf{Pt}^{coh}\), its degreewise qc-map description and comparison with \(\mathbf{Pt}\) all originate in the cited works. The theorem here is the **identification of our existing chain construction with theirs**. No priority/new-discovery claim for the general concept.

**Test examples:** for Stone S with equality order, all chain degrees are diagonal h(S) and \(\mathbf C(S)=h(S)\). For finite two-point chain (Sierpiński), \(\mathbf C\simeq1\); Haine's \(\mathrm{Clopen}(K)\subset\mathrm{Open}(K)\) comparison also realizes to a contractible object. For the previously audited pointed triangle W_S(P,v), our realization \(\mathbf C(W)\simeq h(S)_+\wedge\underline{S^1}\) is a concrete nonconstant classifying anima of the coherent points of its spectral sheaf topos.
