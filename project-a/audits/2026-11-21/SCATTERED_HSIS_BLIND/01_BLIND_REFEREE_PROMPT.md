# Independent blind referee prompt
## Point-free topology — scattered point covers and selected-image graph composition

Please perform an independent and *adversarial* mathematical audit of the accompanying submission. We welcome a negative verdict or a sharply restricted positive theorem. Do not defer to assertions in the packet as settled simply because their proofs are written in detail.

The submission asks whether scatteredness is precisely the compact-Hausdorff target-space condition that makes **every** continuous surjection safely adjoinable, with its graph and converse, to the finite point-generated/full rectangular relation calculus in Loc.

### Priority A: point-cover theorem

1. Check the Cantor–Bendixson transfinite induction **for arbitrary locale base changes**, not only ordinary spaces. At successor stages, verify that the new layer is **closed in the appropriate open partial stage** and that its inverse image decomposes into point fibers, even if the test locale is pointless. At limit stages verify the use of joins of **open** sublocales, not arbitrary sublocale joins.
2. Check that a point-surjective e:S→Y actually receives a factor from the discrete point cover. Record exactly where classical choice is used. Test finite T0 examples, the Sierpinski locale, convergent sequences, and high Cantor–Bendixson ranks.
3. For non-scattered Hausdorff Y, check that a nonempty perfect **closed** K exists and that B(K) is nonzero **and pointless**. Confirm that its pullback of Y_disc→Y vanishes. Is compactness needed for this converse?

### Priority B: graph-extension sufficient criterion — essential for target dichotomy

4. Check carefully the assertion f^{-1}([B])=[f^{-1}(B)] for continuous compact-Hausdorff f. The proposed proof uses a closed graph and the theorem that a product of a spatial locale with a compact-Hausdorff spatial locale is spatial. 5. For p(A,E)=[A×E], verify **exact**, not merely bounded, p-rectangle actions when composing with Γ_f, its converse, and kernel pair K_f=X×_Y X. 7. Verify the full typed graph multiplication table, including K_f²=K_f, inverse graphs, identities, and type-incompatible zero products. For generator triples RRR, RRL, LRR, RLR, RLL, LLR, LRL, LLL check associativity and the nonzero-overlap reciprocity. Test zero and empty-set cases. Is closure under finite joins enough, or is an inadmissible infinitary interchange hidden?

### Priority C: compact-Hausdorff dichotomy and negative witness
