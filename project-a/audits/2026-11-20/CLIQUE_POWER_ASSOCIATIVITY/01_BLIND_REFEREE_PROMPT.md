## Mathematical problem

A point-generated complete ordinary graph on a spatial sublocale U need not be the whole localic rectangle U×U. Localic relational composition is defined by genuine pullback followed by image, and is not assumed associative in general. The authors propose that finite joins of point-generated cliques nevertheless have **power-associative** composition on compact metrizable Hausdorff locales, with a precise overlap-graph formula for every length at least two.

The principal claims in 02_REPAIRED_THEOREM_AND_PROOF.md are:

- **(G)**: For arbitrary induced spatial endpoint sublocales of compact metric spaces, their localic product is generated by ordinary endpoint point pairs and Booleanizations of suitable two-colour compact Cantor graphs.
- **(B)**: If two point-generated bipartite relations have independently dense subsets of the same compact metric middle space, their localic composite is the **full** endpoint product, even when it is nonspatial.
- **(C2)**: Two point-generated component cliques compose to the full support rectangle exactly when the spatial support sublocales have nonzero localic intersection.
- **(PA)**: Every fully parenthesized power k≥2 of a finite join of component cliques equals the join of the full support rectangles whose overlap-graph distance is at most k−1, independently of bracketing.
- **(5V)**: The particular three relations [I_0×Q_1], [I_1×Q_2], [I_2×Q_3] from the five-vertex overlap path yield equal full outer products under either threefold bracketing.
