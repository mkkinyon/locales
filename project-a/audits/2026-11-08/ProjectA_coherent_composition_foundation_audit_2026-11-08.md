## 1. Scope and statement

Let `X` be a **coherent** locale, `P=pt(X)` its spectral point space and `P_patch` its compact Hausdorff patch space. A relation `R↪X×X` is *proconstructible spectral* here if it is the sublocale determined by a **patch-closed subset** `R_pt⊆P×P` equipped with the spectral subspace topology. All maps used in the proof are spectral (preimages of compact opens are compact open). The same argument works for relations among several spectral locales.

**Theorem.** If `R,S` are proconstructible spectral relations on `X`, their image-defined localic composite `R∘S` is again proconstructible spectral and

```
pt(R∘S) = {(x,z)∈P² : ∃y∈P, (x,y)∈R_pt and (y,z)∈S_pt}.
```

The class is closed under converse, finite sublocale meets and joins, diagonal identity, and binary localic composition. **Composition is associative within this class**, and composition distributes over finite joins. This is a restricted associative calculus; it does **not** imply that `Loc` is regular or that arbitrary sublocalic relations compose associatively. The phrase "relation algebra" should be avoided if meant to imply closure under Boolean complement: a patch-closed set need not have patch-closed complement.

## 2. Lemma A: ideal completion turns lattice pushouts into frame pushouts

Write `DLat` for bounded distributive lattices with bounded lattice homomorphisms and `Frm` for frames with frame homomorphisms. If `K∈DLat`, its frame of (order) ideals `Idl(K)` satisfies the universal property

```
Frm(Idl K,L) ≅ DLat(K,U L)
```

naturally in `K` and in the frame `L`. The extension of `h:K→U L` is explicitly `ĥ(J)=⋁_{k∈J}h(k)`. Arbitrary joins are preserved because joins of ideals are generated by finite joins of their members. Finite meets are preserved because

```
(⋁_{i∈I}h(i))∧(⋁_{j∈J}h(j))
  = ⋁_{i∈I,j∈J} h(i∧j)
  = ⋁_{k∈I∩J}h(k).
```

The reverse map restricts a frame homomorphism to the principal ideals `↓k`. Thus `Idl` is left adjoint to the underlying bounded distributive lattice functor and preserves pushouts.

For a coherent locale `X`, `O(X)≅Idl(K(X))`, where `K(X)` is its bounded distributive lattice of compact opens. Consequently **the frame pushout of coherent frames along coherent frame maps is the ideal completion of the corresponding lattice pushout**. Taking `Loc` opposites: pullbacks of coherent locales along spectral maps are coherent, and their spectral point spaces are the ordinary pointwise fiber products. To see the *pointwise* assertion directly, a point of `Spec(K_A ⨿_{K_Z} K_B)` is a bounded lattice map from the pushout to `2`, equivalently a pair of prime filters on `K_A,K_B` agreeing on `K_Z`. The compact-open generators coming from the two sides induce the usual fiber-product subspace topology.

**Important boundary:** this does not establish pullback stability for arbitrary localic surjections. It applies to the full *coherent/spectral-map subcategory*, with finite limits calculated compatibly in `Loc`.

## 3. Lemma B: patch-closed point subsets give the required spectral sublocales

Let `Z` be spectral, and `S⊆pt(Z)` patch closed. Then `S` is compact Hausdorff in the induced patch topology. The spectral subspace topology on `S` is spectral. In particular, for every compact open `k⊆Z`, the trace `k∩S` is compact open in `S` (patch compactness), and every compact open of `S` is a **finite union** of such traces. Therefore

```
K(Z) → K(S),   k↦k∩S
```

is a surjective bounded distributive lattice map; the induced map of frames `O(Z)→O(S)` is likewise surjective, so the spectral inclusion `S→Z` is an honest localic sublocale embedding. Its spectrum has **exactly** the stated points: `S` is spectral/sober, so no extra sobrification points appear.

The same statements apply to every patch-closed subspace of `P^n`. Pullbacks or intersections of such sublocales along **spectral maps** correspond, by Lemma A, to the ordinary pullbacks/intersections of their patch-closed point subsets. This is the missing point-to-locale justification for the composable-triples source, not merely a claim that two sets of points intersect.

*Direct citation check:* the standard theorem that proconstructible subsets of spectral spaces are exactly spectral subspaces whose inclusions are spectral is stated in Dickmann–Schwartz–Tressl, *Spectral Spaces*, §1.9 / spectral-subspace discussions. The preceding compact-open argument supplies the property we actually need and is independent of a blanket "every fitted sublocale is spatial" assertion (which is false).

## 4. Lemma C: image of a spectral map is its patch-closed set-theoretic image

Let `f:T→Z` be a **spectral map** of spectral spaces, and give its point image `C=f(T)` the subspace topology. The induced patch map is continuous, the patch space of `T` is compact, and the patch space of `Z` is Hausdorff. Hence `C` is patch closed in `Z`, so Lemma B applies: it defines a spectral sublocale `C↪Z`.

The factorization `T→C→Z` has `T→C` onto ordinary points. Because `C` is **spatial**, being onto points implies that its locale inverse-image homomorphism is **injective**: if two opens of `C` differ, an ordinary point distinguishes them, and some preimage point witnesses the difference. Thus `T→C` is a locale surjection, and the **actual localic image** of `f` is the proconstructible sublocale `C`.

This is the exact location where **spatiality of a specific proconstructible target** is needed. "Onto points" is not a general-purpose substitute for localic surjectivity when a target may have a pointless residual.

## 5. Binary composition and associativity

Take proconstructible `R,S⊆P²`. The pointwise composable-triple set

```
E={(x,y,z)∈P³:(x,y)∈R_pt and (y,z)∈S_pt}
```

is patch closed in `P³`. The corresponding sublocale is **exactly** the localic pullback/intersection `R_12∧S_23` by Lemmas A–B. The projection `E→P²`, `(x,y,z)↦(x,z)`, is spectral, and by Lemma C its localic image is the spectral sublocale on the patch-closed pointwise image. This proves the theorem's binary formula.

For three relations `R,S,T`, each bracketing has ordinary point relation

```
{(x,w):∃y,z, x R y, y S z, z T w}.
```

Both bracketed localic composites remain proconstructible, so equality of these point sets implies **equality of the actual sublocales**. Therefore `(R∘S)∘T=R∘(S∘T)` for such `R,S,T`. The binary composite formula also implies distributivity over finite sublocale joins: the finite join of proconstructible sublocales is represented by the finite union of their patch-closed point sets. (This uses their spatiality; it is not claimed for arbitrary localic relations.)

## 6. Consequences for the named relations in Project A

The canonical specialization relation `A` on `X` has point relation `≤` on `P`; its converse is `B`. The Priestley specialization order is patch closed, so both are proconstructible spectral. The externally audited coherent point criteria give

```
I_pt={(x,y):∃z x≤z≥y} = (A∘B)_pt,
D_pt={(x,y):∃z x≥z≤y} = (B∘A)_pt.
```

The previously checked coherent descriptions identify `I,D` with the **corresponding spatial spectral sublocales**. By the composition theorem, therefore,

```
I=A∘B,     D=B∘A
```

as **genuine localic sublocales**. This is a new **internal proof reconciliation** of master-ledger entry C02, not a separate blind referee verdict.

Furthermore the alternating formulas for the mixed products and finite powers are now legitimate **within this class**:

```
I∘D=A∘B∘A,           D∘I=B∘A∘B;
I²=A∘B∘A∘B=U,       D²=B∘A∘B∘A=V.
```

Here `U=I²` and `V=D²` also have separate earlier coherent audits; this argument discharges their **specific associativity dependency**, not their entire proof histories. Finite joins remain in the safe class, so the coherent comparability-word identities, including `J²=U∨V`, are legitimate localic equalities. Coherent finite compositions used in `H`'s spectral graph calculus are safe **at successor stages**; at limit stages the patch closure is essential, and cannot be replaced by a bare sublocale join.

**Nothing here justifies:** arbitrary-locale `I=AB` or `D=BA`; arbitrary association of `I,D`; association of `D³` for Arrieta's noncoherent spatial base; or image/base-change identities with fitted sublocales that are not spectral/proconstructible. The independent negative mixed-composition theorem remains unaffected.
