## 1. Intrinsic criterion for the safe class — independently checked

Write `O(Z)=Idl(K)` for a coherent locale and let `q:O(Z)→O(S)` be a *surjective* frame homomorphism representing a sublocale. The following are equivalent classically:

1. `S` is the proconstructible/spectral sublocale associated with a patch-closed set of points of `Z`.
2. `q` sends compact elements to compact elements.
3. `q=Idl(h)` under suitable identifications, for a surjective bounded distributive lattice homomorphism `h:K→K'`.

Key check of (2)⇒(3): any `x∈O(S)` has a preimage `u=∨_{k≤u}k`, so `x=∨_{k≤u}q(k)`. A *compact* `x` equals a finite join of `q(k)` with `q(k)≤x`; hence every compact of `O(S)` lies in `q[K]`. These compacts generate `O(S)` and are closed under finite joins and meets. Thus `O(S)=Idl(q[K])` and `q=Idl(q|_K)`. Conversely the lattice quotient gives a spectral subspace whose point set is the patch-closed set of congruence-saturated prime filters. **One must not substitute the bare condition “same point set.”**

The class is thus intrinsically frame-theoretic; its sublocale order is reflected by inclusion of the associated patch-closed sets. The class is closed under arbitrary sublocale meets and finite joins, *not* arbitrary joins.

## 2. Correct categorical content of Theorem T

For spectral maps of coherent locales, `Idl:DLat→Frm` is left adjoint to the underlying bounded-lattice functor and therefore takes DLat pushouts to actual frame pushouts. This yields *true Loc pullbacks*, even when universality is tested against arbitrary locales. The prime-filter description of a lattice pushout identifies the spectral point-space pullback with the ordinary topological fiber product.

A patch-closed subset `S` of a spectral space is spectral with compact-open lattice given by traces of the ambient compact opens. Its inclusion is the `Idl` of a *surjective* lattice homomorphism, so it represents a genuine spectral sublocale. Under spectral base change, the pullback retains this form. The composable pair relation is also the actual triple-coordinate meet, by its universal property in `Loc`.

For a spectral map `f:T→Z`, the point image `C=f(T)` is patch-closed. Factor `f:T→C↪Z`. Because `C` is spatial and the first map is onto **points of C**, the frame map `O(C)→O(T)` is injective; hence the first factor is a locale surjection. Thus `im_Loc(f)` equals the *constructed* spectral sublocale `C`. This is why the invalid general inference “spatial source and closed image imply exact image points” is avoided.

The composite of two proconstructible relations is therefore the proconstructible sublocale on their set-theoretic relational composite. Associativity, converse, the unit, and **finite** distributivity follow inside this class. This establishes the desired categorical repair without claiming associativity in all of `Loc`.

## 3. Repaired special identifications

### Specialization relation A

The defining inequalities `π₁*u≤π₂*u` are generated by the inequalities for compact opens `k`. Quotienting `K(X×X)` by the corresponding bounded lattice congruence yields a **compact-preserving frame quotient** by the `Idl` adjunction. For `B=A^smile`, likewise. The order is `x≤y ⇔ x∈cl{y}`, so spectral opens are up-sets.

### Fitted diagonal D

This is the most important repair. For a compact sublocale `S` of a coherent locale `Z=Idl K`, let `Φ_S={k∈K:S⊆o(k)}`. Compactness of `S` makes every ambient open neighborhood of `S` contain a compact-open neighborhood from `Φ_S`, so `fit S=⋂_{k∈Φ_S}o(k)`.

The frame congruence of the latter intersection is generated by the lattice congruence

`a ≡_Φ b ⇔ (∃f∈Φ_S) a∧f=b∧f`.

For the nontrivial inclusion: if `u∧k=v∧k` and `a≤u` is compact, compactness of `a∧k` and algebraicity of `v` yield a compact `b≤v` with `a∧k≤b∧k`. Thus `a∧k=(a∧b)∧k`, so `a≡_Φ a∧b`; after joining over compact `a≤u` and using convexity of frame-congruence classes, `u` is equivalent to `u∧v`, and similarly `v`. Hence the open congruence for each `k∈Φ_S` is contained in the lattice-generated congruence. The reverse containment follows from the definition of `≡_Φ`. No unjustified point-set identification is used.

Therefore `fit S≅Loc(Idl(K/≡_Φ))` as an *embedded spectral sublocale*. This applies even when the compact `S` is not spatial. When `S` is spatial proconstructible, its fitted hull has point set the upward specialization saturation of the point set of `S`. In particular `Δ` and `I=clΔ` are compact, so `D=fitΔ` and `V=fit I` belong to the safe class. For spectral points, `I` is the common-upper-bound relation, `D` the common-lower-bound relation.

### Exact identities

For coherent `X`, as equalities of **actual localic sublocales**:

`I=AB`, `D=BA`, `I D=ABA`, `D I=BAB`, `I²=ABAB`, `D²=BABA=V`.

Define `C=A∨B` and `J=I∨D`. Then `C²=J`, `C³=ABA∨BAB`, `C⁴=I²∨D²`, `J²=I²∨D²`, and the familiar two alternating-word formula for each finite `C^n`. Since the **separate earlier** coherent theorem gives `U=cl(D)`, the new hull formula below gives `U=ABAB=I²`, whence `C⁴=J²=U∨V`. The blind packet itself did not define `U`, and the referee correctly refrained from claiming to have checked that separate input.

## 4. New uniform hull identities — accepted as referee-origin

For any proconstructible spectral `R⊆X²` on a coherent locale, **both** `cl R` and `fit R` are proconstructible: the closure is a closed sublocale of a coherent locale; the fitting is covered by the compact-open-filter congruence lemma since `R` is compact. On ordinary points, their sets are the downward and upward coordinatewise specialization saturations respectively. Using associativity and pointwise representation,

`cl(R)=A∘R∘B`,  `fit(R)=B∘R∘A`.

This gives a new unifying proof of `I=AB`, `D=BA`, `V=D²`, and, using the independently established `U=cl D`, `U=I²`. It applies only when `R` belongs to the proconstructible class. Limit stages of transfinite hulls cannot be treated as bare unions, because infinite sublocale joins may leave the class.

## 5. Cautions and limitations in the supplementary suggestions

1. **Constructive extension:** §6.2/§7.2 of the report proposes a proof inside `DLat^op`, but explicitly leaves the constructive regularity/amalgamation step unchecked. The classical theorem is established; the constructive generalization is *not* independently certified here.
2. **Stably compact extension:** §7.1 is explicitly speculative and needs new finite-limit/image results. Defer.
3. **Allegory formulation:** §7.3 is a plausible structural reformulation and its modular law is compatible with pointwise relation theory, but it was not part of the technical packet and is not needed for our claims. Do not attach its proposed general name or consequences to an audited theorem without a separate precise definition/check.
4. **Word reductions:** §7.4's wording “two composites agree iff the words agree after cancellation by A²=A, B²=B” must be read as a *formal sufficient test*, not an iff for any **fixed** coherent locale. For a one-point locale `A=B=Δ`, all the words coincide, including formally different alternating words. A universal-if-and-only-if claim would require a separate free-relation argument. This is a small overstatement in a supplementary discussion, not a flaw in the main report.
5. **Priority:** standard Stone/Priestley duality and the compact-open filter presentation are not claimed novel. The referee could not verify the numbering of two bibliographic references; citation cleanup remains.
6. **Examples:** the `Spec ℤ` example of a patch-closed nonclosed `D` is consistent with the formulas. The `ω+1` example demonstrates failure of arbitrary-join preservation. Neither establishes new broad noncoherent identities.
7. **Transfinite H:** successor steps in the proconstructible class are safe; limits demand a patch-closure mechanism. Do not change the old `H` proof's limit convention.
