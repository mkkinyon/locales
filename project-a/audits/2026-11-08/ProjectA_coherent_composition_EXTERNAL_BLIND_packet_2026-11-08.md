# Project A — Blind technical packet: the coherent proconstructible composition calculus

**8 November 2026. Scope:** one mathematical dependency in Project A: whether the localic, image-defined composition of proconstructible relations on coherent locales is associative and calculated by ordinary spectral points, and whether this validates the alternating-word identities for the canonical diagonal hulls. This document states the candidate theorems and their proofs for independent examination. **No correctness verdict or priority claim is implied.**

## 0. Problem, conventions, and claims to audit

`Loc = Frm^op`. For a locale `X` write `L=O(X)`; `Δ_X↪X×X` denotes the image of the diagonal. A **coherent** locale is one with `O(X)` compact and algebraic and with its compact elements closed under finite meets. Classically `X` is equivalent to the sober spectral point space `P=pt(X)`; `K=K(L)` is the bounded distributive lattice of compact opens and `L≅Idl(K)`. The compact Hausdorff **patch** (constructible) topology on `P` is generated by compact opens and their complements.

A locale map between coherent locales is **spectral/coherent** here if its frame inverse image preserves compact opens. The spectral point map is the corresponding map of spectral spaces. A **proconstructible spectral sublocale** of a coherent locale `Z` means the localic sublocale determined by a **patch-closed subset** `S⊆pt(Z)` furnished with the spectral subspace topology. This definition is *not* intended to include arbitrary sublocales with the same underlying points.

Write the diagrammatic localic relation composite (first `R`, then `S`):

```
R ∘ S := im( R ×_Y S → X×Z )
```

for `R↪X×Y`, `S↪Y×Z`, where the image and pullback are computed in the **entire category `Loc`**, not merely in a subcategory of coherent locales. For endorelations on `X`, equivalently take `R_12 ∧ S_23 ↪ X³` and image under `π_13`.

**Principal claim T.** For coherent locales `X,Y,Z` and proconstructible spectral relations `R↪X×Y`, `S↪Y×Z`, their localic composite is proconstructible spectral and is exactly the sublocale on the ordinary pointwise relational composite:

```
pt(R ∘ S) = { (x,z) : ∃y, (x,y)∈pt(R), (y,z)∈pt(S) }.
```

Within this class, localic composition is associative, has the diagonal as identity, is compatible with converse, and distributes over **finite** sublocale joins. No Boolean-complement claim is made.

**Application C.** For a coherent `X`, the canonical specialization-order sublocale `A` (defined in §5), its converse `B`, `I:=cl Δ`, `D:=fit Δ`, `U` (Urysohn-type relation), and `V:=fit I` have the coherent identifications described in §5. In particular `I=A∘B`, `D=B∘A`, and the mixed/alternating powers may be calculated associatively in the safe class. Verification of the named-relation identification is a *separate* obligation from T.

### Why this question arose

The corresponding argument **cannot be justified by a blanket claim that relation composition is associative in `Loc`**. Localic images are defined using frame congruences; locale surjections are not generally pullback-stable. Thus a surjection onto the first two-step image may fail to remain surjective when pulled back against a third relation. Even reflexive symmetric endorelations can have different bracketings. The claim here is only a repair for coherent **spectral/proconstructible** relations.

## 1. Lemma A — coherent pullbacks are real `Loc` pullbacks

Let `DLat` be bounded distributive lattices and bounded homomorphisms, and `Frm` frames and frame homomorphisms. The ideal completion functor is left adjoint to the underlying lattice functor:

```
Frm(Idl K, L) ≅ DLat(K, U L).
```

Given a bounded lattice map `h:K→L`, its unique extension is

```
ĥ(J) = ⋁{h(k): k∈J},    J∈Idl K.
```

It preserves joins because an ideal join consists of elements below finite joins of members. It preserves finite meets because

```
(⋁_{i∈I}h(i))∧(⋁_{j∈J}h(j))
  = ⋁_{i,j}h(i∧j) = ⋁_{k∈I∩J}h(k).
```

Therefore `Idl` preserves colimits, including pushouts and coequalizers. Every coherent-frame homomorphism preserving compact elements is the ideal completion of a bounded lattice homomorphism between compact elements. It follows that the frame pushout of coherent frames along coherent maps is the ideal completion of the **bounded distributive lattice pushout**. Dually, the `Loc` pullback of coherent locales along spectral maps is coherent and is the ordinary spectral-space fiber product.

For the last assertion, a point of the lattice pushout `K_A ⊔_{K_Z} K_B` is a bounded lattice map into `2`, equivalently a pair of prime-filter points of `K_A,K_B` agreeing on `K_Z`; the compact-open generators from each factor give the fiber-product topology. The same argument handles finite products and intersections of spectral sublocales.

**Audit task A:** Verify every categorical identification, including the assertion that the pullback calculated by `Idl` is the pullback in **all** of `Loc`, and that the pointwise spectral pullback has the correct induced topology. Identify whether any unmentioned map is required to be spectral.

## 2. Lemma B — patch-closed sets and actual spectral sublocales

Let `Z` be a spectral space and let `S⊆Z` be patch closed. The patch topology on `S` is compact Hausdorff, and the spectral subspace topology on `S` is spectral (hence sober). For each compact open `k` of `Z`, its trace `k∩S` is compact open in `S`: it is open, and patch compactness makes it quasi-compact in the coarser spectral topology.

The traces of compact opens give a basis for `S`. An arbitrary compact open `c` of `S` is covered by such traces, so compactness reduces the cover to finitely many. Consequently

```
K(Z) → K(S),    k ↦ k∩S,
```

is a **surjective bounded distributive lattice homomorphism** (finite unions of traces are traces of finite unions). Restriction of opens `O(Z)→O(S)` is then a surjective frame homomorphism, so `S→Z` is a genuine localic sublocale inclusion. The locale has exactly the points of `S`, with no additional sobrification points.

Pulling a proconstructible inclusion back along a spectral map gives another such inclusion: the inverse image of a patch-closed subset is patch closed, and Lemma A identifies the resulting `Loc` pullback with the spectral fiber product. The intersection of finitely many proconstructible sublocales is likewise represented by the intersection of the patch-closed point sets.

Finite **joins** of these sublocales are represented by finite **unions** of patch-closed point sets: the finite union is patch closed and spectral, and the map from the disjoint union of the spectral subspaces onto their union is point-surjective, hence a locale surjection onto that spatial target. As a result, the finite union is the smallest sublocale containing the pieces.

**Audit task B:** Check the spectrality of `S`, surjectivity on compact opens, the *precise* meaning of the sublocale determined by `S`, the pullback identification and the finite-join statement. Do not confuse arbitrary joins of sublocales with unions of point sets.

## 3. Lemma C — the localic image of a spectral map

Let `f:T→Z` be a spectral map of spectral spaces. It is continuous for the respective patch topologies, and `T_patch` is compact while `Z_patch` is Hausdorff. Thus its ordinary image `C=f(T)` is **patch closed**. By Lemma B, `C` equipped with the spectral subspace topology defines a spatial spectral sublocale `C↪Z`.

Factor `f` as `T→C→Z`. The first map is surjective on **ordinary points**. As `C` is spatial, this implies injectivity of the inverse-image frame map `O(C)→O(T)`: whenever two opens of `C` differ, a point of `C` distinguishes them and an ordinary point of `T` above it distinguishes their pullbacks. Hence `T→C` is a **locale surjection**; the localic image of `f` is exactly `C↪Z`.

**Audit task C:** Verify that the point-image `C` has its correct spectral subspace topology, that `T→C` is spectral, and that this really is the *categorical localic image*. Compare with the invalid inference “a spatial source plus a closed localic image gives image points equal to point images,” which does **not** hold in general. Explain why the present factorization avoids that error.

## 4. Proof of principal claim T

Let `R⊆pt(X×Y)` and `S⊆pt(Y×Z)` be patch closed, represented by their spectral sublocales. In the patch space of `X×Y×Z`, the composable-triples set

```
E = {(x,y,z): (x,y)∈R, (y,z)∈S}
```

is patch closed (inverse images and intersections of patch-closed sets). Lemmas A and B show that the **actual** localic pullback `R×_Y S` is represented by this spectral space `E`; this is not merely an equality of sets of points.

The endpoint projection `E→X×Z` is spectral (restriction of a spectral projection). By Lemma C its **localic image** is exactly the proconstructible sublocale represented by the patch-closed set

```
{(x,z): ∃y, (x,y)∈R, (y,z)∈S}.
```

This establishes the binary formula. Every intermediate relation in a finite composite remains proconstructible. Consequently both parenthesizations of three relations have exactly the same point set

```
{(x,w): ∃y,z, (x,y)∈R, (y,z)∈S, (z,w)∈T}.
```

Since **these particular** sublocales are spatial spectral sublocales defined by their patch-closed point sets, they are equal. This proves associativity. The finite-join property from Lemma B and ordinary distributivity of pointwise relation composition give binary finite distributivity. The diagonal and converse behave as expected.

## 5. Application to the Project A relations: independent obligation

For coherent `X` with spectral point space `P`, write `x≤y` for specialization in the convention `x∈cl{y}` (equivalently: every open containing `x` contains `y`). Let `A↪X²` be the **localic specialization relation** determined universally by the inequalities

```
π_1^*(u) ≤ π_2^*(u)   for all u∈O(X).
```

Concretely it is the intersection of the sublocales `[u_1 ≤ u_2] := c(u_1)∨o(u_2)`. Put `B=A^smile`. The point sets are the graph of `≤` and its converse. This graph is patch closed, because violation at a pair is witnessed by a compact open `k` with `x∈k`, `y∉k`, yielding a patch-open rectangle `k×(P\k)`.

The **additional localic claim** is that this *presented* `A` coincides with the proconstructible spectral sublocale having that point set. A proposed proof: because every open is a join of compact opens, it suffices to impose the inequalities on compact-open generators. Each such inequality is a bounded lattice relation `k_1∨k_2=k_2` between compact opens of the coherent square. The resulting frame quotient is the ideal completion of the corresponding bounded distributive lattice quotient by Lemma A's adjunction/coequalizer property. Hence the presented `A` is coherent and spatial, and equality of its spectral point set with the patch-closed order graph identifies the localic sublocale. **Please audit this extra argument rather than assuming that point sets suffice.**

The named relations are:

- `I := cl(Δ)` (closed inseparability hull).
- `D := fit(Δ)` (least fitted sublocale containing the diagonal; the intersection of all open sublocales containing it).
- `U` the Urysohn-type closed relation, and `V := fit(I)`.
- `J := I∨D` (sublocale join).

The **coherent point identities** asserted are

```
I_pt = { (x,y): ∃z, x≤z≥y } = (AB)_pt;
D_pt = { (x,y): ∃z, x≥z≤y } = (BA)_pt.
```

For `I`, compact-open neighborhoods and patch compactness yield the common-upper criterion. For `D`, the fitted-diagonal open-cover criterion yields common-lower specializations on spectral points. The **critical additional sublocale claim** is that `I` and `D` are the corresponding proconstructible spectral sublocales, not possibly larger pointless fitted sublocales. `I` is closed in a coherent spectral square; for `D`, a proposed justification uses the compact-fitted-sublocale/Hofmann–Mislove correspondence, or the coherent compact-open-filter congruence calculation: a filter of compact opens gives a spectral quotient `Idl(K/θ)` with `a≡b` iff `a∧f=b∧f` for some filter member `f`. Please verify exactly the required theorem and hypotheses, including whether fitting the **compact** diagonal is indeed compact fitted and whether the filtered intersection has the claimed spatial/spectral representation.

**Conditional on those identifications and T**, we obtain as genuine localic equalities

```
I = A∘B,                     D = B∘A;
I∘D = A∘B∘A,                D∘I = B∘A∘B;
I² = A∘B∘A∘B,               D² = B∘A∘B∘A.
```

Here `A²=A`, `B²=B`. The previously established coherent relations `U=I²` and `V=D²` are treated as *separate mathematical inputs*: this audit is asked to check the categorical validity of their alternating-word consequences, not reprove every earlier separation theorem. Thus `U=ABAB`, `V=BABA` conditional on these inputs.

Let `C=A∨B` (comparability relation). For coherent `X`, finite joins and composition stay in the proconstructible calculus, so purely from reflexivity and idempotence:

```
C² = AB∨BA = J;
C³ = ABA∨BAB;
C⁴ = ABAB∨BABA = U∨V;
J² = U∨V.
```

More generally for `n≥1`, `C^(2n)=(AB)^n∨(BA)^n`; `C^(2n+1)` is the join of the two fully alternating words of length `2n+1`. The latter statements depend on finite distributivity and ordinary insertion of reflexive factors. **Check them carefully**, including `J²`; do not silently assume an identity follows from associativity alone.

## 6. Exact limitations and suggested test cases

The following statements are **not** claimed:

- Image-defined composition is associative for all sublocales of a locale, including arbitrary symmetric reflexive relations.
- Arbitrary closed relations on a noncompact locale form the same associative class.
- The named relations on *noncoherent* locales satisfy `I=AB`, `D=BA`, or associative mixed words.
- A pointwise identity automatically identifies nonspatial localic images or fitted hulls.
- The proconstructible class is closed under Boolean complement or arbitrary sublocale joins.
- An arbitrary transfinitely indexed union of patch-closed relations is patch closed; in formal-hull iterations, limit stages require separate patch closure.

Possible tests:

1. A finite upper-set Alexandrov poset: all relevant sublocales are point-determined and composition is ordinary relation composition.
2. A spectral space with nonclosed specialization fibers, to ensure the patch-closed subspace argument is genuinely used rather than ordinary topological closedness.
3. A patch-closed subset that is not an ordinary topologically closed subset, especially when checking the status of `D`.
4. A noncoherent compact `T_1` locale, where `A=B=Δ` on ordinary points but `I` may be nontrivial. This should not refute the *coherent* assertion; it probes the boundary.
