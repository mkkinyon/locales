### Theorems now supported

Let X,Y be arbitrary locales, let E=c(a) be a closed sublocale of X×Y, and p1:E→X and p2:E→Y its projections. Write r_E=(p2)_* p1^*. Then

    r_E preserves NONEMPTY directed joins  ⇔  p2 is proper.

Here *proper* means (p2)_* preserves nonempty directed joins AND obeys the closed Frobenius law. No continuity, coherence, spatiality, compactness, separation, or transitivity assumptions are needed. The proof is choice-free and point-free in impredicative frame/elementary-topos mathematics, **conditional on the standard constructive pullback-stability theorem for proper maps**; a predicative/CZF proof is not claimed.

The auxiliary Theorem K asserts that for the upper powerlocale W=P_U X, the sublocale

    K = ⋂_{u∈O(X)} [1⊠□u ≤ u⊠1]  ↪  X×W

has a proper projection K→W. [s≤t] denotes the largest sublocale satisfying s≤t, given by c(s)∨o(t).

## 1. The actual error and the minimal repair

The original packet's coverage presentation C is correct: O(X×P_U X) comprises **C-ideals** e⊆L×L, where L=O(X), subject to downclosure, arbitrary first-coordinate joins (including the empty join), and inhabited directed second-coordinate joins. Hence (0,v)∈e for every v. However the packet claims all ordinary principal downsets ↓(s,u) are C-ideals, and identifies s⊠□u with ↓(s,u). This is FALSE unless u=1: ↓(s,u) omits (0,v) for v≰u. The correct generator is the **C-ideal generated by** (s,u), which we denote ⟨s,u⟩_C, and

    s⊠□u ≤ e  ⇔  (s,u)∈e

for each C-ideal e. The special ↓(u,1) *is* a C-ideal.

In Lemma 1, Step 3 the original computation k(↓(1,u)) is therefore ill-typed. The nucleus k=e↦e^K is defined on C-ideals, and ↓(1,u) need not be one. Replace the defective step by

    k(1⊠□u) ≤ k(u⊠1)
      ⇔ 1⊠□u ≤ k(u⊠1)                       [k a nucleus]
      ⇔ (1,u)∈k(↓(u,1))                     [generator membership]
      ⇔ 1∧u≤u,                              [k(↓(u,1))={(s,v):s∧v≤u}]

which holds. This shows the sublocale S_k determined by k satisfies all inequalities defining K, closing precisely the gap. The rest of Lemma 1 uses only generator membership, not the invalid principal-ideal equality. Other occurrences of ↓(1,d) in the Frobenius proof are explicitly used as **subsets whose C-ideal closure** is the generator 1⊠□d, and remain legitimate.

The surviving formula is

    (π_K)_*(i_K^*(e)) = Ψ(e) = ⋁{□u : (u,u)∈e}.

This must replace the erroneous text wherever a final proof is produced. Do not silently edit the historical referee-origin candidate packet.

## 3. An additional issue in the referee's introductory plausibility check

The preliminary spatial sketch (§1(a) of the second referee report) says that E_y⊆U implies y∈r_E(U). **This implication is not valid in general** because r_E(U)=int{y:E_y⊆U}. It holds once the desired upper-semicontinuity is known, so using it to prove compactness would be circular. This does NOT affect the referee's scrutiny of the presented point-free proof in §§2–6, which never makes this inference.

A correct topological proof of compact fibers, already given in the first blind referee report, is: for directed opens (U_i) with E_y⊆⋃U_i, the closed fiber E_y makes V_i=U_i∪(X\E_y) an open directed cover of X; then r_E(X)=Y=⋃r_E(V_i), so y∈r_E(V_i) for some i and E_y⊆U_i. The rest of the topological result is independently established in that earlier report.
