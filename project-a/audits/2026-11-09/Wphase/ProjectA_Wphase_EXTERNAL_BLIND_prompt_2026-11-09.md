## Background and motivation

Project A studies localic separation relations on the diagonal in a locale square. After a long, separately reviewed investigation of finite-cover approximations, we deliberately **deferred** the open problems Q and Q′ and began treating

\[
\mathcal W=\operatorname{cl}(\operatorname{fit}\Delta)

Over several research segments we obtained Boolean-valued semantics, new fittedness criteria, explicit noncoherent examples, an exact condition for \(\mathcal W=\mathcal H\wedge\mathcal U\), and a particularly striking construction of a **connected, compact, noncoherent locale** realizing the strict diamond

\[
\mathcal W=\mathcal H\wedge\mathcal U<\mathcal H,\mathcal U<\mathcal H\vee\mathcal U<\mathcal F.

We want to check all of this before treating it as established mathematics. **A fatal counterexample, a missing locale-theoretic justification, or an invalid inherited lemma is a valuable report.**
