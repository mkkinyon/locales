## Context and reason for the audit

Our longer-term project studies localic separation relations arising from the diagonal of a locale. One such relation is
\(\mathcal W=\operatorname{cl}(\operatorname{fit}(\Delta))\). We introduced a finite-cover approximation \(\mathcal W_2\), and asked whether
\[
(Q')\qquad \mathcal W_2=\mathcal W\quad\text{for every frame},
\]
which would imply the weaker
\[
(Q)\qquad \mathcal W_2=\mathcal U\Longrightarrow\mathcal W=\mathcal U.
\]
**Neither question is claimed to be solved.** The work has generated positive theorems, necessary conditions for counterexamples, and multiple counterexample attempts that failed for informative reasons. The recent results have not received an independent audit. We intend to close this phase, consolidate what survives review, and turn to studying \(\mathcal W\) as a first-class relation in its own right.

A reasoned answer of **“no useful next suggestion” is completely welcome**. We do not ask you to embark on open-ended research.
