This reconciliation is our mathematical assessment of the report; do not confuse the referee's cold-read verdict with our subsequent checks. All research-priority/novelty claims remain provisional.

## 1. Executive disposition

The referee judged all labelled packet claims A1–G4 mathematically correct, with neither fatal nor major correctness errors. Our spot checks support that assessment. Treat the main theorem package as **externally corroborated working mathematics**, subject to normal literature/prior-art checks. The report's own *new deductions* must, however, be evaluated separately. We accept the finite-filter construction and Boolean density reformulations; we reject two overstatements explained in §§3–4 below.

The focal question remains OPEN:

    (Q) For every frame L, does W2=U imply W=U?

A stronger suggested question also remains OPEN:

    (Q') For every frame L, is W2=W?

(Q') is equivalent to d_W2 being fixed by the full diagonal-neighborhood operator Phi, and implies (Q). Do not claim evidence that it is true beyond the examples checked.

## 2. Accepted useful improvements

**Finite-cover filter.** B2 (finite-cut) implies that the filter B2 generated by binary square neighborhoods is precisely the filter of all elements lying above some square neighborhood w_C from a FINITE cover C. Thus D2=intersection_{C finite} O(w_C). The operator

    Phi2(q)=join_{C finite}(w_C → q)
           =join_{w in B2}(w → q)

is a genuine pre-nucleus. It has Fix(Phi2)=D2, Phi2(0)=d_U, and W2=U iff Phi2(d_U)=d_U. It is preferable to the earlier operator Psi, which was monotone/inflationary but not proven a pre-nucleus.

**Isbell density reduction.** The least dense sublocale B(U) exists by Isbell's theorem. Therefore

    W=U iff B(U)≤D,
    W2=U iff B(U)≤D2.

For the canonical nonregular boundary G=U∧O(d_U**), the original regular-core theorem plus the Boolean part of the interior gives

    W=U iff B(G)≤D,
    W2=U iff B(G)≤D2.

The referee also supplies a Boolean splitting B(U)=B(int U) sqcup B(G), with B(int U) always contained in D. The splitting deserves standard notation checks but is compatible with our frame calculations.

**Further useful qualifications.** The report points out that the Lindenloef/countable-distributivity theorem adds no new second-countable cases beyond atomicity, since a countably based atomless complete Boolean algebra admits a countable dense atomless part (Cohen algebra). We take this as a research heuristic pending separate source/precision check, rather than promoting every structural assertion about Boolean algebras as audited.

**Source/prior art.** The full operator Phi is the kappa_L of Arrieta–Picado–Pultr (2022), Lemma 5.4 of the authors' preprint; their Lemma 4.4 covers arbitrary-cover-compatible homomorphisms. Isbell's least dense sublocale is standard. Do not claim these named constructions as new.
