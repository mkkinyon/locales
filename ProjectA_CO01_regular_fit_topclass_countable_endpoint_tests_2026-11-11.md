# CO-01 main reduction — regular/fit ambient square and countable asymmetric top-class tests

**11 November 2026. New internally checked structural theorem, not an associativity verdict or external audit.** This substantially sharpens the [full endpoint-congruence criterion](ProjectA_CO01_Arrieta_cube_symmetry_spatial_invisibility_2026-11-11.md) by exploiting a property special to Arrieta's underlying space V: **regularity of V makes every sublocale of its localic square fitted**. It reduces equality of entire endpoint congruences to equality of their **top classes**, and thence to countable unions of basic Cantor-clopen rectangles. Combined with the [countable Θ-cover normal form](ProjectA_CO01_Arrieta_countable_clopen_generators_finite_cover_gap_2026-11-11.md), both the defining relations and the endpoint tests admit countable Boolean clopen presentations. No novelty claim about classical regular⇒fit or characterizations of fit frames.

## 1. Ambient localic regularity and top-class determinacy

Let C=2^N, D=∪K_n and V=C\D, as in the earlier notes; V is a zero-dimensional Tychonoff (hence regular) topological space. Therefore the frame F=O(V) is regular. **Finite localic products of regular locales are regular** (equivalently finite frame coproducts of regular frames are regular); one direct verification uses the rectangle basis and \(u\prec a,\ v\prec b \Rightarrow u\boxtimes v\prec a\boxtimes b\): if u∧u*=0, a∨u*=1 and v∧v*=0, b∨v*=1, then the complement of u⊠v contains (u*⊠1)∨(1⊠v*) and distributing \((a\vee u^*)\boxtimes(b\vee v^*)=1\) proves the well-inside property. Rectangles of this form join-generate every a⊠b, hence Q₂=F⊕F is regular.

Classical theorem (Picado–Pultr, *Frames and Locales*, V.1.3.2–3; also Arrieta 2024 §2.3): **regular ⇒ fit**, and a frame is fit iff *every sublocale* is fitted, equivalently iff any two frame congruences with the same 1-class coincide. Consequently every sublocale S⊆V² is fitted:
\[
S=\bigcap_{w\in\mathcal N(S)}\mathfrak o(w),\qquad
\mathcal N(S)=\{w\in Q_2:S\subseteq\mathfrak o(w)\}.
\tag{1.1}
\]
If the frame quotient for S is \(f_S:Q_2\twoheadrightarrow O(S)\), its 1-class is exactly \(\mathcal N(S)=\{w:f_S(w)=1\}\). Therefore
\[
\boxed{S=T\iff \mathcal N(S)=\mathcal N(T)}
\tag{1.2}
\]
for **arbitrary** sublocales of the regular locale V², including the nonspatial localic relations A, Q=A², L=QA, R=AQ and the honest path image P₄. Thus the original full two-variable endpoint-congruence comparison is stronger in syntax than necessary: only **which endpoint opens become 1** need be tested.

## 2. Literal CO-01 top-class criterion

Use \(Q_n=F^{\oplus n}\). Let G_{ij} be the frame congruences generated by \(\gamma_{\mathcal C}^{ij}=1\) for all Θ-admissible covers, equivalently for the **countable clopen normal forms** of the companion note. Put \(K_2=\ker(Q_2\to Q_3/(G_{12}\vee G_{23}))\) via endpoints (1,3), \(H_L=K_2^{12}\vee G_{23}\) on Q₃, and \(\alpha_{13}:Q_2\to Q_3\) the endpoint insertion. Then
\[
\mathcal N(L)=\{w\in Q_2:\alpha_{13}(w)=1\text{ in }Q_3/H_L\}.
\tag{2.1}
\]
The right cubic relation equals L^smile, so \(\mathcal N(R)=\tau\mathcal N(L)\), with \(\tau\) the coordinate swap on Q₂. Hence
\[
\boxed{
(A²)A=A(A²)\iff
\big[\alpha_{13}(w)=1\pmod{H_L}\iff
\alpha_{13}(\tau w)=1\pmod{H_L}\big]\quad\forall w\in Q_2.
}
\tag{2.2}
\]
For a negative answer it is sufficient to exhibit **one open \(w\)** for which the left equivalence fails; no arbitrary pair of congruent frame elements is needed.

The earlier sufficient endpoint-path criterion also simplifies. Let J_L be the kernel of Q₃→O(E₄) via full-path positions (1,3,4), H_L⊆J_L. Since L and P₄ are sublocales of the fit V² and P₄⊆L,
\[
\boxed{
L=P_4\iff
\forall w\in Q_2,\ 
[\alpha_{13}(w)=1\pmod{J_L}
\Rightarrow \alpha_{13}(w)=1\pmod{H_L}].
}
\tag{2.3}
\]
This **top-only criterion** is a genuine reduction from the full endpoint kernel equality. It is sufficient (not necessary) for CO-01 to have a positive answer, because P₄ is symmetric.

## 3. Countable asymmetric Cantor cylinder cover presentation of all endpoint opens

Let \(\mathcal B=\{[s]\cap V:s\in 2^{<\mathbb N}\}\) be the **countable Cantor clopen-cylinder basis** of V. The localic product frame \(Q_2=F\oplus F\) is join-generated by the basic rectangles \(b\boxtimes c\) with b,c∈B. Since B² is countable, **every element** of Q₂ is
\[
w_E=\bigvee_{(b,c)\in E} b_1\wedge c_2
\qquad\text{for some countable } E\subseteq\mathcal B\times\mathcal B.
\tag{3.1}
\]
The tensor representation does **not** require assuming the localic product equals the ordinary spatial product. The diagonal pullback is
\[
\delta^*(w_E)=\bigvee_{(b,c)\in E}(b\wedge c).
\]
If w_E belongs to \(\mathcal N(L)\), it contains Δ and automatically \(\delta^*(w_E)=1\), so the overlaps \(\{b\cap c:(b,c)\in E\}\) form an ordinary cover of V. Such an E is an **asymmetric paired clopen cover of the diagonal**.

Let \(E^\smile=\{(c,b):(b,c)\in E\}\). Combining with (2.2) gives the exact **countable Boolean matrix test**
\[
\boxed{
\text{CO-01 associates}\iff
\forall E\subseteq\mathcal B^2:\ 
\left[\alpha_{13}(w_E)=1\pmod{H_L}\iff
\alpha_{13}(w_{E^\smile})=1\pmod{H_L}\right].
}
\tag{3.2}
\]
For a negative answer it is enough to find one countable family E with an asymmetric top-class verdict. For a positive answer one must prove the symmetry of this forcing predicate for **all** such E. In the stronger positive test (2.3), substitute w=w_E. All A generator congruences are themselves given by countable **symmetric Θ-admissible Cantor-clopen covers**, as shown in the companion note. Hence both sides of the problem have explicit countable clopen-cylinder presentations.

## 4. A strict infinitary barrier for endpoint separators

The required density of A can be checked directly (and was asserted in audit VIII). Given any nonzero basic open rectangle \(U\boxtimes W\) of V², choose Cantor cylinders \(U_0,W_0\) whose nonempty V-traces lie inside U,W. Choose a **finite-support coordinate permutation** σ moving the finitely many coordinates constrained by W_0 to indices disjoint from those constrained by U_0. There is then a nonempty Cantor cylinder \(B\subseteq U_0\cap\widehat\sigma^{-1}(W_0)\). Since σ fixes every sufficiently large coordinate, the all-cover coincidence criterion of audit VIII gives \(h_{\rm id}\ A\ h_\sigma\), and the pulled-back rectangle has value \(q(U\cap\widehat\sigma^{-1}W)\ne0\), as it contains the nonempty V-trace of B. Hence the pullback of A to every nonempty basic rectangle is nonzero, so \(\operatorname{cl}(A)=V²\). Earlier audit VIII also establishes that A has precisely diagonal ordinary point pairs. Therefore Q,L,R,P₄ all contain A and are dense.

If \(S\) is a dense sublocale and \(w\in Q_2\) is complemented (clopen), \(S\subseteq\mathfrak o(w)\) implies w=1: otherwise \(\mathfrak o(w)\) is a **proper closed** sublocale containing dense S, contradicting closure(S)=V². Any *finite union* of basic Cantor-clopen rectangles (3.1) is complemented. Therefore:
\[
\boxed{
w_E\in\mathcal N(L)\ \land\ |E|<\infty\ \Longrightarrow\ w_E=1.
}
\tag{4.1}
\]
Such a trivial equation is symmetric and cannot separate the cubic bracketings. **Any open endpoint separator E must be infinite**. More precisely it must define a **proper dense open** sublocale containing one cubic composite but not its converse; both brackets contain the dense A. This is the endpoint analogue of the strict finite-admissible-cover gap of the companion note, though it follows by a different argument (density rather than Θ-admissibility).

This does NOT mean arbitrary finite-cover equations or finite patches of a larger infinite proof are irrelevant; it says a **single finite union of basic clopen rectangles cannot be the final separating endpoint open**. Also asymmetry and density of w_E alone are insufficient: the complement of one offdiagonal closed ordinary point is a proper asymmetric dense open that contains **both** cubic relations, by their exact singleton point fibres. A separator must detect the nonspatial structure.

## 5. What was gained and what remains

**Gains:** (i) ambient regularity removes the need to compare entire endpoint congruences; (ii) each top-class equation is a single (possibly countably infinite) open-coverage assertion; (iii) all endpoint opens are countable unions of Cantor clopen rectangles; (iv) the defining A congruences also have countable clopen normal form; (v) no finite clopen rectangular endpoint separator exists. This significantly sharpens the exact CO-01 target and rules out purely finite-cylinder proofs or counterexamples.

**Not proved:** symmetry of \(\mathcal N(L)\), the weaker path endpoint equality (2.3), full source surjectivity, or an explicit infinite clopen separator. Thus **CO-01 remains open**. This is a structurally meaningful simplification, not a verdict. New segment internally checked only; independent external audit pending if any headline theorem is proposed.
