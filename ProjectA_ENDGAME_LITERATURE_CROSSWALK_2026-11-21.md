## A. The hierarchy and its categorical semantics

**L01 — Cameron and Vinod Kumar P. B. (2026).** *Graphs on Topological Spaces*, arXiv:2610.14783, https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14783 . SOURCE VERIFIED (abstract, original full-text arXiv rendering); earlier full TeX read in project. The authors define Sp, Ins, Cm, Ury, Fun, prove their graph hierarchy, study finite Alexandroff examples, graph squares, and Dowker-style homotopy. **Project connection:** our \(\Delta,\mathcal I,\mathcal D,\mathcal U,\mathcal F,\mathcal A,\mathcal S\) aim to realize these in \(\operatorname{Sub}(X^2)\); the original pointwise hierarchy and homotopy statements are **known**. The precise equality of points of \(\mathcal D=\operatorname{fit}\Delta\) with Cm is an elementary project translation, not grounds for a priority claim. Our \(\mathcal S^2=\mathcal J,\mathcal S^4=\mathcal U\vee\mathcal V\) in coherent locales adds actual localic composition and requires the separate coherent calculus. **UNRESOLVED:** identify older point-free formulation of the complete five-relation hierarchy.

**L02 — Arrieta, Picado and Pultr (2022).** *A New Diagonal Separation and Its Relations With the Hausdorff Property*, *Applied Categorical Structures* 30 (2022), 247–263, DOI 10.1007/s10485-021-09655-9, author preprint https://www.mat.uc.pt/preprints/ps/p2117.pdf . SOURCE VERIFIED (abstract, §§2–5, Theorem 4.5 and 4.6.1). \(\operatorname{fit}(\Delta)=\Delta\) is **their established F-separatedness condition**, paired with ordinary closure of the diagonal/strong Hausdorffness. Their Theorem 4.5 gives the exact parallel-frame-map cover-respecting test; Theorem 4.6.1 cites Dowker–Strauss for the disjoint-open test. These methods and extreme equality classes are **prior work**. **Project scope:** we study **full intermediate sublocales** and their mutual comparison/composition, not merely zero-hull equality.

**L03 — Arrieta (2024).** *Localic Separation and the Duality Between Closedness and Fittedness*, *Topology and Its Applications* 342, 108785, DOI 10.1016/j.topol.2023.108785, author/publisher PDF https://pure-oai.bham.ac.uk/ws/portalfiles/portal/215992239/1-s2.0-S0166864123003784-main.pdf . SOURCE VERIFIED (Definitions §§2.3 and 4.6, examples and implications). Totally unordered (TU) means any frame maps \(h\le k:L\to M\) coincide; by the universal property of our oriented specialization sublocale \(\mathcal A\), the condition \(\mathcal A=\Delta\) is its **immediate categorical reformulation**, not a fresh separation axiom. Definition 4.6 gives the **published anti-Urysohn extreme case**, equivalent to \(\mathcal U=X^2\) in our rectangle notation. Also directly relevant: nontrivial separation counterexamples and fitted point conditions. **UNRESOLVED:** detailed theorem-by-theorem equivalence with our nontrivial hull equality classes, noncoherent examples, and \(\mathcal A\) spatiality theorem.

**L04 — Heunen and van der Schaaf (2024).** *Ordered Locales*, *J. Pure Appl. Algebra* 228(7), 107654, DOI 10.1016/j.jpaa.2024.107654, full author PDF https://www.pure.ed.ac.uk/ws/portalfiles/portal/426481945/Ordered_locales_HEUNEN_DOA13022024_VOR_CC_BY.pdf . SOURCE VERIFIED (abstract, Example 7.2). Their **order on opens** and open-cone adjunction recover specialization on points; this is a DIFFERENT construction from our **oriented localic subrelation** \(\mathcal A\subset X^2\), but an essential crosswalk. Example 7.2 explicitly recovers an opposite specialization order. **UNRESOLVED:** characterize precise comparison of the intrinsic locale-order relation and the subobject \(\mathcal A\).

**L05 — Normality, functional relation, and algebraic spectra.** Picado–Pultr, *Separation in Point-Free Topology* (2021), https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-030-53479-0 ; Dowker–Papert, *On Urysohn's Lemma* (1967), https://dml.cz/dmlcz/700880 ; W. H. Cornish, *Normal Lattices* (1972), DOI 10.1017/S1446788700010041 ; and subsequent conormal/minimal-prime literature. PREVIOUS LEDGER, not new full primary-source verification. Normal \(L\Rightarrow\mathcal U=\mathcal F\), and coherent \( \mathcal I=\mathcal U\) matches conormality of compact opens / unique minimal prime, are **largely translations of established structure**. Do not claim novelty for normality, Urysohn interpolation, or Cornish's minimal-prime characterization. **UNRESOLVED:** exact antecedents to specific relational equalities on \(X^2\) and to some preservation results.

## B. Localic relations, associativity and point-generated sublocales

**L06 — Klein and general categorical relations.** A. Klein, *Relations in Categories*, *Illinois J. Math.* 14(4) (1970), 536–550; A. M. Pitts (1983), B. Banaschewski (1990) on pullback non-stability of locale epimorphisms (precise references in L07). SOURCE VERIFIED as explicitly cited/discussed in L07, **not** a fresh line-by-line reading of Klein. The *general mechanism* of nonassociativity of image/pullback relational composition in Loc is KNOWN. Therefore do not headline discovery of nonassociativity as a novel result; our value is in natural **associative subclasses, positive hypotheses and explicit bounds/examples**.

**L07 — Nesta van der Schaaf (2026).** *Localic Relations with Open Cones*, arXiv:2605.04038v2 (2 September 2026), https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.04038 . SOURCE VERIFIED (abstract, introduction, §12.2 and bibliography). Studies internal localic relations with open source/target maps, cone operators, conic frames, adjunction, strongly dense inclusions, relation-theoretic reflexivity/transitivity. In §12.2 explicitly states failure of general associativity due to non-pullback-stability of epis, citing Klein [Kle70], Pitts [Pit83], Banaschewski [Ban90]. Its category rocLoc does **not** use relational composition for its morphisms (intro). It cites Moshier–Picado–Pultr 2017 for **generating sublocales**. **Project distinction:** our coherent proconstructible, rectangle, graph/witness and matrix subalgebras are explicit composition-stable associative classes; not evidently the open-cone setup. **UNRESOLVED (HIGH):** test overlaps of individual relations with open-cone hypotheses and compare any cone-based presentation of \(\mathcal A,\mathcal I,\mathcal D\).

**L08 — Jung, Kegelmann, Moshier (2001).** *Stably Compact Spaces and Closed Relations*, *Electronic Notes in Theoretical Computer Science* 45 (2001), 209–231, original preprint https://achimjungbham.github.io/pub/papers/Jung-Kegelmann-Moshier-2001-Stably-compact-spaces-and-closed-relations.pdf , readable version https://www.brics.dk/NS/01/2/BRICS-NS-01-2.pdf . SOURCE VERIFIED (§2.8–2.9 in readable PDF). Morphisms \(R:X\dashrightarrow Y\) are **closed subsets of \(X\times Y_\kappa\)**, composition is ordinary relation product, the specialization order is identity; compact Hausdorff case reduces to closed subsets of \(X\times Y\). This is an **important established associative closed-relation category**. **Project distinction:** our actual localic image/pullback compositions include sublocales not closed or spatial and the full \(p/q\) distinction. **UNRESOLVED:** precisely match the closed \(P\)-entry algebra of FINMAT to this category, including the use of proper images.

**L09 — Simmons, Niefield–Rosenthal, Picado–Pultr.** Picado–Pultr, *Axiom T_D and the Simmons Sublocale Theorem*, *Comment. Math. Univ. Carol.* 60(4) (2019), 541–551, DOI 10.14712/1213-7243.2019.030, https://www.dml.cz/manakin/handle/10338.dmlcz/147971 ; also Picado–Pultr, *On the Relation Between Subspaces and Sublocales* (2021/22), https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0022404921001924 ; Ball–Picado–Pultr (2016) on pointfree scatteredness. SOURCE VERIFIED (2019 summary and reference trail, later abstract); earlier project has fuller reading. **Known theorem:** for \(T_D\) spaces, all sublocales are induced by subspaces precisely in the scattered case. **Current project-derived scope (upgraded by a distinct focused blind audit):** \(\operatorname{Sub}(P\times K)\cong\prod_{k\in K}\operatorname{Sub}(P)\) for **arbitrary locale \(P\)** and **locally compact scattered Hausdorff \(K\)**, and the genuine binary localic matrix formula MUL*, with finite-support associative lift FINMAT*. The earlier compact-Hausdorff parameter theorem was a separate independently checked precursor. [Reconciled MAT*/MUL*/FINMAT* proof and status](ProjectA_MATSTAR_MULSTAR_REFEREE_RECONCILIATION_2026-11-21.md). **UNRESOLVED (HIGH):** search separately for an earlier parameterized theorem or localic matrix formulation; classical scatteredness criterion by itself is **not** new.

**L10 — Moshier, Picado, Pultr (2017).** *Generating Sublocales by Subsets and Relations: A Tangle of Adjunctions*, *Algebra Universalis* 78 (2017), 105–118. Accessible full text https://www.academia.edu/57585479/Generating_sublocales_by_subsets_and_relations_a_tangle_of_adjunctions . SOURCE VERIFIED (abstract/§§3–5). Their “relations” \(R\subseteq L\times L\) are **relations among frame elements** used to generate congruences/sublocales, not automatically *internal binary localic relations* \(R\hookrightarrow X\times X\). Key important supporting technique, but should not be misclassified as studying our same composition operation.

**L11 — Project graph and matrix algebra (externally refereed subject to conditions).** See [reconciliation](ProjectA_BALANCED_WITNESS_SCATTERED_MATRIX_POSTREFEREE_RECONCILIATION_2026-11-21.md). (RC) is separately externally checked compact-Hausdorff p/q rectangular composition; balanced witness (G,T,O) is sufficient conditional on RC; monomial graph group requires nonempty fibre for faithful abstract wreath identification. The earlier compact-parameter MAT/MUL/FINMAT/PATH were checked in that audit; a **later distinct blind technical packet** accepted the stronger **arbitrary-locale P / locally compact scattered K** MAT*/MUL*/FINMAT* version. Its arbitrary-subspace and indexed-image addenda are post-report **I**, not originally E. Broader TWIST graph extrapolation **not established**. Referee addendum supplies discrete-index invariance of the whole binary relation calculus and finite-flank law. **UNRESOLVED (HIGH):** priority of p/q rectangular saturation, balanced-witness and scattered-index matrix constructions, and relationship to ordinary closed-relation categories and allegories. Do not imply the original ambient set of all sublocale relations is associative.

## C. Dowker, homotopy and classifying anima

**L12 — Brun and Salbu (2023; online 2022).** *The Rectangle Complex of a Relation*, *Mediterranean J. Math.* 20 (2023), article 7, DOI 10.1007/s00009-022-02213-0, https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00009-022-02213-0 . SOURCE VERIFIED (Theorem 4.3, definition of rectangle complex). The functorial rectangle-complex/Dowker homotopy equivalence for **set-theoretic relations** is known, including the relation to formal concept analysis. **Project distinction:** sheaf/internal/localic witness objects and failure of descent, not classical rectangle nerve itself.

**L13 — Brun, Fosse, Salbu (2023).** *Dowker Duality for Relations of Categories*, arXiv:2303.16032v1, https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.16032 and https://arxiv.org/pdf/2303.16032 . SOURCE VERIFIED (abstract, initial definitions; earlier full-project crosswalk examines Thms 4.5–4.6). A relation of categories is a functor \(\mathcal R\to\mathcal C\times\mathcal D\). Their bisimplicial rectangle nerve and contractible/empty fibre hypothesis recover weak equivalences of Dowker nerves. **UNRESOLVED:** exact localic/sheaf generalization; previously audited project results record genuine non-descent obstacles. Strongly separate order categories from chaotic categories.

**L14 — De Silva et al. (2026).** *Dowker's Theorem for Higher-Order Relations*, *Journal of Applied and Computational Topology* 10 (2026), article 11, published 8 June 2026, DOI 10.1007/s41468-026-00239-x, https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s41468-026-00239-x . SOURCE VERIFIED (abstract, intro). Covers **multiway set relations** and functorial homotopy equivalences through a generalized rectangle complex; studies ternary configurations in detail. **High-priority overlap** for our higher diagonal/witness complexes, but *not* a theorem about localic descent. Compare carefully before claiming anything about "higher" Dowker duality.

**L15 — Björner (2003).** *Nerves, Fibers and Homotopy Groups*, *J. Combin. Theory Ser. A* 102 (2003), 88–93, https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/bjorner1.pdf . SOURCE VERIFIED (abstract, introduction). Connectivity versions of the nerve and poset-fibre theorems are classical. Project crosscut connectivity results should **credit** these finite/ordinary precedents; the nontrivial potential extension lies in *canonical comparison arrows in sheaf or condensed anima settings*.

**L16 — Peter J. Haine (2026).** *Classifying Anima of Condensed ∞-Categories of Points*, arXiv:2602.21330v1, https://arxiv.org/html/2602.21330v1 . SOURCE VERIFIED (Example 0.1, Theorem 2.20 and introductory comparison). For spectral \(X\), condensed coherent-point categories consist of quasicompact \(K\to X\) maps ordered by pointwise specialization; their classifying anima agree under the theorem's spectral-\(\infty\)-topos conditions. Our [project classifying-anima note](PointFree_classifying_anima_identification_2026-11-11.md) **identifies** the already used patched specialization-chain object with this **known** categorical construction, not a claim to have invented classifying anima. Important distinction: spectral sheaf topos \(\operatorname{Sh}(X)\) is not scheme étale topos \(X_{\acute et}\). **New note remains internally checked, not blind audited.**

## C′. Additional source comparisons after the original L01–L16 inventory

**L17 — John R. Isbell (1981).** *Product Spaces in Locales*, *Proc. Amer. Math. Soc.* **81**, 116–118, DOI https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9939-1981-0589150-5 . The original article's metadata/abstract and **Plewe's explicit reproduction of Isbell's theorem** were checked; **the full three-page Isbell article has not been line-by-line read in this consolidation**. For completely regular spaces, the universal spatial-multiplier characterization is equivalent to the condition that every nonempty closed subspace contain a point with a locally compact neighborhood (the classical \(C\)-scattered condition). That antecedent rules out crediting our later product-spatiality criterion as an independent discovery. [Exact evidence-level comparison](ProjectA_PLEWE_1996_FULL_PRIMARY_SOURCE_COMPARISON_2026-11-21.md).

**L18 — Peter T. Johnstone and **Shuhao Sun** (1988).** *Weak Products and Hausdorff Locales*, in F. Borceux (ed.), *Categorical Algebra and its Applications*, Lecture Notes in Mathematics **1348** (Springer, 1988), pp.173–193, DOI https://doi.org/10.1007/BFb0081358 . The publisher lists the second author as **Sun Shu-Hao**; Monash's institutional record renders the name **Shuhao Sun**.  The [primary-source review](ProjectA_ENDGAME_JOHNSTONE_SUN_WEAK_PRODUCTS_PRIMARY_SOURCE_2026-11-21.md) identifies their functorial weak product and its dense inclusion into the full localic product; for spatial factors the weak product is exactly the point-generated spatial product. Consequently our \(p(A,B)=[A\times B]\) and \(q(A,B)=[A]\times_{\rm Loc}[B]\) are **classical weak/full product** generators, not new definitions of spatial multiplication. Their cited results do **not** by themselves establish our mixed actual-localic RC composition theorem.

**L19 — Till Plewe (1996).** *Localic Products of Spaces*, *Proc. London Math. Soc.* (3) **73**, 642–678, DOI https://doi.org/10.1112/plms/s3-73.3.642 . The project owner supplied the **entire original journal article**, read directly in Session 11; [full-paper primary-source comparison](ProjectA_PLEWE_1996_FULL_PRIMARY_SOURCE_COMPARISON_2026-11-21.md). Plewe Theorem 1.6 reproduces Isbell's spatial-multiplier theorem, Theorem 2.2 gives the metrizable classification, and **Theorem 2.6 gives the rational-factor test for every metrizable space A**, not merely separable A. Theorem 3.2, pp.654–655, explicitly passes from a nonzero pointless sublocale to its **compact closed support**, proves density of the coordinate traces, and extracts a Cantor witness; these are direct published precedents for **the method** in our G′ argument. Our full support *sublocale-generation equation* is formulated more strongly and used for mixed RC, but exact historical novelty of that equation is **not resolved**. Plewe §6, p.665, warns of **infinite**-join product failure, fully consistent with the general **finite**-join pullback law.
