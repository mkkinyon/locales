## 1. Classifying-anima identification

Checked against Peter Haine, *Classifying anima of condensed ∞-categories of points*, https://arxiv.org/html/2602.21330, Example 0.1, Theorem 2.20. For spectral X and extremally disconnected profinite K, the coherent-point category of Sh(X) has objects maps f:K→X such that each quasicompact open inverse image is clopen, with pointwise specialization morphisms \(f\le g\iff f(k)\in\overline{\{g(k)\}}\). Compact opens and their complements generate the patch topology, so \(f\) is exactly a patch-continuous K→V map. A chain of such maps is exactly a patch-continuous K→Ch_n(X) map; coordinate deletion/repetition matches the ordinary nerve. This directly verifies the **degreewise isomorphism**. Groupoid completion/realization in the same Stone-sheaf site gives the stated classifying-anima identification.

**Limits retained:** Haine's comparison \(B Pt^{coh}\simeq B Pt\) is established for **spectral infinity-topoi** (his Theorem 2.20), including Sh(X) for spectral X. The actual all-points nerves need not be degreewise equal. The compared topos is spectral/Zariski Sh(X), **not** a scheme's étale topos. The identification must be restricted to a common compatible test site; it proves no universal site-size-independent intrinsic localic shape theorem. Coherent assumption remains. **Disposition: survives bounded internal review**, no independent review of this new comparison yet.

## 2. One-sided ladder

Checked that \(B^{ch}_{p,q}\) and \(B^{up}_{p,q}\) are patch-closed in the appropriate compact Stone powers for closed U upward cofinal. At fixed p, the q-direction is the full Čech nerve of the two **surjective Stone** witness maps to Ch_p and I_p; at fixed q, the p-direction of the *arbitrary tuple* object is the Čech nerve of the nonempty common-downset projection onto D_q^U. The map between total realizations is the original **canonical inclusion**. At q=0 appending u is a continuous simplicial contraction; for every q the chain fibre has an inhabited 0-simplex locally after a Stone cover, so f_q is 0-connective. The audited \(\infty\)-topos shifted realization lemma yields n-connectivity with f_q (n−q)-connective for q=1..n−1. A map to point being k-connective means its source is (k−1)-connected, so the finite shifts listed are correct.

**Warnings:** a set U=Max(X) need not be patch closed when X is infinite; never assume it is an admissible compact crosscut without checking. Relative **internal** connectedness over the parameter Stone space is the hypothesis; connectedness of ordinary pointwise fibres alone is not substituted. The all-contractible conclusion requires relative equivalences in all q.

## 3. Two-sided ladder

Checked the trisimiplicial spaces: fixed p, the (r,s) diagrams are iterated Čech nerves of independent lower and upper witness-surjections onto Ch_p respectively M_p. Fixed r,s, arbitrary tuples are the Čech nerve of \(\{(x,\mathbf a,\mathbf u):a_i\le x\le u_j\}\twoheadrightarrow R_{r,s}\). The base R_{r,s} must be the **actual image of interpolation**, not merely the locus of pairwise \(a_i\le u_j\) comparisons. The two axis contractions work because for r=0, a₀ lies in every nonempty window and is its least point, and for s=0 u₀ is its greatest. Thus the first possibly noncontractible bidegree is (1,1). All windows inhabited locally yield effective epimorphisms g_{r,s}, and the twice-applied connectivity lemma gives the correct (n−r−s) hypothesis. The universal 2-connectivity reappears with no additional conditions. The layered two-antichain example gives the claimed sharp shift: a 2×2 window for m layers has m−2 interior antichain layers and its chain realization is S^{m−3} for m≥3.

**Warnings:** this does not claim the higher meet-hull realization itself equals chain realization generally, nor does the finite result follow for infinite X merely from separate ordinary fibres. Need independent review of the new multi-simplicial identifications before ledger promotion.
