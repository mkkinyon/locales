## Three interconnected research themes

1. **Separation and relational algebra.** Diagonal closure/fitting, the hierarchy I, D, U, V, W, H, F, coherence, algebraic characterizations, composition and preservation. Its established long-form record remains the [Project A entry point](PROJECT_A_START_HERE.md), [master theorem ledger v1.9](ProjectA_master_theorem_ledger_v1_9_2026-11-09.md), [open-problem register v1.6](ProjectA_open_problems_register_v1_6_2026-11-09.md), and [coherent composition canonical proof](ProjectA_coherent_composition_canonical_proof_2026-11-08.md).
2. **Higher relations and simplicial constructions.** Localic specialization S=A∨A^smile, graph powers S²=J, S⁴=U∨V, higher hulls I^(n)=cl Δ_n, D^(n)=fit Δ_n, bisimplicial common-witness rectangles and Čech nerves. Start with [Cameron–Kumar revisited](ProjectA_Cameron_Kumar_revisit_graph_to_locale_hierarchy_2026-11-10.md), [the Dowker bisimplicial note](ProjectA_Dowker_bisimplicial_Cech_patch_descent_2026-11-10.md) and [the Brun–Fosse–Salbu crosswalk](ProjectA_Brun_Fosse_Salbu_Dowker_crosswalk_2026-11-10.md). Read the reconciliation first for site and scope repairs.
3. **Cover nerves and point-free homotopy / shape.** Frame-cover nerve reconstruction, arbitrary-frame open-surjection descent, natural simplicial spans, refinement systems and potential localic homotopy invariants. Start with the [original Project C notebook](C-frame-nerves.md), [frame-cover Dowker note](ProjectAC_frame_cover_Dowker_open_descent_2026-11-10.md), and [higher-diagonal/cover-nerve reconstruction](ProjectAC_higher_diagonal_cover_nerve_reconstruction_2026-11-10.md). Do not equate a chosen-cover nerve with intrinsic localic shape.

## Status and research discipline

- The **10 November independent report** corroborates the recent mathematics *after specified repairs*; its new non-descent result is referee-origin, independently checked in the reconciliation. The old working notes have not been silently rewritten.
- The coherent spectral composition calculus is an explicit prerequisite for many specialization and graph-power identities; arbitrary localic composition need not associate.
- Patched and unpatched Dowker equivalences are statements in **specified sheaf ∞-topoi**, not homotopy equivalences of the original unpatched simplicial locales or determinations of localic shape. The unpatched coherent D-witness maps can fail **descent** entirely.
- The higher diagonal hull reconstruction formulas hold for every locale in classical mathematics; the nonface closed tests are simplicial only after a meet-closed-cover repair.
- Publication priority, constructive scope and broader localic shape interpretations require separate investigation.
