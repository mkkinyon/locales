## Claim audits

**A. Two-apex X(S).** The Priestley order consists of Stone S as an equality antichain and two isolated maxima p,q above every s∈S. The closed downsets S∪{p}, S∪{q} are clopen, cover X, have terminal chain-nerve realizations, and intersect in S, with C(S)=h(S). The finite Čech square gives C(X)=1 ⨿_{h(S)}1. In the actual coherent proconstructible upper hull I_n, every tuple lies wholly in one apex downset; each component and their intersection are strict Čech nerves of nonempty Stone spaces over 1. Hence I(X)=1 and audited edgewise gives D(X)=1. In homology the reduced normalized suspension complex gives H̃_1 = ker(ε:Z[h(S)]→Z); higher reduced homology sheaves zero. For infinite S, [id_S]−[const_{s0}] has infinitely many distinct point values; a section of constant discrete group sheaf over compact S has finite image. **Checks needed:** sheafified free abelian functor, Mayer–Vietoris, constancy criterion and the assumption S is represented in the chosen κ-site. Correctly distinguishes unmarked h(S) from its underlying point set.

**B. All-locale open D-side descent.** For any open cover ∨_i u_i=1, put w_i=∧_{j=0}^n π_j^*u_i. On Ch_n, specialization order makes w_i=π_0^*u_i, whence ∨w_i=1. For D_n=fit Δ, Δ^*(∨w_i)=1, so D_n≤O(∨w_i), also ∨w_i|_{D_n}=1. These are genuine open-chart covers **for arbitrary locales and infinite index sets**. Joyal–Tierney effective descent of open surjections supplies degreewise localic descent; this is NOT a statement about unpatched lower-witness projection s_q nor unpatched geometric realizations. For finite compact-open covers in coherent X, the charts are coherent, patch-clopen Stone, and strict Čech descent yields canonical C→D as realization of local arrows C(U_S)→R_X^↓(U_S). LB_u uses the *proper prime ideal* generated by {k:u≰k}; equivalence to a common ambient lower point for all of U and to H_n^↓(U)=U^{n+1} in all degrees is checked by compact-open prime-ideal extension and witness selection. **Review risk:** present the full indexed Čech diagram, not a naive multiplicity-free poset colimit; separate arbitrary-locale open-cover gluing from patched coherent codescent.

**C. Mixed covers and ordinal sums.** For a finite downset cover {Z_i} and finite upset cover {U_j}, a chain is in Z_i∩U_j by selecting a cover member for its final and first vertices respectively. Actual hulls need NOT have such a joint mixed cover: (a,b) in I and (p,q) in D escape the mixed cells in the two-sided layer example. The disjoint-union Priestley ordinal sum P⊕Q has Ch_n as a simplicial join of the two chain nerves, cut by first P/last Q blocks. Patch takes finite coproducts/products to Stone, h preserves finite coproducts/products, and sheaf realization gives C(P⊕Q)=C(P)*C(Q). Hulls I,D contract by insertion of any p0∈P, q0∈Q: the relevant ambient witness persists even for tuples entirely in either layer. Direct localic factorization is secured by coherent proconstructible spatial image lemma. The two-sided A⊕S⊕A construction yields Σ²h(S) for C, and terminal I,D by independent one-sided codescent. For m layers, Σ^m h(S); H̃_m = ker ε and all lower homotopy sheaves vanish for m≥2, hence Hurewicz π_m=ker ε. **Review risk:** ensure the extra-degeneracy is an actual simplicial homotopy and beware of claiming objectwise evaluation T↦Σ^m Cont(T,S) *after* sheafification. An objectwise statement is permissible before sheafification, not at each sheaf test object afterward.

**D. Stone-indexed pointed face-poset wedge.** For finite poset P and a *minimal* distinguished vertex v, W_S={v} ⊔ (S×(P\{v})) with fiberwise comparisons and v below exactly the same labels it was below in P; no new cross-fiber comparisons. Closed order, locally clopen upsets yield Priestley; compact-open lattice is locally constant S→Ups(P) with constant membership of v. For every n, Ch_n(W) and I_n(W) decompose as clopen 1 plus copies of S indexed non-basepoint tuples: a non-v coordinate forces any common upper witness into a unique s-fiber. This is a genuine strict simplicial **Stone-level** gluing along a clopen summand, so sheafified Yoneda does preserve the specific pushout (not arbitrary Stone quotient pushouts). Consequently C(W)=h(S)_+∧C(P) and I(W)=h(S)_+∧I(P), and the canonical C→I is h(S)_+∧(C(P)→I(P)). For P=F(∂Δ^{m+1}) and basepoint a vertex, the previously audited finite canonical comparison is an equivalence; the audited edgewise theorem transfers to D. Homology sheaf H̃_m=Z[h(S)], not ker ε, because this is a based wedge indexed by S; for m≥2 it is π_m by internal Hurewicz. [id_S] gives nonconstancy for infinite S. **Review risk:** the D_n locales generally do NOT decompose by fibers (cross-fiber tuples have common lower witness v). Conclude D equivalence only through audited edgewise theorem. Ensure P basepoint minimal; a nonminimal point could induce cross-fiber upper-witness issues.

**E. Homology reconstruction negative and coalgebra positive.** The fact that Z[βN/(a∼b)]≅Z[βN] for two distinct remainder points as **unmarked condensed abelian groups** is stated without proof by Clausen–Scholze, *Condensed Mathematics and Complex Geometry*, Lecture III, printed page 26, immediately after Question 3.6 (https://people.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/scholze/Complex.pdf). The quotient is Stone and not ED, hence not homeomorphic to βN; both sets have same infinite cardinality. This refutes reconstruction from H̃_m sheaf alone; it says nothing about equivalence of full E_m(S) homotopy objects. The natural coalgebra δ([s])=[s]⊗[s], ε([s])=1 on M_S=Z[h(S)] has group-like sheaf equal h(S): finite quotient coefficient comparison over integers forces exactly one basis vector; finite stage projections jointly monic by Clausen–Scholze Prop 3.3, so a compatible group-like family corresponds to a unique continuous T→S. Coalgebra morphisms are thus exactly linearizations of continuous Stone maps, proving full faithfulness. **Review risk:** coalgebra is *extra marked structure*, not automatically induced on H̃_m by forgetting all fiber-generator data. The external cited isomorphism is taken as a published statement, not a theorem independently proved by this audit; a referee should verify applicability to the κ-bounded Stone site. ## Targeted high-priority potential hidden errors

1. **Actual proconstructible sublocales versus point sets:** gluing of I_n and all localic open-cover assertions must not rely on arbitrary pointwise-to-localic equality.
2. **Site/generators:** sheafified Yoneda h only preserves the explicit clopen-summand pushouts and finite coproducts; it does NOT preserve all quotient pushouts. Effective Čech covers are finite jointly surjective Stone families.
3. **Constancy proof:** evaluate sheaf sections on S itself and at point maps, distinguish the constant sheaf from representable h(S), and avoid equating a sheaf's T-sections with the pre-sheaf formula without sheafification.
4. **Infinite sheaf homotopy:** connectivity and π_m via internal Hurewicz are in the chosen ∞-topos, not merely for global sections.
5. **Coalgebra:** check strong monoidality of the free abelian functor and the group-like equalizer, and the exact interpretation of the Clausen–Scholze counterexample.
