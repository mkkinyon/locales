### What was rechecked

1. **Coherent presentations and finite approximation.** For a coherent locale `X` with bounded compact-open lattice `K`, the `(n+1)`-fold product has compact-open lattice the bounded distributive coproduct `T_n(K)`. The three congruences for the chain object, closed diagonal hull and fitted diagonal hull are generated by finite compact-open equations (details in Packet §§1–2). Since `K` is a filtered colimit of finite *embedded* bounded sublattices, those congruences are likewise a filtered colimit. The resulting maps are actual coherent spectral sublocales. Thus all three simplicial localic constructions are degreewise inverse limits of finite-poset analogues, compatibly with simplex operators. **Do not infer** geometric realization commutes with this inverse limit. This proof relies on the prior audited all-frame diagonal reconstruction and on the coherent spectral correspondence; it should be included as a self-contained prerequisite in the packet.
2. **Map-level finite crosscut criterion.** For a finite poset `P` and maximal elements `M`, the lower witness complex strongly collapses onto the induced maximal crosscut complex. For the upper witness inclusion, compare the covers by `Ord(↓m)` and full simplices on `↓m`. For the lower inclusion, the map `Φ(σ)={m in M: max(σ)≤m}` into the opposite maximal-crosscut face poset has Quillen fibers the face posets of `Ord(B_S)`. The comparison to the *specified canonical inclusion* via `r(x)=m(x)` works: for nested chain faces, all vertices of the two subdivided maps lie in `Φ(σ_min)`. Correctness verified, **novelty not established**. Classical antecedent: Rota–Björner crosscut and Barmak on stronger simple-homotopy nerve lemmas.
3. **Coherent maximal-point cover.** In compact Priestley spaces, every point extends upward to a maximal point, by a standard compact-chain/Zorn argument. For finitely many maxima `M`, the patch-closed principal downsets `↓m` form a *finite* cover. Each degree of both the chain and closed-hull simplicial objects is covered by the corresponding subobjects; Čech intersections are chain objects or Cartesian powers of `B_S`. The contractibility hypothesis on all nonempty `Ch(B_S)` ensures both have terminal realization. The argument uses **finite jointly surjective Stone families**, not singleton proper surjections alone.
4. **Twofold edgewise comparison.** Define `ε([n])=[n]*[n]=[2n+1]`, with `ε(α)=α*α`. Split a chain of length `2n+2` into its first and last `n+1` entries: this gives an actual simplicial locale map `esd₂ Ch→diag E(A)`, because cross-block comparisons hold by internal transitivity. Its composites to the closed and fitted sides are `i_I∘a` and `i_D∘b`. Define a simplicial homotopy between the first- and last-block maps using `H_n(x,θ)=(x_{i+(n+1)θ(i)})_{i=0}^n` for monotone `θ:[n]→[1]`. The ordinal-map identity `(α*α) f_{θ∘α}=f_θ α` verifies *all* simplicial structure maps, not merely faces. The map is well-defined on locales via projections and the presented order inequalities. The standard **ordinary** edgewise-subdivision lemma makes `|a|`, `|b|` equivalences for simplicial spaces; objectwise presheaf realization followed by colimit-preserving sheafification legitimately extends this to the specified ∞-topos. With the already-reviewed patched Čech equivalences `|diag E|→I,D`, the *canonical arrows under C* correspond. Therefore their homotopy cofibers agree. The explicit undercategory verification is important; mere equivalence of targets would not suffice.
5. **Relative finite closed-cover descent.** For compact opens `u_i` meeting to zero, `Z_i=C(u_i)` are closed, patch-closed and downward-closed in spectral specialization. A chain belongs to any downset containing its last point, and a tuple with an upper witness `y` belongs to any downset containing `y`. Hence the **actual** patched simplicial terms have finite jointly surjective covers by `Ch(Z_i)` and `H^X(Z_i)=I(X)∧Z_i^{n+1}`. Their fiber products are the corresponding objects on `Z_S`. The local assumption `|Ch(Z_S)|→|H^X(Z_S)|` an equivalence gives the global canonical `C→I` by commuting the Čech and simplex realizations. The D-side follows **only using the edgewise result**. **Critical distinction**: `H^X(Z_S)` allows common upper bounds in `X` and is generally NOT `I(Z_S)`; the packet keeps this explicit.
6. **Compact-open interval identities and primality.** The closed sublocale `C(v)` for compact `v` has compact lattice `[v,1]_K`; an element `k` meets `C(v)` iff `k ≰v`. Meet-prime bottom corresponds to a greatest specialization point; join-prime top to a least point. A greatest point makes the relative upper-witness object `Z^{n+1}`, a Čech nerve over the point; a least point gives an extra degeneracy for the relative upper-witness object even without ambient common upper bound. These are **genuine spectral-localic identities** because all involved sublocales are proconstructible/coherent, not just identifications of ordinary point sets.
7. **Ambient common-upper condition (UB).** Let `T_v={k∈K:k≰v}`. Centeredness of this collection under finite meets in **K of X**, rather than in `[v,1]`, is equivalent (by prime-filter extension) to a point `g∈X` above all `z∈C(v)`. Proof: all compact-open neighborhoods of each such `z` are members of `T_v`; the resulting prime filter contains them. Conversely an upper bound is in every such compact open. Under UB, `H_n^X(C(v))=C(v)^{n+1}` as coherent spectral sublocales. The upper witness `g` need not belong to `C(v)`: that is the strict weakening. **Do not weaken UB to pairwise nonzero meets**; the full finite-intersection property is essential.
8. **Specialization zigzags (SZC).** A finite zigzag of bounded-lattice endomorphisms whose maps are alternately pointwise `≤/≥` induces an internal natural-transformation zigzag of specialization functors and contracts the realized chain nerve if its final map factors through a point. Pointwise spectral order is sufficient because the source is spectral/spatial and the order inclusion is a compact-preserving presented sublocale. The four-point path `a<c`, `b<c`, `b<d` has no extrema; the endomorphism `f(a)=c`, `f(b)=b`, `f(c)=c`, `f(d)=d` is order-preserving, and `id≤f≥const_b`. Checked directly.
9. **Intrinsic specialization-nerve contractibility (SNC).** In the **specified** Stone sheaf ∞-topos, define `C(Z)=|h(Patch Ch•(Z))|`; SNC means `C(Z)≃1`. For a finite poset, this is contractibility of its ordinary order complex, not necessarily contractibility of the finite Alexandrov space itself. The distinction is established by Cianci–Ottina's nine-point examples and Stong's beat-point criterion (external sources checked at abstract/theorem level). Finite closed-cover Čech descent computes `C(Z)` as the **constant homotopy type of the cover nerve** whenever all nonempty intersections are SNC. The cover is by `C(c_i)` for compact opens with meet zero and every intersection is `C(∨_{i∈S}c_i)`. This is a recursive compact-open certificate of SNC when the abstract cover nerve is contractible. Do not call it a finite algorithm without an oracle for the contraction of intersections.
10. **Universal coherent realization.** For a nonempty finite simplicial complex `T`, its nonempty-face poset `P=F(T)` is finite. Choose vertex `v`; adjoin a convergent sequence of new maximal points `a_n→a_∞`, with `v<a_n`, `v<a_∞` only. In the Stone topology old faces are isolated, and the order relation is closed because its only new limit comparisons are included. Priestley separation uses principal upsets of old faces, isolated singleton new maxima, and cofinite tails containing the limit. The patch-clopen downsets `↓F` for facets `F` and `Z_A={v}∪A` cover the whole space. Each nonempty intersection has a greatest or least point. The abstract nerve is the nerve of facet intersections, with one cone attached over the simplex on facets containing `v`; attaching that cone does not affect the homotopy type. The classical facet-nerve theorem gives `|N|≃|T|`. Consequently all three **patched sheaf** realizations are constant on `|T|`, via the canonical inclusions. Nonisolated patch point and infinitely many maximal points are genuine. This does NOT identify intrinsic shape or ordinary spectral weak homotopy of `X_T`.
11. **Dunce-hat obstruction.** For a finite pure two-dimensional triangulation `T` of the dunce hat with no free edges, `P=F(T)` has no beat points: no edge has a unique strict coface and edges have incomparable vertex subfaces; each facet has incomparable lower faces; each vertex has several incomparable upper edges. Since the dunce hat is contractible, `|Ord(P)|≃1`, but `P` is a noncontractible finite space by Stong. In `X_T`, `P` is a **spectral retract**: include original faces and send all new sequence points to `v`. This map is patch-continuous and monotone; hence spectral. Any finite zigzag `id_{X_T}⋚...⋚const` would restrict along the retract to an impossible finite zigzag for `P`. Thus SNC does not imply SZC even for infinite coherent locales.
12. **Enhanced relative criterion.** For each nonzero finite intersection `Z_S`, either a least point, or [UB and SNC] suffices to make **both local objects terminal**, hence their comparison an equivalence. The previous [UB and SZC] criterion is a special case. The exact finite four-point relative example has a contractible path and no extrema. A further infinite circle construction with a dunce-hat face poset at a nontrivial intersection has consistent finite combinatorial intersection tables; see the packet. This strengthened hypothesis is sufficient, not necessary.

## Counterchecks / edge cases

- Finite `P={a,b,c,d}` with two minimal and two maximal elements and all four cross-comparisons: `|Ch•(P)|≃S¹` while both witness nerves contract. Thus coherence alone **cannot** imply canonical equivalence. This remains a control counterexample.
- A strongly Hausdorff locale has `A=Δ`, so `Ch_n=Δ_{n+1}` and the higher hulls may differ depending on strong Hausdorff/fittedness; no blanket contractibility conclusion follows. For Stone locales `Ch=I=D` degreewise, illustrating why the prime-endpoint cover condition is **not necessary**.
- Infinite maximal sets need not be patch-closed: no maximal-point cover is assumed in the intrinsic frame criterion.
- With one closed-cover member `Z_1=X`, the relative descent theorem is vacuous; the useful finite criterion is that there be a **proper cover** with locally tractable intersections.
- Use *classical* logic when inferring nonzero spectral locale has a spectral point and extending filters. The entire formalism is not claimed constructively.

## Internal findings and required repairs to exposition

1. **No fatal problem identified**, but do not promote these four segments to the verified ledger before external review. In the previous 10 November Dowker audit, the site repair was essential; include the site explicitly in all statements.
2. **Major proof-dependency control:** the relative cofiber/arrow equivalence uses the edgewise theorem **and** both patched proper-descent equivalences; no claim can transfer to unpatched `s_q`, which was independently proved not even descent in the earlier referee report.
3. **Major interpretative caution:** “intrinsic” SNC is only an **isomorphism invariant of coherent frames relative to the chosen Stone sheaf homotopy theory**, not intrinsic localic shape. The closed-cover computation uses *jointly* surjective Stone families and their effective Čech colimits. State that patch of a coherent finite-limit diagram is finite-limit preserving (right adjoint/compact-open argument), rather than assuming arbitrary patch of a locale map preserves products.
4. **Presentational clarification:** distinguish the finite-poset map-level crosscut theorem (Rota/Björner/Quillen) from any newly packaged locale-theoretic statement; state that we have not checked if the map-level version already exists in the classical literature.
5. **Presentational clarification:** `K(Z_S)=[v_S,1]_K` carries `v_S` as bottom; meet/joins in the interval are inherited. When `Z_S` is empty, the contractibility hypothesis is vacuous and its simplicial realization is **initial**, not terminal.
6. **Presentational clarification:** for finite-stage inverse-limit theorem, specify that the bonding finite-poset maps are spectral and order-preserving; do not claim stagewise surjectivity of the induced **chain/hull** maps, or an equality of realized infinite objects with homotopy inverse limits.
7. **External-literature checks:** the claim “weakly contractible but noncontractible finite spaces need at least nine points, exactly two nine-point examples” is supported by Cianci–Ottina (2020); no-free-edge finite dunce-hat triangulations are supported by Benedetti–Lutz and later work. Edgewise geometric realization of the two block maps is supported by standard edgewise subdivision results and Brown–Chan–Galatius–Payne (§3.1), but a fully explicit citation/statement should be demanded of the referee.
8. **Machine sanity checks:** a finite truncated 10-point example of the `p,q,r,A` construction was enumerated: cover sets were `Z_p={a,b,c,d,f,p}`, `Z_q={a,b,c,d,e,q}`, `Z_r={e,f,r}`, `Z_A={a,A}`; nontrivial intersections exactly `Z_{pq}={a,b,c,d}`, `Z_{pr}={f}`, `Z_{qr}={e}`, `Z_{pA}={a}`, `Z_{qA}={a}`, `Z_{pqA}={a}`. The abstract nerve has the cycle `p-q-r-p` plus filled triangle `p-q-A`. This is a consistency check, not verification of the infinite patch topology.
