## Main task

Check **every mathematical assertion**, including equations, proof sketches, examples, remarks, and stated qualifications. In particular, determine whether:

1. The compact-open quotient presentations of the coherent chain nerve, closed higher diagonal hull and fitted higher diagonal hull are correct **as localic subobjects**, and whether all three really commute degreewise with cofiltered finite-poset approximation.
2. The edgewise-subdivision map from the chain nerve into the diagonal bisimplicial rectangle nerve is simplicial *on the nose*; the explicit homotopy of the two block projections is valid; and these observations, with the patched Čech/Dowker hypotheses, identify the **actual canonical arrows under the chain realization**, not just the abstract target homotopy types. Check the induced homotopy-cofiber assertion.
3. Every step in the finite closed-sublocale Čech descent theorem takes place in the specified Stone sheaf ∞-topos, particularly the patch functor, the joint-surjectivity of degreewise covers, their intersections, and the distinction between ambient upper-witness objects and the intrinsic higher closed diagonal of a sublocale.
4. The prime-endpoint, ambient-upper-bound (UB), specialization-zigzag (SZC), and specialization-nerve-contractibility (SNC) criteria are all valid **for actual coherent locales**, including the finite closed-cover nerve formula and recursive certificates.
5. The infinite coherent constructions are indeed Priestley spaces, their proposed closed covers have compact-open complements, their intersection tables and nerve homotopy types are correct, and they realize the homotopy types claimed. Verify the dunce-hat spectral retract and the claim that no finite zigzag of spectral endomorphisms exists.
6. No assertion silently assumes that pointwise equality of arbitrary sublocales implies localic equality, that arbitrary localic relation composition associates, or that geometric realization commutes with inverse limits. Identify any missing assumptions concerning classical choice, site size, spatiality, sheafification, or descent.
7. Assess which claims are already standard crosscut/nerve/finite-space theorems or formal deductions, versus locale-specific results; do not make unsupported novelty or priority claims.
