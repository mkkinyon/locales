## 0. Conventions and the chosen homotopy theory

- Mathematics is **classical**, with the prime ideal/filter theorem and choice available. A coherent locale `X` has compact-open bounded distributive lattice `K(X)`, and `O(X)≅Idl(K(X))`. A finite `T₀` locale is identified with the Alexandrov locale of a finite poset.
- **Specialization order:** `x≤y` means every spectral open containing `x` also contains `y`. Hence spectral opens are **upsets**, `↑x` is the set of generizations, and `↓x` the set of specializations. A Priestley space is a compact zero-dimensional Hausdorff space with a closed partial order such that every `x≰y` can be separated by a clopen upset containing `x` but not `y`. Spectral topology is generated by clopen upsets. Finite spectral products correspond to coproducts of compact-open bounded lattices.
- All `≤` relations between maps are in the internal specialization order. Its genuine localic relation `A↪X×X` imposes `π₁^*(u)≤π₂^*(u)` for every open `u`; for coherent `X` it is proconstructible spectral. Its generalized points are pairs of maps `f≤g`. No composition of arbitrary localic relations is assumed associative.
- Put `Ch_n(X)={x₀≤⋯≤x_n}` as the finite intersection of the pullbacks of `A` for adjacent pairs. Let `Δ_{n+1}↪X^{n+1}` be the diagonal and write `I_n(X)=cl(Δ_{n+1})`, `D_n(X)=fit(Δ_{n+1})` (so `I_0=D_0=X`). For coherent `X` these are proconstructible spectral sublocales of `X^{n+1}`, and their **spectral points** are respectively tuples with a common upper and a common lower witness. There are actual simplicial sublocale inclusions `Ch•(X)↪I•(X)` and `Ch•(X)↪D•(X)`.
- For coherent `X` let `Patch(X)` be its Stone patch locale. For proconstructible spectral sublocales, patching identifies them with their patch-closed point sets equipped with their induced Stone topology; finite-limit constructions on these spectral maps are preserved by patching. We do **not** identify arbitrary localic sublocales from their ordinary points.
- Fix a suitably large essentially small site `Stone_κ` with finite jointly surjective covering families, its subcanonical sheaf ∞-topos `H=Shv_∞(Stone_κ,J)`, and sheafified Yoneda `h`. The size bound is chosen to contain all Stone locales and the finite limits/finite coproducts in this packet. Here *representables are sheaves*, finite jointly surjective families induce effective epimorphisms, and `h` preserves finite limits. Put
  `C(X)=|h(Patch Ch•(X))|`, `I(X)=|h(Patch I•(X))|`, `D(X)=|h(Patch D•(X))|`.
  Geometric realizations and homotopy colimits throughout are computed **in `H`**. The notation `underline(|T|)` denotes the *constant sheaf ∞-topos object* associated with the ordinary homotopy type of the finite simplicial complex `T`. No assertion is made about intrinsic shape of a locale or about unpatched realizations.
- A *spectral map* is a locale map preserving compact opens on inverse images. The localic image of a spectral map between coherent locales is the proconstructible spectral sublocale whose patch-closed point set is its point-image; the surjective first factor is established from the spatiality of the target. This nontrivial lemma is sketched in Appendix A and should also be checked.

## 1. Self-contained background: higher hulls and rectangle descent

For an arbitrary locale `X`, opens of `X^{n+1}` are join-generated by coordinate rectangles
`a₀⊠⋯⊠a_n = ⋀_{j=0}^n π_j^*(a_j)`.
The largest open disjoint from the diagonal is

`d_{n+1} = ⋁_{a₀∧⋯∧a_n=0} (a₀⊠⋯⊠a_n)`.

Hence `I_n(X)` is the closed complement `C(d_{n+1})`. For any indexed open cover `U=(u_i)` of `1`, set
`w_U^{(n+1)}=⋁_i (u_i⊠⋯⊠u_i)`.
The open sublocale `O(w_U)` contains the diagonal. Conversely every open containing the diagonal has a rectangle decomposition `w=⋁_t(a_{t,0}⊠⋯⊠a_{t,n})`, and the opens `c_t=⋀_j a_{t,j}` cover `1` with `w_{(c_t)}≤w`. Therefore

`D_n(X)=⋀_{U covers 1} O(w_U^{(n+1)})`

as an **actual** fitted sublocale. If `X` is coherent, it suffices to use finite compact-open covers. Both `I_n,D_n` are then proconstructible spectral: their defining compact-open congruences will appear in §2. Prime-filter extension proves their spectral point descriptions given in §0: for `I`, all compact-open neighborhoods of the coordinates have nonzero total intersection iff they have a common generization; for `D`, every finite compact-open cover contains a member common to all coordinates iff there is a common specialization. Check the latter by extending the proper ideal generated by compact opens omitted by the intersection of their neighborhood prime filters.

For the oriented relation `A`, define the bisimplicial **rectangle object**

`E_{p,q}(A) = {(x₀,…,x_p;y₀,…,y_q) : x_i≤y_j for all i,j}`

as the intersection of pulled-back copies of `A` inside `X^{p+1}×X^{q+1}`. Let `K_p` be the genuine localic image of `E_{p,0}→X^{p+1}` and `L_q` the image of `E_{0,q}→X^{q+1}`. In coherent `X`, the spectral image lemma and the pointwise witness descriptions identify **actual** sublocales

`K_p=I_p(X)`, `L_q=D_q(X)`.

The rows and columns of `E` are strict Čech nerves:

`E_{p,q} ≅ E_{p,0} ×_{K_p}⋯×_{K_p} E_{p,0}` (q+1 factors),

`E_{p,q} ≅ E_{0,q} ×_{L_q}⋯×_{L_q} E_{0,q}` (p+1 factors).

After `Patch`, these are Čech nerves of **surjective Stone maps**; even if the original unpatched `E_{0,q}→D_q` fails effective descent, the patch surjection is a cover. Applying `h`, realizing rows/columns, and commuting colimits gives the **patched Dowker equivalences**

`I(X) ←~ T(X) ~→ D(X)`, where
`T(X)=|h(Patch diag(E_{••}(A)))|`.

Here the diagonal realization of a bisimplicial object agrees with its iterated realizations. **Audit request:** verify all images and Čech identifications are actual localic, patch preserves the finite limits used, and the chosen site makes both surjections covers. Do not invoke associativity of arbitrary image-defined relation composition.

## 2. Claim A: compact-open presentations and finite-poset approximation

Let `K=K(X)`, and `T_n(K)=K⊔⋯⊔K` be its `(n+1)`-fold coproduct in bounded distributive lattices. Write `k^(j)` for `k` in coordinate `j`.

**Claim A1.** The compact-open lattices of the three coherent simplicial locales are the corresponding quotients of `T_n(K)` generated by:

1. `Ch_n`: `k^(j)≤k^(j+1)` for `k∈K`, `0≤j<n`;
2. `I_n`: `k₀^(0)∧⋯∧k_n^(n)=0` whenever `k₀∧⋯∧k_n=0` in `K`;
3. `D_n`: `∨_{u∈U}(u^(0)∧⋯∧u^(n))=1` for every finite compact-open cover `U⊆K`, `∨U=1`.

**Proof sketch.** (1) is the presented internal specialization relation. (2) imposes the vanishing of each compact rectangular summand of the largest open disjoint from the diagonal. (3) is exactly the finite-cover presentation of fitting from §1. These congruences give genuine compact-preserving quotient maps rather than point-set descriptions.

Every bounded distributive lattice is the filtered union `K=colim_λ K_λ` of its finite bounded sublattices. Write `P_λ=Spec(K_λ)`; the maps `P_μ→P_λ` are spectral surjections by prime-filter extension.

**Claim A2.** As compatible simplicial coherent locales,

`Ch_n(X)≅lim_λ Ch_n(P_λ)`, `I_n(X)≅lim_λ I_n(P_λ)`, `D_n(X)≅lim_λ D_n(P_λ)`.

The proof uses that the equations in A1 involve finitely many compact-open elements and are reflected at an appropriate finite sublattice, so quotient presentations commute with filtered colimits. **No** claim is made that the induced bonding maps between chain spaces or hull spaces are surjective, nor that their geometric realizations commute with the displayed limits. Consequently levelwise equivalences at a cofinal collection of finite stages yield only a pro-space comparison, not a computed homotopy type of the infinite locale.

## 3. Claim B: a finite-poset crosscut comparison, including the canonical maps

For a nonempty finite poset `P` let `Ord(P)` be its order complex, and let `K_up(P)`, `K_low(P)` have nonempty faces precisely the subsets with common upper, respectively lower, bounds. Let `M=Max(P)` and for a nonempty subset `S⊆M`, `B_S=⋂_{m∈S}↓m`. Put `N_M=K_low(P)|_M`.

**Claim B1.** `K_low(P)` strongly collapses onto `N_M` for **every** finite `P`: every nonmaximal vertex `x` is dominated by a chosen maximal `m(x)≥x`; adjoining `m(x)` to a face containing `x` preserves the common-lower-witness property. Independently, `K_up(P)` is covered by full simplices on `↓m`, whose nonempty intersections are simplices on `B_S`, and hence `K_up(P)≃N_M`.

**Claim B2 (map-level crosscut criterion).** If every nonempty `B_S` has contractible `Ord(B_S)`, then **both canonical inclusions**

`Ord(P)↪K_up(P)`, `Ord(P)↪K_low(P)`

are homotopy equivalences.

**Upper proof.** Compare the finite covers of `Ord(P)` by `Ord(↓m)` and of `K_up(P)` by full simplices on `↓m`. Their corresponding intersections are simultaneously empty or contractible; the relative good-cover lemma proves that the specified inclusion is a homotopy equivalence.

**Lower proof.** Choose a simplicial retraction `r:K_low(P)→N_M`, mapping `x↦m(x)`. For a nonempty chain-face `σ⊆P`, let `Φ(σ)={m∈M:maxσ≤m}`. This defines an order-preserving map `F(Ord(P))→F(N_M)^op` whose Quillen lower fibers at `S` are `F(Ord(B_S))`. Quillen's theorem A makes its realization an equivalence. Compare it to the **specified** `r∘inclusion` after barycentric subdivision: for nested chain-faces `σ₀⊂⋯⊂σ_k`, the vertices selected by both maps lie in the simplex `Φ(σ₀)`, making them contiguous. Hence the original lower inclusion is an equivalence by two-out-of-three.

**Controls:** The four-point poset `a,b<c,d` (all four comparisons) has `Ord(P)≃S¹`, but its two witness complexes are contractible. This fails the intersection hypothesis since `B_{c,d}={a,b}`. For face posets of finite simplicial complexes the hypothesis holds because intersections of principal facet downsets are themselves principal or empty. The criterion is classical in spirit (Rota/Björner crosscuts); assess whether the precise canonical-map formulation is also already standard.

## 4. Claim C: the edgewise bridge identifies the canonical maps, not just the targets

For a simplicial object `Y`, define twofold edgewise subdivision `esd₂ Y` by the ordinal-sum functor `ε([n])=[n]*[n]=[2n+1]`, `ε(α)=α*α`. Thus `(esd₂ Ch)_n=Ch_{2n+1}`. Let `a,b:esd₂ Ch→Ch` select the first or second `n+1` coordinates.

**Claim C1 (strict simplicial map).** There is a map of simplicial **locales**

`F:esd₂ Ch•(X)→diag E_{••}(A)`

sending `(x₀≤⋯≤x_{2n+1})` to `(x₀,…,x_n; x_{n+1},…,x_{2n+1})`. The map factors through `E_{n,n}` by internal transitivity and is natural for all simplicial operators via `ε(α)=α*α`. Its composites with the two rectangle projections are exactly `i_I∘a` and `i_D∘b`, for the **canonical** chain-to-hull inclusions `i_I`, `i_D`.

**Claim C2 (explicit homotopy).** For each monotone `θ:[n]→[1]`, define

`H_n((x_j),θ)=(x_{i+(n+1)θ(i)})_{i=0}^n`.

Because `θ` is monotone, this is an increasing chain. The identity `(α*α)∘f_{θ∘α}=f_θ∘α` is claimed to verify that `H` is an actual simplicial homotopy `esd₂ Ch × Δ[1]→Ch` with endpoints `a,b`, **before patching**.

**Claim C3 (equivalence of arrows).** The standard edgewise-subdivision theorem makes the realizations of `a,b` equivalences for simplicial spaces. One can apply it objectwise to simplicial presheaves of spaces, then use colimit-preserving sheafification to obtain it in `H`. The diagonal/iterated-realization comparison is justified in the same way. Combining C1, C2 and the patched Dowker equivalences in §1 gives an equivalence **in the undercategory `C(X)/H`**:

`(C(X)→I(X)) ≃ (C(X)→D(X))`.

In particular, their homotopy cofibers `I(X)⊔_{C(X)}1` and `D(X)⊔_{C(X)}1` are equivalent. Thus one canonical map is an equivalence iff the other is. **Audit request:** independently check the strict homotopy, the use of actual localic maps, the edgewise equivalences for both block projections, and the undercategory identification. A comparison of abstract target homotopy types alone does not suffice.

## 5. Claim D: finite closed-sublocale descent for the canonical map

Fix compact opens `u₁,…,u_r∈K(X)` with `⋀_i u_i=0`, and closed proconstructible downsets `Z_i=C(u_i)`. For nonempty `S⊆[r]`, write `v_S=∨_{i∈S}u_i` and `Z_S=C(v_S)`. The closed sublocale `C(v_S)` has compact-open lattice `K(Z_S)≅[v_S,1]_{K(X)}`; it is empty exactly when `v_S=1`.

Define the **relative ambient upper-witness** simplicial sublocale

`H_n^X(Z)=I_n(X)∧Z^{n+1} ↪ X^{n+1}`.

Its coherent spectral points are tuples in `Z` with a common upper witness **in `X`**, possibly outside `Z`. Generally `H_n^X(Z)≠I_n(Z)`. The canonical chain inclusion restricts to `Ch•(Z)↪H•^X(Z)`.

**Claim D1 (finite-descent theorem).** If for every nonempty `S` the map

`|h(Patch Ch•(Z_S))| → |h(Patch H•^X(Z_S))|`

is an equivalence, then the global canonical map `C(X)→I(X)` is an equivalence, and by Claim C3 so is `C(X)→D(X)`.

**Proof.** For every n, any chain lies in some `Z_i` containing its last element, since each `Z_i` is downward closed. Any tuple in `I_n` has an ambient upper witness `y`; choose a `Z_i` containing `y` and all tuple coordinates lie in `Z_i`. Thus both families `Ch_n(Z_i)→Ch_n(X)` and `H_n^X(Z_i)→I_n(X)` are finite jointly surjective Stone covers after patching. Their iterated Čech intersections are the same constructions on `Z_S`; this equality is actual localic (all terms proconstructible). In the Stone sheaf ∞-topos, realize the degreewise Čech nerves, then commute the Čech and simplicial realizations. The stipulated equivalences on each intersection imply the desired comparison of the specified global arrows. **Audit the cover property and intersection identities carefully**.

**Claim D2 (prime-endpoint sufficient test).** If each nonzero interval `[v_S,1]` has either meet-prime bottom or join-prime top, D1 applies. Meet-prime bottom means `a∧b=v_S⇒a=v_S or b=v_S`, yielding a greatest spectral point in `Z_S`. Then `H_n^X(Z_S)=Z_S^{n+1}` is a Čech nerve over a point, and the chain nerve contracts by its terminal point. Join-prime top means `a∨b=1⇒a=1 or b=1`, yielding a least spectral point. Then the chain nerve is contracted by its initial point, and `H•^X(Z_S)` has an extra degeneracy adding that least point to any ambient-upper-witness tuple.

## 6. Claim E: ambient-upper witnesses and frame-theoretic contractions

Let `Z=C(v)` be a **nonempty** closed coherent sublocale, with `v∈K=K(X)`, and define `T_v={k∈K:k≰v}`. These are the compact opens of `X` meeting `Z` nontrivially. The **ambient upper-bound condition (UB)** is

`∀ finite nonempty (k₁,…,k_m)⊆T_v : k₁∧⋯∧k_m≠0` in `K(X)`.

**Claim E1.** UB iff there is a spectral point `g∈X` above **every** spectral point of `Z`. If UB holds, a prime filter extending the proper filter generated by `T_v` gives `g`, since every compact-open neighborhood of a `z∈Z` belongs to `T_v`. Conversely `g` is in every compact open meeting `Z`. This yields the actual proconstructible equality `H_n^X(Z)=Z^{n+1}` for all `n`, even if `g∉Z`. Therefore the patched realization of `H•^X(Z)` is terminal.

A **specialization-zigzag contraction (SZC)** of `Z` is a finite zigzag of spectral endomorphisms

`id=f₀ ⋚ f₁ ⋚ ⋯ ⋚ f_m=const_z`

for some spectral point `z`, where each adjacent pair is comparable *as maps* in the internal specialization order, with either direction allowed. Equivalently, in `K(Z)=[v,1]`, a finite zigzag of bounded distributive lattice endomorphisms `φ_i` starts with identity, ends with evaluation at a prime filter followed by `2↪K(Z)`, and has adjacent `φ_i` pointwise comparable.

**Claim E2.** SZC implies the contractibility of the realized chain nerve `C(Z)`: each comparable pair induces an internal natural transformation of specialization functors, hence a simplicial homotopy; composing these homotopies connects the identity to a constant map. The converse is **false** already for finite coherent locales: finite-poset ordinary homotopy corresponds to finite comparable-map zigzags, whereas contractibility of the *order complex* is strictly weaker (Cianci–Ottina's nine-point examples, or the dunce-hat face poset below).

**Claim E3 (weaker compact-open criterion).** In Claim D1, it suffices to assume for each nonzero `Z_S` either (i) a join-prime top in `[v_S,1]` (least point) or (ii) UB for `v_S` together with SZC for `Z_S`. In case (i), the least point contracts both local nerves. In case (ii), UB makes the ambient upper-witness nerve a Čech nerve over a point while SZC contracts the chain nerve. Both local realizations are terminal. The prior meet-prime-bottom test is a special case of (ii), since a greatest point gives UB and `id≤const_g`.

**Explicit four-point core.** Let `Z={a,b,c,d}` with `a<c`, `b<c`, `b<d`, no other comparabilities. No point is least or greatest. The monotone map `f(a)=c`, `f(b)=b`, `f(c)=c`, `f(d)=d` gives `id≤f≥const_b`. If an ambient coherent `X` contains this as a closed downset with a common upper bound `p` of all its points, UB holds and Claim E3 applies although both endpoint primality tests fail.

## 7. Claim F: specialization-nerve contractibility and recursive good covers

**Definition.** A nonempty coherent locale `Z` is *specialization-nerve contractible* (SNC) if `C(Z)=|h(Patch Ch•(Z))|≃1` in `H`. This is an isomorphism invariant of coherent frames **relative to this specified homotopy theory**, not a claim about an intrinsic localic shape functor. For finite posets, `C(Z)` is the constant object on the ordinary order-complex homotopy type.

Let `c₁,…,c_r∈K(Z)` with `⋀_i c_i=0` and closed downward sublocales `Y_i=C(c_i)`. Their finite cover nerve is

`N(c)={nonempty S⊆[r]: ∨_{i∈S}c_i≠1}`.

**Claim F1 (finite chain good-cover formula).** If every nonzero `Y_S=C(∨_{i∈S}c_i)` is SNC, then

`C(Z)≃underline(|N(c)|)`.

Proof: `Patch Ch_n(Y_i)` jointly cover `Patch Ch_n(Z)` in every degree, since a chain ending inside a downset lies entirely within it. The intersections are exactly `Patch Ch_n(Y_S)`. Form their Čech nerve, realize first in chain degree, and use SNC for each nonempty intersection (terminal) and the empty intersection (initial). What remains is the ordered simplicial set nerve of the cover, whose realization is the constant object on `|N(c)|`. In particular, if `N(c)` is contractible, then `Z` is SNC. This may be **iterated recursively** on noncontractible intersections with simpler good covers; a one-element cover `Y₁=Z` is a tautological case, not a useful certificate.

**Claim F2 (generalized relative criterion).** In Claim D1, for each nonzero `Z_S`, it suffices that either `Z_S` has a least spectral point, or [UB for `v_S` and SNC for `Z_S`]. The latter can be certified by F1 rather than a zigzag, and this is strictly more general than Claim E3.

## 8. Claim G: coherent realizations of every finite simplicial homotopy type

Let `T` be any **nonempty** finite abstract simplicial complex. Let `P=F(T)` be its finite poset of nonempty faces ordered by inclusion, and choose a vertex `v` of `T`. Adjoin a countable convergent sequence `A={a₁,a₂,…,a_∞}`, all new elements being **maximal** and pairwise incomparable; add comparisons `v<a_n`, `v<a_∞`, and no others beyond transitivity. Give `P` the discrete Stone topology and `A` the convergent-sequence topology, so `a_n→a_∞` is the only nontrivial convergence.

**Claim G1 (Priestley/coherence).** This order is closed: the only new limit comparisons `(v,a_n)` converge to `(v,a_∞)`, which is present. It satisfies Priestley separation: for original face `σ`, `↑σ` is a clopen upset (if `σ=v`, it includes all of `A`; otherwise it is finite); isolated new maxima have clopen upset singletons; a sufficiently long cofinite tail `\{a_∞\}∪\{a_n:n≥N\}` is a clopen upset containing the limit point and excluding any prescribed old face or isolated new maximum. Thus the patch ordered space defines an **infinite coherent locale `X_T`**. It has infinitely many maximal points and nonisolated patch point `a_∞`.

**Claim G2 (finite closed-cover computation).** For every facet `F` of `T`, let `Z_F=↓F` (the nonempty faces contained in `F`), and set `Z_A={v}∪A`. These are patch-clopen downward-closed subspaces, hence closed coherent sublocales with **compact-open complements**. They cover `X_T`. Intersections of the facet pieces are empty or `↓(∩ F_i)` and have a greatest element; intersections with `Z_A` are empty or `{v}`, and `Z_A` itself has least point `v`. Thus every nonzero intersection is SNC and satisfies the prime-endpoint criterion on the relative common-upper-witness objects. The finite closed-cover nerve `N'` is the usual facet-intersection nerve `N_F`, with a cone adjoined on the simplex of facets containing `v`; this attached cone does not change homotopy type. The classical facet good-cover theorem gives `|N_F|≃|T|` (indeed `Ord(F(T))=sd T`). From Claims D1, C3, F1,

`C(X_T) ≃ I(X_T) ≃ D(X_T) ≃ underline(|T|)`, **through the canonical chain-to-hull maps**.

**Claim G3 (dunce-hat obstruction to zigzags).** Choose a finite pure two-dimensional triangulation `T` of the **contractible dunce hat with no free edges**; such triangulations are standard, e.g. eight-vertex examples. Then `P=F(T)` has no beat points, so is noncontractible as a finite Alexandrov space by Stong's theorem, although `|Ord(P)|≃|T|≃*`. Thus `P` admits no finite zigzag of pointwise comparable monotone endomorphisms from `id` to a constant. There is a **spectral retract** `P↪X_T→P` sending all of `A` to the chosen vertex `v`: the map is patch-continuous, monotone and fixes `P`. Any zigzag on `X_T` would retract to a forbidden zigzag on `P`. Hence `X_T` is SNC, and its canonical chain-to-hull comparisons are equivalences, **but it has no SZC**. This shows the weakening in F2 is strict, not merely a different proof certificate.

## 9. Claim H: a noncontractible ambient example beyond the prime-endpoint criterion

Let `Z={a,b,c,d}` with `a<c`, `b<c`, `b<d` as in §6. Adjoin isolated points `e,f,p,q,r`, with every `x∈Z` below both `p` and `q`, and `e<q,r`, `f<r,p`; no further strict comparisons. Adjoin a Stone convergent sequence `A={a₁,a₂,…,a_∞}` of **maximal** points, with `a<a_n`, `a<a_∞`, otherwise incomparable. All points outside the sequence are patch isolated. Let `X` be the resulting spectral Priestley locale.

**Claim H1.** The order is patch-closed and Priestley, and the following patch-clopen downsets define compact-open-complement closed sublocales covering `X`:

`Z_p=↓p=Z∪{f,p}`,
`Z_q=↓q=Z∪{e,q}`,
`Z_r=↓r={e,f,r}`,
`Z_A={a}∪A`.

The nontrivial intersections are

`Z_p∩Z_q=Z`, `Z_p∩Z_r={f}`, `Z_q∩Z_r={e}`, `Z_p∩Z_A={a}`, `Z_q∩Z_A={a}`, `Z_p∩Z_q∩Z_A={a}`;

all others are empty. The sublocale `Z` has **neither least nor greatest point**, but UB holds via `p` (or `q`) and SZC holds by the finite zigzag `id≤f≥const_b` of §6. All other nonempty pieces have a least/greatest point. The finite cover nerve is a triangle boundary `p-q-r-p` together with a filled triangle `p-q-A` along its edge `pq`, thus homotopy equivalent to `S¹`. Therefore Claims D1, E3 and C3 give `C(X)≃I(X)≃D(X)≃underline(S¹)` via the canonical arrows.

**Claim H2 (optional stronger example).** Replace the four-point `Z` inside the preceding construction by the nonempty-face poset `P=F(T)` of a no-free-edge dunce-hat triangulation; retain the new maxima `p,q`, isolated `e,f,r`, convergent maximum sequence `A` above chosen vertex `v∈P`, with `Z_A={v}∪A`. The intersection `Z_p∩Z_q=P` is SNC by Claim F1, and has ambient upper bound `p`, but admits **no** SZC (Stong). All other nonempty intersections have extrema. The same cover-nerve pattern yields `C(X)≃I(X)≃D(X)≃underline(S¹)`. This example is intended to demonstrate a genuine use of the weaker SNC criterion rather than an endomorphism-zigzag certificate. No claim is made that it cannot also be proved by some different closed cover.

## 10. Explicit boundaries and proposed priorities for checking

The submitted results prove statements only **in the specified sheaf ∞-topos on Stone locales**. They do not establish a new homotopy model structure on `Loc`, intrinsic localic shape, unpatched Čech descent of the common-lower-witness maps, nor that realization commutes with the finite-lattice inverse limits. SNC is a homotopy-invariant condition within this chosen theory and is not claimed to classify all coherent frames. The prime-endpoint/UB/SZC/finite-cover criteria are sufficient, not necessary.

Highest-risk dependencies are (i) the strict map of simplicial locales in C1 and the **under-the-source** comparison in C3; (ii) using finite jointly surjective **Stone** covers after patching with the actual relative localic intersections in D1/F1; (iii) the lattice-presented *actual* coherent images and quotient relations; and (iv) the infinite Priestley constructions and retract, which are not proven simply by finite point enumeration. Check these first, and be willing to refute parts of the packet.

## Appendix A. Localic coherence and images (sanitized technical prerequisite)

For bounded distributive `K`, `O(Spec K)=Idl(K)`. A proconstructible spectral sublocale `S↪X` of a coherent locale is the one obtained by restricting its spectral point space `P_X` to a **patch-closed** subset `S_pt`, with induced spectral topology. Its compact-open quotient is `K(X)↠K(S)`; the corresponding frame quotient is `Idl` of that lattice quotient. Conversely a compact-preserving sublocale quotient corresponds to such a bounded distributive lattice quotient and hence a patch-closed spectral subspace. Within this subclass, patch-closed point subsets **do** determine actual sublocales. Arbitrary sublocales do not have this property.

The localic product of coherent locales is coherent, with compact-open bounded lattice the coproduct. The pullback of proconstructible spectral inclusions is the intersection of their pulled-back patch-closed subsets and is again coherent/proconstructible; in algebraic terms its compact-open quotient arises from the lattice pushout. If `f:T→Z` is a spectral map of coherent locales, its point-image `f(P_T)` is patch closed, as the image of a compact patch Stone space in a Hausdorff Stone space. Let `C↪Z` be the corresponding proconstructible sublocale. The factor `T→C` is a locale surjection: it is onto the points of the **spatial** locale `C`, and hence `O(C)→O(T)` is injective. Therefore `C` is the *actual localic image*, not just a guessed point set. Applying this fact to the upper/lower witness projections identifies the actual higher hull images as in §1.

The specialization relation is a genuine compact-preserving quotient sublocale: in the bounded compact-open lattice of `X×X`, impose the congruences `k^(0)=k^(0)∧k^(1)` for every compact open `k`; its `Idl` quotient represents the ordered pairs, not merely the pointwise relation. Ordered chains and rectangle objects are finite intersections of these proconstructible sublocales; compact-open quotient presentations establish the same spatiality and localic existence in each degree.

In the specified Stone sheaf site, a finite jointly surjective family `S_i→S` can be replaced by its finite coproduct map `⊔S_i→S`, a surjective map of compact zero-dimensional Hausdorff locales. Such maps are quotient and effective descent for representables, making the representable family Čech nerve an effective colimit after sheafification. Patching here preserves the finite-limit diagrams from coherent spectral locales, e.g. pullbacks of closed/proconstructible inclusions and products. These statements must not be silently extended to arbitrary locale maps or arbitrary non-subcanonical sites.
