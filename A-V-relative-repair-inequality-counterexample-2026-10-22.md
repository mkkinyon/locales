# Failure of naive relative pseudocomplement repair for arbitrary p

Let X be original rational-bridge Hausdorff space, with rational opens L_0,R_0 and bridge points z_n in closure of both copies. Let q be forward rational-graph null open and w=q∧τ(q) the symmetric bar neighborhood. Then d∨w=1 and its canonical bar has exact rank 3.

Take p=b=L_0. Then b→p=1. But p∨b*∨r_w(b)<1: at any bridge z_n, z_n∉L_0 and z_n∉L_0*=X\cl(L_0). Also z_n∉r_w(L_0), since if an open u∋z_n had u⊠L_0≤w≤τ(q), then its reverse rational-graph pullback would be u_R∩Q, which is nonempty because all bridge neighborhoods have right rational trace nonempty. Contradiction. Hence inequality b→p≤p∨b*∨r_w(b) fails even for a symmetric bar neighborhood w.

This refutes the unrestricted relative repair inequality for arbitrary p; it does not refute the special choice p=∨{a:Φ_w(a)=1} because for this rank-three w the special p equals 1. The dependence on p being generated by all good opens is indispensable for any residual repair strategy.
