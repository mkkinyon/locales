## 1. A single motivating question

Starting from Cameron–Vinod Kumar P. B., *Graphs on Topological Spaces* (arXiv:2610.14783), ask what changes when pointwise graphs Sp, Ins, Cm, Ury, Fun are replaced by actual **sublocale relations on \(X\times_{\mathrm{Loc}}X\)**. The resulting project has two governing issues:

1. **Separation:** which intrinsic relations arise as closure/fitting/reflection constructions of the diagonal or specialization, and which equalities are geometrically meaningful?
2. **Composition and witnesses:** when does localic pullback/image relational composition reproduce graph-path intuition, and where do pointless sublocales, infinite covers, or failed descent invalidate pointwise arguments?

The higher Dowker/Čech branch is the corresponding **simultaneous-witness** question: binary relations alone may not retain the topology/homotopy of witness families.

The project is unified in mathematics, but its major results belong to several genuinely different technical regimes; don't merge them under an unqualified all-locale theorem.

## 2. The intrinsic separation hierarchy (primary backbone)

For locale X, all relations below live in the **localic square**, not in a frame direct product:
\[
\Delta=\Delta_X,\quad \mathcal I=\operatorname{cl}(\Delta),\quad
\mathcal D=\operatorname{fit}(\Delta),\quad
\mathcal V=\operatorname{fit}(\mathcal I),\quad
\mathcal W=\operatorname{cl}(\mathcal D).
\]
Additional nodes: \(\mathcal M=\mathcal I\wedge\mathcal D\), \(\mathcal J=\mathcal I\vee\mathcal D\), closed-and-fitted \(\mathcal H\), Urysohn-style closed \(\mathcal U\), functional \(\mathcal F\). \(\mathcal A\) is the oriented specialization sublocale presented by \(u_1\le u_2\) for every open u, with \(\mathcal S=\mathcal A\vee\mathcal A^\smile\).

**Core point:** \(\mathcal I\) and \(\mathcal D\) are two canonical hulls of specialization:
\[
\mathcal S\le\mathcal I\wedge\mathcal D,\qquad
\operatorname{cl}(\mathcal S)=\mathcal I,\quad
\operatorname{fit}(\mathcal S)=\mathcal D.
\]
For finite Alexandroff spaces, graph points of \(\mathcal D\) match Cameron–Kumar's closure-meeting graph; the hierarchy contains more structure than the ordinary graph comparisons because sublocale points do not determine every relation. The underlying diagonal properties and many extreme equality cases are **classical** (Isbell; Arrieta–Picado–Pultr; Arrieta). The organizing localic-graph dictionary is a project synthesis, whose exact literature overlap is still being checked.

**Coherent locales:** proconstructible/spectral calculus allows genuine associative finite composition and hull formulas, including \(\mathcal A\mathcal A=\mathcal A,\mathcal A\mathcal A^\smile=\mathcal I,\mathcal A^\smile\mathcal A=\mathcal D\) and \(\mathcal S^2=\mathcal J,\ \mathcal S^4=\mathcal U\vee\mathcal V\). These are **not** asserted for arbitrary locales. For coherent X, the previously established identities include \(\mathcal W=\mathcal U\). See [coherent composition canonical](ProjectA_coherent_composition_canonical_proof_2026-11-08.md), [Cameron–Kumar crosswalk](ProjectA_Cameron_Kumar_revisit_graph_to_locale_hierarchy_2026-11-10.md), [comparability J/T theorem](ProjectA_J_T_COMPARABILITY_GRAPH_STABLY_COMPACT_CANONICAL_2026-11-17.md).

**Noncoherent phenomena:** no global collapse. An externally audited second-countable, locally compact, sober T1 étale example has
\[
\mathcal D=\Delta,\qquad
\mathcal W=\mathcal I < \mathcal W_2=\mathcal U=X\times_{\mathbb R}X,
\]
refuting universal Q and Q′. Do not treat historical ledger labels “Q open” as current. See [dense-rational-bridge canonical](ProjectA_Q_DENSE_RATIONAL_BRIDGE_CANONICAL_2026-11-14.md). Other counterexamples concern \(\mathcal V\)-rank, strict hull diamonds and failure of naive graph realization; preserve their individual hypotheses.

## 3. Positive and negative localic relational composition (second backbone)

Actual relational composite \(R\circ S\) means the **image** of the **genuine pullback** \(R\times_XS\). In general this is not associative. The general obstruction was already documented categorically (A. Klein 1970; van der Schaaf 2026, §12.2, citing failed pullback stability of epis). Our results should be presented as **positive associative domains within the general operation**:

- **Coherent proconstructible regime.** Under coherent/patch hypotheses, composition is described by a spectral/pointwise-compatible calculus and associates on the permitted classes. [canonical proof](ProjectA_coherent_composition_canonical_proof_2026-11-08.md).
- **Compact-Hausdorff rectangles.** Distinguish \(p(A,B)=[A\times B]\) (point-generated) from \(q(A,B)=[A]\times_{\mathrm{Loc}}[B]\) (full potentially nonspatial product). The independently audited rectangular rule RC says that composing any two p/q generators returns the full outer q rectangle exactly when the middle **induced sublocales** meet nontrivially; finite-join algebra is associative. [postreferee](ProjectA_CLIQUE_RECTANGULAR_CALCULUS_POSTREFEREE_CANONICAL_2026-11-20.md).
- **Graph extensions.** Locally closed charts and later balanced-witness criteria give sufficient conditions to adjoin graph relations and their converses while retaining associativity. Some finite-chart necessity claims were refuted by later examples. The general TWIST partial-graph extrapolation **is not established**, even though its complete-lattice transport component is correct. [graph extension postreferee](ProjectA_GRAPH_EXTENSIONS_POSTREFEREE_CANONICAL_2026-11-21.md), [balanced witness reconciliation](ProjectA_BALANCED_WITNESS_SCATTERED_MATRIX_POSTREFEREE_RECONCILIATION_2026-11-21.md).
- **Scattered-index matrix calculus.** For compact Hausdorff P and scattered compact Hausdorff K, all sublocales of \(P^n\times K^n\) decompose into **full possibly pointless P^n-sector sublocales**. Genuine binary compositions of any R,S satisfy
\[
(R\circ S)_{ik}=\bigvee_{j\in K}R_{ij}\circ_P S_{jk}.
\]
This does **not** make arbitrary relations associative. Row-and-column finite matrices over an associative fibre algebra are associative and converse-closed; graph-path expansions have only finite intermediate walks. Discontinuous index permutations may act through genuine graph relations. The referee's discrete-index comparison shows the entire **binary** relation calculus cannot detect the scattered index topology (though openness/closedness and locale isomorphism are not preserved). [reconciliation](ProjectA_BALANCED_WITNESS_SCATTERED_MATRIX_POSTREFEREE_RECONCILIATION_2026-11-21.md).

**Interpretive theme:** between naive set relations and unconstrained locale relations lies a rich class of *controlled witness* calculations where one may use finite distributivity, patch compactness, or atomic index coordinates without illegitimate infinitary exchanges. Important adjacent literature includes Jung–Kegelmann–Moshier on closed relations, Moshier–Picado–Pultr on generated sublocales, and van der Schaaf on open cones. Exact novelty judgments are **pending**.

## 4. Higher relations, Dowker and classifying anima (third backbone)

For coherent X with patch Stone space V and specialization order \(\le\), define canonical simplicial **simultaneous-witness** spaces, such as
\[
\mathrm{Ch}_n=\{(x_0,\ldots,x_n):x_0\le\cdots\le x_n\},\quad
I_n=\{(x_i):\exists u\ \forall i,\ x_i\le u\},\quad
D_n=\{(x_i):\exists a\ \forall i,\ a\le x_i\}.

Their patched Stone sheaf objects and realizations allow higher comparison maps. Important fourth-audit results include binary reconstruction of patched higher hulls, the chain-meet 1-type description, and a sharp 2-connectivity comparison, subject to stated coherent/site/premise hypotheses; do not present these as intrinsic *arbitrary-locale shape*.
