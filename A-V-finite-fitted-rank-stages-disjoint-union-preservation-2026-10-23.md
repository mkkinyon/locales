# Finite fitted rank stages preserve arbitrary disjoint unions

23 October 2026. Let n>=2 and X=disjoint union of locales X_i, equivalently L=product_i O(X_i). Define V_n as the fitted sublocale of X×locX generated by open bar nuclei of finite separation rank<=n.

THEOREM. V_n(X) has only diagonal component blocks and restricts on the block X_i×locX_i exactly to V_n(X_i). Off-diagonal blocks X_i×locX_j (i≠j) have zero V_n intersection. This includes infinite disjoint unions and nonspatial locales.

PROOF. Product distributivity for localic products gives
  X×locX = disjoint union_(i,j) (X_i×locX_j),
so Q=product_(i,j) Q_ij where Q_ij=O(X_i×locX_j).

Any nucleus j on such a product is coordinatewise: writing c_ij for the complemented open selecting the (i,j) block, meet-preservation and inflationarity imply
  j(x)∧c_ij = j(x∧c_ij)∧c_ij.
Hence the coordinate of j(x) at block (i,j) depends only on x_ij. The full nuclei lattice and joins are therefore componentwise.

OFFDIAGONAL. For i≠j let c=X_j be the complemented global component open, c' its complement. Rank-two partition strip
  p=(c⊠1)∨(1⊠c')
is a bar and restricts to ZERO on block X_i×locX_j (first component i≠j, second j); explicit depth2 certificate as in A-V-fixed-rank-fitted-clopen-descent-K-fans-2026-10-23.md. Thus its open nucleus o_p is TOP on that block. Since p has rank<=n, the rank-n nucleus j_n also acts as top there, so V_n has no offdiagonal block.

DIAGONAL. Restrict any global rank<=n canonical bar v to X_i²: the pulled-back leaves form a valid tree of rank<=n for the local bar. Hence the restriction of global j_n to the (i,i) block is ≤local j_n(X_i). Conversely, by the no-overhead clopen extension lemma (valid for n>=2), any local rank<=n bar u_i extends to a global rank<=n bar U that agrees with u_i on X_i² and is TOP outside. Thus every local generator nucleus appears as diagonal restriction of a global generator, giving ≥ and therefore equality.

CONSEQUENCES.
- V_n(X)=coproduct_i V_n(X_i), as a sublocale relation supported on diagonal components, for every n>=2. This extends prior preservation of full V under disjoint union to every finite rank-n fitted approximation.
- If V_n(X_i)=V(X_i) on every component i, then V_n(X)=V(X), without any bound on individual finite tree rank.
- On X_infty=disjoint union of iterated K-fans, V_2=V despite the existence of a symmetric canonical bar of exact ordinal rank omega.
- The failure of V_2=V on the rational graph bridge example is not caused by disjoint-union aggregation; the obstruction is genuinely fused within one locale.

NOTE: n>=2 is necessary for offdiagonal elimination by rank2 partition strips. For n=0,1 only rank0 bar w=1 exists and j_n=id, so V_n is the entire square and the diagonal-component statement fails for a nontrivial disjoint union.
