# Project A — normal forms and the rectangular ideal in the partial-homeomorphism graph algebra
## 21 November 2026 · INTERNAL algebraic structural corollary

**Prerequisites:** The [compact-Hausdorff finite rectangular composition calculus](ProjectA_CLIQUE_RECTANGULAR_CALCULUS_POSTREFEREE_CANONICAL_2026-11-20.md) is independently blind-refereed. The [extension by locally closed partial-homeomorphism graphs](ProjectA_GRAPH_LOCAL_HOMEOMORPHISM_POSITIVE_OPEN_PROPER_NEGATIVE_2026-11-20.md) is **internal**, as are the present deductions. [Internal adversarial audit](ProjectA_GRAPH_CHART_RANK_INTERNAL_ADVERSARIAL_AUDIT_2026-11-21.md) concerns the graph-extension prerequisites. No novelty/priority claim.

Let G be compact Hausdorff. For ordinary A,B⊆G set p(A,B)=[A×B] and q(A,B)=[A]×_Loc[B]. Let \(\mathcal R\) be all **finite joins** of 0,p(A,B),q(A,B), and let \(\mathcal P\) be all **finite joins** of graphs Γ_h of locally closed partial homeomorphisms h:U_h→V_h (including the empty map). Write
\[
\mathcal A=\{r\vee s:r\in\mathcal R,\ s\in\mathcal P\}.
\]
This is precisely the internally derived associative, finite-join-distributive, converse-closed algebra generated by all these relations; Γ_id=Δ_G is its unit.

### Proposition 1 (rectangular two-sided ideal)

\[
\boxed{\mathcal A\circ\mathcal R\subseteq\mathcal R,\qquad
\mathcal R\circ\mathcal A\subseteq\mathcal R.}
\]
Consequently \(\mathcal R\) is a two-sided, finite-join-closed, converse-closed **ideal** in the idempotent semiring (under \(\vee,\circ\)) \(\mathcal A\).

**Proof.** The independent referee's (RC) says product of any two p/q rectangular generators is 0 or a full q-rectangle, hence in \(\mathcal R\). The internally established graph action says for t∈{p,q}
\[
t(A,B)\circ\Gamma_h=t(A,h(B\cap U_h)),\qquad
\Gamma_h\circ t(A,B)=t(h^{-1}(A\cap V_h),B),
\]
again in \(\mathcal R\). Composition distributes over **finite** joins, which proves both inclusions. Converse exchanges p(A,B) and p(B,A), q(A,B) and q(B,A). □

The finite-join graph part \(\mathcal P\) is a converse-closed composition subalgebra, generated by the **inverse semigroup** of individual locally closed partial homeomorphism graphs. Do not assert that the finite-join closure \(\mathcal P\) itself is an inverse semigroup; its elements may be multivalued relations.

### Proposition 2 (three normal-form types for indexed words)

For a **fully parenthesized finite word** w of individual generators from {p,q,Γ_h}, the output type is uniquely determined (independent of bracketing by the internal associativity theorem):

1. If **no rectangle letters** occur, the output is Γ_h for one locally closed partial homeomorphism h (possibly empty).
2. If **exactly one rectangle letter** t(A,B), the output is t(A',B') for the **same type** t∈{p,q}, after the partial graph blocks on either side restrict/reindex the two endpoint sets. The output may be zero when a restriction is empty.
3. If **at least two rectangle letters** occur, the output is **either zero or a full q(A',B') rectangle**.

**Proof.** Consecutive graph letters compose to one Γ_h of a locally closed partial homeomorphism. Graph actions on rectangles preserve the p/q type. Once a second rectangle is encountered, the independent (RC) saturation replaces the resulting product by 0 or a full q rectangle. Every later graph action on a q rectangle stays of q type; every later rectangle–rectangle composition again yields q or 0. Zeros remain zero. Associativity ensures this left-to-right evaluation equals every bracketing. □

The third case makes the referee's "one composition saturates to a full localic rectangle" into a **universal saturation-after-two-rectangle-events** rule, even for arbitrarily interleaved partial-homeomorphism graph events. It is a useful symbolic normal form for computing graph paths in this positive algebra.

### Relation to current finite-fiber map results

The [Michael selection corollary](ProjectA_OPEN_FINITE_TO_ONE_STONE_MAPS_SELECTION_THEOREM_2026-11-21.md) places the graph of any open uniformly finite-to-one map from compact metrizable X to compact metrizable zero-dimensional Y in \(\mathcal P\) (after disjoint-union embedding), with exactly N locally closed graph charts when the maximum fiber size is N. Thus words built from such graphs and arbitrary rectangular witnesses have the normal forms above.

The [Cantor-to-Cantor at-most-two-to-one negative map](ProjectA_FINITE_FIBRE_GRAPH_CHART_RANK_CANTOR_COUNTEREXAMPLE_2026-11-21.md) has **infinite chart rank** and its graph cannot be adjoined associatively to \(\mathcal A\), as detected by a separate graph-probe associator. This clarifies why *bounded fiber multiplicity* does not by itself imply finite symbolic witness complexity.

**Big-picture interpretation:** \(\mathcal P\) records the reversible locally closed **partial paths**; \(\mathcal R\) is a two-sided ideal recording complete bipartite **witness saturation**. The first encounter of two rectangular letters makes subsequent path information insensitive to the p versus q distinction: the output already contains the entire potentially nonspatial endpoint product. This packages a substantial portion of the graph-theoretic program as a concrete algebra of localic relations, without asserting associativity on all sublocales of G².
