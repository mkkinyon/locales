## 2. Exact frame normal form

For any a∈O(V), define the set of indices

    Θ(a)={n∈N: ∃W∈O(C), K_n⊆W, and W∩V⊆a}.

Then every Y-open O is UNIQUELY of the form a∪S with a∈O(V) and S⊆Θ(a), using the same letter N for the attached discrete set. The frame

    O(Y) = {(a,S): a∈O(V), S⊆Θ(a)}

has componentwise arbitrary joins and finite meets; top is (V,N) and bottom (0,∅). Proof: an open with trace a and containing n has a basic n-neighborhood {n}∪(W∩V) wholly inside it, giving n∈Θ(a); conversely unite the open trace a with such basic n-neighborhoods for each n∈S.

An arbitrary family {(a_i,S_i)} is a Y-cover iff

    ⋁_i a_i=V,    ⋃_i S_i=N.

The Θ condition implies that its trace {a_i} is an ordinary V-cover satisfying the EXTRA CONDITION:

    (★) for every n∈N there exists i with n∈Θ(a_i).

Conversely, ANY V-cover {a_i} with (★) extends to a Y-cover by choosing S_i=Θ(a_i). We call such covers **admissible**.

## 3. The mixed fitted-diagonal fiber P_n is ZERO for every n

Write G=D_Y. Restrict G to the locally closed block {n}×V. On a generalized pair with first coordinate the constant point n, each Y-cover test reduces to

    ⋁_{O∈cover, n∈O} z^*(O∩V)=1.

Because Y is T1, the two-element covers {O,Y\{n}} exist for every Y-neighborhood O of n. The second open omits n and cannot contribute. Thus G|({n}×V) is EXACTLY the fitted V-sublocale

    P_n=⋂_{W∈O(C),K_n⊆W} O_V(W∩V).

But C is REGULAR; hence each CLOSED K_n⊆C is a FITTED sublocale:

    K_n = ⋂_{W⊇K_n} O_C(W)

as an intersection of open sublocales. Let j:V→C be the sublocale embedding given by the ordinary subspace inclusion (its frame inverse image is the surjection O(C)→O(V)). Pulling back and using the fact inverse image preserves all sublocale meets and open sublocales,

    P_n = j^-1(K_n).

Because K_n is a FINITE discrete locale, it is a finite union of one-point sublocales {k}. Pullback preserves finite sublocale unions, so j^-1(K_n)=∨_{k∈K_n}j^-1({k}). Each inverse image j^-1({k}) is ZERO: it is a sublocale of the one-point locale {k}, hence either 0 or the point, and nonzero would force a point of V mapped to k, impossible because k∉V.

Therefore

    **P_n=0 for all n.**

As N is discrete, N×V≅⊔_n V and an arbitrary sublocale is determined by its n-slices. So

    **G∧(N×V)=0=G∧(V×N).**

This is not a pointwise argument; the nonzero-pointlessness risk in individual V blocks is eliminated by the FINITENESS of each K_n, not by spatiality of V alone.

## 5. Exact admissible-cover formula for A

## 6. A finiteness observation: basic finite clopen covers impose no conditions

For k≥1 and n>k, the finite K_n meets EVERY basic length-k cylinder of C: prescribe the k initial bits arbitrarily, set bit n=1 and all later bits 0. Thus if O_i⊊C is a C-clopen (finite union of length-k cylinders) then K_n⊄O_i for all sufficiently large n.

Let a_i=O_i∩V, with O_i clopen in C. If n∈Θ(a_i), there is W⊇K_n with W∩V⊆O_i. Since V is DENSE in C and O_i CLOSED, necessarily W⊆O_i, hence K_n⊆O_i. Thus a_i is Θ-admissible for n≫0 only if O_i=C, i.e. a_i=V.

**Therefore any FINITE admissible V-cover consisting of traces of Cantor-clopen sets must contain V itself**, and its D-cover test is tautologically top. Yet such finite clopen covers separate Δ_V since V is zero dimensional. This explains concretely why the restricted cover family can yield A>Δ_V.

This is a finitary NORMAL-FORM obstruction; it does not say that every admissible cover is infinite or that arbitrary finite admissible covers are trivial (finite covers containing non-clopen dense opens may be admissible).

## 11. Concrete obstruction: an admissible cover with NO admissible star refinement

This is a genuine negative answer to the standard *cover-star-refinement route* toward transitivity of A, NOT a solution to associativity of A³.

Fix a point p∈V. Choose a nontrivial CANTOR-CLOPEN cylinder B⊂C with p∈B and C\B≠∅. Put

    a=V\{p} (an open, dense subset of V),
    b=B∩V (open proper neighborhood of p).

Then C0={a,b} is an admissible V-cover. Indeed W=C\{p} is Cantor-open, contains EVERY K_n (because p∈V disjoint all K_n), and W∩V=a. Hence Θ(a)=N, so C0 satisfies (★).

**Proposition (ordinary cover-theoretic).** The admissible cover C0 has NO admissible STAR REFINEMENT.

Proof. Suppose E is an admissible V-cover star-refining C0. Pick e_p∈E with p∈e_p, and Cantor-open O_p with O_p∩V=e_p. Since O_p is a nonempty Cantor-open, and C\B is a nonempty Cantor-clopen, the finite sets K_n MEET BOTH O_p and C\B for all sufficiently large n (each K_n at sufficiently large n meets every fixed finite cylinder).

Choose such an n. Since E is admissible, some e_n∈E has n∈Θ(e_n), i.e. a Cantor-open W_n⊇K_n satisfies W_n∩V⊆e_n. Both W_n∩O_p and W_n∩(C\B) are nonempty Cantor-open; since V is DENSE, each meets V. Thus e_n meets e_p (so Star_E(e_n) includes e_p and hence contains p) and e_n meets V\b (so Star_E(e_n) is not contained in b). It is also not contained in a=V\{p} since it contains p. This contradicts the assumption Star_E(e_n) is contained in one of {a,b}.

Thus the admissible-cover family is **NOT star-refinable**, despite the intrinsic metric zero-dimensional regular locale V having star refinements of all its open covers.

**Important logical caveat:** this proves failure of a familiar SUFFICIENT construction for A²≤A, not failure of transitivity of A, and certainly not failure of associativity of A³. An intersection of cover-entourages may be transitive even if the cover family does not admit star refinements. To obtain a localic counterexample one still needs a NONZERO generalized composable-pair source whose endpoint image escapes an admissible cover's diagonal-neighborhood open.
