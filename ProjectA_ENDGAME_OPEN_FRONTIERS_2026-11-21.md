Determine exact hypotheses needed for splitting arbitrary sublocales, whether source and target versions are enough for **actual** matrix MUL, and publish a separate proof or leave as I/C. **Do not pretend the compact-Hausdorff E audit already covers it.**
2. **Exactly specified partial-monomial TWIST calculus.** The complete sublocale-lattice transport between a compact scattered index and its discrete underlying set is accepted. The generic extrapolation to graph relations on unspecified locally closed sectors was rejected as insufficiently proved. Can one formulate a common-ambient, pullback/image-preserving sector theorem with honest hypotheses? The referee gave a valid *common-index partial monomial* subcase. Archive that subcase; leave the broad statement open.
3. **Canonical Coherent Theorem B proof dependency.** Compare its intrinsic compact-open quotient image proof with standard regular-category proof for Stone **closed** relations. This is primarily an exposition/priority problem, not an obvious open theorem: actual Loc image equality is proved, but the most economical bridge to literature remains to be written.
4. **Fourth-audit premises P1–P6 and site conventions.** Record precisely which independent earlier audits supplied which assumptions of the patched higher hull/Čech results. Site-size independence and intrinsic shape were never proved; don't omit these qualifications in the final overview.
5. **Proof-status splits for referee addenda.** Keep exact labels for (i) discrete-index comparison, (ii) row-/column-finite addenda, (iii) Stone-clique exact rank \(n+2\), (iv) fourth-audit cross-checked 2-connectivity/acyclic-constancy, (v) internal all-groups H¹ strengthening. No blanket E label.

### Priority B: specific substantive mathematics worth resuming later
6. **Intrinsic characterization of the intermediate localic hulls.** Is there a more structural frame/locale description of \(\mathcal V=\operatorname{fit}\mathcal I\), \(\mathcal W=\operatorname{cl}\mathcal D\), and the least simultaneously closed-and-fitted hull \(\mathcal H\) beyond known closure/filter representations and coherent graph powers? Consult [post-V consolidation](A-postV-consolidation-2026-10-24.md) and W audits.
7. **Characterize the restricted classes where \(\mathcal H\) is transitive.** The **universal assertion is already false**, even for coherent locales, by the **externally checked two convergent zigzag rays** \( \mathcal H^2\nleq\mathcal H\), [post-V audit](A-postV-external-audit-reconciliation-2026-10-24.md). For coherent locales, \(\mathcal H\) transitive iff \(\mathcal H=\mathcal F\). In the noncoherent setting an **internally checked** locally compact sober scattered T1 example has **transitive \(\mathcal H<\mathcal F\)**, [noncoherent note](A-H-transitivity-noncoherent-counterexample-2026-10-24.md). Thus only finer classification questions—not “Is H always transitive?”—remain open.
8. **Finite-rank filtration and finer ordinal behavior.** \(\mathcal V\subsetneq\mathcal V_{\rm fin}\) and exact \(\omega\)-rank examples are settled. A general classification of achievable separation-tree ranks/limiting fitted nuclei, especially under compactness, regularity or sobriety, was not completed; higher ordinal searches were intentionally deprioritized.
9. **Associative fragments beyond finite rectangular sums.** RC, locally closed graph extensions and locally finite matrices are positive. Find useful necessary/sufficient pullback-image criteria for general graph correspondences or safe nonrectangular extensions without demanding full associativity of \(\mathrm{Sub}(X^2)\). Graph probes and balanced-witness criteria give controls.
10. **Quotient/effectivity and ranks beyond the explicit finite-fence model.** The repaired four-step explicit model and generic-fibre localization are proved under their concrete conditions, but a general theorem prescribing effectivity rank from graph/patch geometry is not established. Fixed parenthesizations matter.
11. **Strong preservation statements under products and quotients.** Existing finite-factor, compact, spectral and stably compact results have different scopes. A truly general preservation classification remains open; beware easy false extrapolations from finite posets or products where all sublocales happen to be spatial.

### Priority C: higher witnesses, Dowker and homotopy
12. **Global higher homotopy from binary data?** The 1-type reconstructs; sharp 2-connectivity does not yield π₂ injectivity and six-point counterexamples refute 2-type recovery. Determine exactly what additional higher simultaneous-witness data restore full higher homotopy, potentially using the crosscut connectivity ladder.
13. **Intrinsic locale shape and site-size independence.** The canonical results live in a specified patched Stone-sheaf ∞-topos; no general intrinsic-localic-shape theorem or uniform site-size-independence was proved. This is a major independent program, not a necessary step for finishing the current archive.
14. **Nonconstant relative Stone gluing and twisted coefficients.** The finite-pair acyclic constancy criterion is settled under stated hypotheses. A converse for arbitrary infinite simplicial pairs, direct fitted-side gluing along arbitrary lower ideals, and full intrinsic H¹ computations remain unresolved or only partially established. Consult [fourth-audit reconciliation](PointFree_audit4_FINAL_RECONCILIATION_2026-11-11.md).
15. **Crosscut ladder and classifying-anima comparison.** Internal patched Stone crosscut connectivity theorem may merit a dedicated audit; Haine's classifying-anima identification is definition-level and not a new construction. Accurate comparison with the latest literature is more urgent than forcing a theorem.
