# Transitivity of the alternating diagonal hull H

21 October 2026. Status: OPEN for arbitrary locales; positive cases retained.

Let H be the least sublocale of X squared containing the diagonal that is both closed and fitted. The coordinate-swap involution preserves both closedness and fittedness and fixes the diagonal; consequently H is reflexive and symmetric. Every closed-and-fitted internal equivalence relation containing the diagonal contains H. Transitivity is not established generally.

**Key reduction.** If D=U then U is both closed (definition) and fitted (because U=D=fit of the diagonal). Hence H is contained in U by minimality; conversely D=U is contained in H because H contains the fitted hull D. Thus H=U whenever D=U. Therefore a universal proof that H is transitive would prove the previously unresolved arbitrary-frame implication D=U implies U transitive. This reduction should prevent mistakenly treating H transitivity as an easier independent problem.

**Positive cases.** In finite Alexandrov locales H=F, hence transitive. In the verified lower-point deleted-plank example H=U and U is transitive by the established continuous-frame theorem. These observations do not settle the arbitrary-frame case.

**Conclusion.** No general proof or counterexample obtained in this segment. Do not claim H is an equivalence relation. Return to the J fixed-point problem, which is independent of this transitivity reduction.
