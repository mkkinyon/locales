## Reading guide

The primary relations in `Q=O(X)⊕O(X)` are `I=clΔ`, `D=fitΔ`, `U=C(d_U)`, and `F=⋂_f ker(f)`; derived `V=fit I`, `W=cl D`, `M=I∧D`, `J=I∨D`, `H` the least simultaneously closed and fitted hull of Δ, and `T(S)=cl(S)∧fit(S)`. The notation `D` (fitted diagonal) has nothing to do with `F`-separatedness. `V_n` is the fitted approximation generated by rank≤n rectangular bars. All localic composition is in diagrammatic order; parentheses are mandatory in general.

**Statuses:** `Published` means external background theorem; `E` means a specifically scoped external mathematical check; `E*` means a referee-origin strengthening subsequently checked internally but not separately blind-refereed as an author claim; `I` means an internally supported working proof; `W` means a specific proof gap or unintegrated repair. A slash indicates distinct sources or parts with distinct provenance. “Referee-origin” marks a proof supplied by a referee, not independent confirmation of a finished author's proof. **Open mathematical problems are exclusively in the companion register.** Status does not assess novelty.

**Core dependency spine:** frame coproduct and sublocale calculus → named relations and hulls → (a) coherent spectral compact-open filters/patch → normal–conormal classifications and `H`; (b) C-ideal separation trees → finite-rank and `V_fin` results; (c) localic images/base change → mixed composition and associativity; (d) proper-quotient theory → functional-collapse consequences. An external audit of (a) does not certify later localic-image work in (c).
