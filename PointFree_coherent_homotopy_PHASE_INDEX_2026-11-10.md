## New research after third audit — **working, not externally audited**

- **[Relative Stone-indexed gluing and nonsuspension common-hull objects](PointFree_Stone_indexed_relative_full_subcomplex_nonsuspension_2026-11-10.md)** — extends the canonical Ch/I fibrewise gluing from a single minimal point to any **lower ideal reflecting common upper witnesses**. Every finite simplicial pair K⊂T becomes admissible after barycentric subdivision. A 9-vertex triangulated torus glued over its essential full-subcomplex circle gives canonical C≃I≃D with nonconstant H₂, nonzero cup products, and a **non-suspension not even a wedge of any family of finite CW complexes** (for infinite Stone S). Explicit finite checks: [periodic torus control script](tools/check_periodic_torus_pair.py). **INTERNALLY CHECKED ONLY; not part of the third referee's blind A1–E3 certificate.**

- **[Relative chain splitting and homologically invisible nonconstant aspherical common hulls](PointFree_relative_chain_splitting_acyclic_aspherical_nonconstant_2026-11-10.md)** — a general derived R-chain formula for Stone-indexed finite-pair gluing; relative field homology detects nonconstancy exactly when the finite pair has nonzero relative homology. A **finite acyclic but noncontractible** 2-complex from the binary icosahedral group, and a stronger **aspherical** finite complex from Higman's group, yield coherent locales whose **canonical C→I,D comparisons are equivalences**, whose common homology sheaves are **all constant (those of a point)**, but whose **π₁ sheaf is nonconstant**. Higman's model is 1-truncated, perfect and has no finite quotients at point evaluation. **New internal working mathematics, not externally audited.**

- **[Twisted coefficients for Higman-based common-hull homotopy](PointFree_Higman_twisted_cohomology_continuity_threshold_2026-11-10.md)** — the chosen continuation: ordinary/finitely generated coefficients and finite-dimensional monodromy remain blind, but the infinite-rank augmentation ideal carries the **Berstein–Schwarz class** \(g\mapsto g-1\), and duality detects degree-two regular/group-ring cohomology. New **internal** calculation of the folded \(H^1\) sheaf in terms of \(\operatorname{LC}(S,I_H)/I_H\) and \(\operatorname{LC}(S,\mathbb Z)\); contrasts sheaf-internal continuous cocycles with arbitrary point-evaluated cocycles. **New working math, not externally audited.**
- **[Parked roadmap for feedback into the original binary hierarchy](PointFree_binary_relation_homotopy_feedback_ROADMAP_2026-11-10.md)** — explicitly preserves the alternative route, with a concrete Stone-indexed face-poset benchmark having **binary \(\mathcal I\) and \(\mathcal D\) incomparable** but **both canonical chain-to-hull realizations equivalent**. Prioritizes cross-fibre relation geometry, skeletal filtrations, and \(\mathcal U,\mathcal V,\mathcal W,\mathcal H\) calculations. **Parked, not abandoned.**

- **[Intrinsic augmentation-ideal nonconstant twisted H¹](PointFree_intrinsic_augmentation_ideal_nonconstant_H1_2026-11-10.md)** — **completed bounded twisted-coefficient segment**. For any infinite Stone S and discrete G with an infinite-order element, the intrinsic \(\Pi=*_{h(S)}G\) augmentation ideal \(J_\Pi\) has a **nonconstant internal twisted first-cohomology sheaf**, as does the full regular ring \(Z[\Pi]\). Explicit families of principal derivations on separate G-factors give infinitely many distinguishable point-values; a four-distinct-reduced-words argument rules out global principal equivalence. Contrasts the previous **constant** folded-coefficient H¹. **New internally checked theorem, not externally audited**. We now return to the [parked binary-relation feedback roadmap](PointFree_binary_relation_homotopy_feedback_ROADMAP_2026-11-10.md).

- **[Binary rectangle groupoid quotient and relative skeletal layers](PointFree_binary_rectangle_pi1_relative_skeleton_2026-11-10.md)** — return to the parked binary hierarchy. Both canonical chain-to-hull maps induce the same quotient on fundamental groupoids, by normal closure of four-edge rectangle loops in \(E_{1,1}\). Relative first skeletal homology retains the extra ordered binary edges; witness triangles kill that homology in degree 2. Explicit Stone-indexed triangle wedge: \(L_I=M^{12}\), \(L_D=M^{10}\oplus N^2\oplus Q^2\) with \(M=R[h(S)],N=R[h(S^2)],Q=\operatorname{coker}(M\to N)\) along the diagonal. **Working only; not externally audited.**

- **[Binary triangle hierarchy and formal-hull rank at fixed homotopy type](PointFree_binary_triangle_hierarchy_formal_hull_rank_2026-11-10.md)** — explicit coherent calculations of all named binary relations on the pointed triangle wedge: cross-fibre D is the incident-edge square, U=W the down-saturated square, V the union of incident-edge rows and columns. Both lower and upper diamonds are strict; U,V incomparable even though their finite pair counts agree. The alternating closed stages are I, I³, I⁵=H=F. More generally the first fixed closed stage on a connected finite pointed-poset wedge is the eccentricity of v in the I-graph; attaching arbitrarily long whiskers to the triangle gives **unbounded finite formal-hull rank with fixed nonconstant canonical common homotopy**. **Working, not externally audited.**

- **[Fixed binary hierarchy, arbitrary finite comparison obstructions](PointFree_binary_fixed_hierarchy_arbitrary_comparison_obstruction_2026-11-10.md)** — the reverse direction: the reverse face poset of any connected finite complex K, with a new minimal vertex below the face poset of a spanning tree, has chain realization |K| and terminal hulls. Attach this gadget to the triangle at its minimal vertex and take the S-indexed pointed wedge. The canonical comparisons are \(E_S\vee(h(S)_+\wedge\underline{|K|})\to E_S\), the projection, with \(E_S=h(S)_+\wedge\underline{S^1}\) fixed. The full displayed binary Hasse pattern and formal-hull rank 2 are fixed. Acyclic noncontractible K even gives non-equivalences with terminal cofibres. **Working, not externally audited.**

- **[Binary reconstruction and fixed higher hulls with unbounded obstruction degrees](PointFree_binary_reconstruction_fixed_hulls_unbounded_obstructions_2026-11-10.md)** — an actual fixed-patched-relation counterexample: the six-point three-layer ordinal sums with middle antichain versus chain have identical \(I_n,D_n\) at every arity but chain types \(S^2\) versus 1. A reconstruction theorem shows the binary \(\mathcal I\) graph determines all patched \(I_n\) (simplicial-vertex neighborhoods recover maximal-witness downsets), dually for D. Moreover the binary pair determines the finite chain fundamental groupoid and the canonical comparisons on 1-types via a duplicated-extrema/flattened incidence poset. A fixed Cantor middle space attached to the Stone-indexed triangle gives the very same patched higher hulls, named binary hierarchy, formal-hull rank 2 and nonconstant targets, with equivalence or nonconstant homological obstructions in arbitrarily high degrees. **Working, not externally audited.**

- **[Coherent binary chain 1-type reconstruction via paired extrema](PointFree_coherent_binary_1type_paired_extrema_2026-11-10.md)** — extends finite reconstruction to *all coherent locales*: take the patch closures of matched maximal and minimal pairs across two realizations of the same patched binary pair. A compact three-layer incidence space and local witness path replacements give an isomorphism of chain edge-path groupoids on the common vertex sheaf, simultaneously compatible with both hull comparisons. No compactness of actual extremal loci is needed. This is the final working research segment before the next audit, **not independently audited**.

## Scope and literature status

These results live in a **patched Stone-sheaf \(\infty\)-topos**, not automatically in ordinary localic shape. The upper/lower witness maps are related **after Patch**; direct unpatched descent for the lower map fails in known coherent examples. The full literature/priority review is **not complete**. In particular the finite-poset abstract homotopy criterion was already proved in Cameron–Kumar's arXiv:2610.14783v1, **§9**; the particular canonical arrow-level strengthening remains a project-specific deduction without a novelty assertion.
