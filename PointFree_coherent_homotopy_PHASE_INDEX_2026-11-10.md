## Scope and literature status

These results live in a **patched Stone-sheaf \(\infty\)-topos**, not automatically in ordinary localic shape. The upper/lower witness maps are related **after Patch**; direct unpatched descent for the lower map fails in known coherent examples. The full literature/priority review is **not complete**. In particular the finite-poset abstract homotopy criterion was already proved in Cameron–Kumar's arXiv:2610.14783v1, **§9**; the particular canonical arrow-level strengthening remains a project-specific deduction without a novelty assertion.
