## Reading guide

The primary relations in `Q=O(X)⊕O(X)` are `I=clΔ`, `D=fitΔ`, `U=C(d_U)`, and `F=⋂_f ker(f)`; derived `V=fit I`, `W=cl D`, `M=I∧D`, `J=I∨D`, `H` the least simultaneously closed and fitted hull of Δ, and `T(S)=cl(S)∧fit(S)`. The notation `D` (fitted diagonal) has nothing to do with `F`-separatedness. `V_n` is the fitted approximation generated by rank≤n rectangular bars. All localic composition is in diagrammatic order; parentheses are mandatory in general.

**Statuses:** `Published` means external background theorem; `E` means a specifically scoped external mathematical check; `E*` means a referee-origin strengthening subsequently checked internally but not separately blind-refereed as an author claim; `I` means an internally supported working proof; `W` means a specific proof gap or unintegrated repair. A slash indicates distinct sources or parts with distinct provenance. “Referee-origin” marks a proof supplied by a referee, not independent confirmation of a finished author's proof. **Open mathematical problems are exclusively in the companion register.** Status does not assess novelty.

**Core dependency spine:** frame coproduct and sublocale calculus → named relations and hulls → (a) coherent spectral compact-open filters/patch → normal–conormal classifications and `H`; (b) C-ideal separation trees → finite-rank and `V_fin` results; (c) localic images/base change → mixed composition and associativity; (d) proper-quotient theory → functional-collapse consequences. An external audit of (a) does not certify later localic-image work in (c).

## Global consistency findings and corrections to apply

1. **103 distinct v0.7 labels; 11 repeated coherent proof rows.** The `C1p–C8l` repetition in §VIII is a proof audit, not a second theorem list. Use this ledger as the single list and retain v0.7 as an annotated work history.
2. **Corrected historical status.** Vermeulen 1994 Cor. 5.17 is unrestricted for *closed proper internal equivalence relations*, not confined to compact locales. It cannot be invoked for an arbitrary reflexive symmetric nontransitive relation.
3. **Properness is not just idempotence of `r_E`.** An internal equivalence relation need not give idempotent `r_E`; it does under properness. The paired-point example is canonical. The continuous-frame Scott converse was proved internally on 20 Oct and independently verified on 8 Nov; the referee strengthened the topological theorem to arbitrary X,Y. The FIRST referee then supplied an explicit point-free proof for **all locales** using a proper generic compact-fitted family; a distinct SECOND blind referee subsequently checked the theorem (status `E`), finding a false principal C-ideal assertion but giving a valid local repair.
4. **No hidden functional collapse.** `I=U` alone fails to imply `U=F` on continuous frames; the later subfit-and-arithmetic counterexample is *working/internal*, so this combination must no longer be listed as conjecturally sufficient, but still needs external proof review.
5. **Coherence cannot be silently removed.** `W=U` holds in coherent locales and may fail compactly outside coherence; `V=fit I` is the true formal partner of `W=cl D`, not automatically the natural dual of `U`.
6. **Separate rank phenomena.** High individual separation-tree rank, strict finite fitted stages `V_n>V_(n+1)`, and strict `V<V_fin` are three distinct assertions. The negative finite exhaustion result is independently checked. The role of `V₂` outside this calculus is a deferred idea.
7. **Post-V lower-diamond repair.** In the compact three-boundary-point example `J=T(J)` even though `T(J)<U∧V`. Do not assert `J<T(J)` for this model.
8. **Categorical composition is not universally associative.** The mixed negative theorem and Arrieta nontransitive fitted diagonal survive external checks. Associativity of Arrieta's fitted-diagonal cube is **open**; its proof cannot be inferred from nontransitivity or from Boolean permutations having relation diameter two.
9. **Later vs earlier external audits.** Full localic specialization `I=A∘B`, broad proconstructible image stability, combined subfit/arithmetic model, strengthened two-generic-point `V_fin` witness, all-relation naturality, and the post-audit noncoherent transitive `H` example must stay `I`, even though neighboring ingredients are `E`.
10. **Historical aliases.** In old dual-far-strip research notes an auxiliary strip-generated approximation was called `W`. Its use is obsolete; **`W` now means only `cl(D)`**. `F` is the real-functional relation, never the fitted-diagonal separation axiom (Fsep).
11. **Priority remains unassessed.** The proof-source ledger is not a novelty survey, and several results are known consequences of classic locale theory.
