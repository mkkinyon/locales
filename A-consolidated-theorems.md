# Project A — Consolidated theorem outline (working manuscript skeleton)

**Consolidated 18 October 2026.** This is a working theorem map, **not an independent referee verification**, and it makes **no claim of novelty**. For proof details follow links rather than relying on this summary. The current status of the continuous-frame implication is **NEGATIVE in general**, despite historical "open" wording in earlier investigation notes.

**Scope:** classical ZFC unless stated otherwise. **Terminology:** weak local compactness means each point has *some* compact neighborhood; strong local compactness means *every* open neighborhood of every point contains a compact neighborhood. Our kω/paracompact point-set proofs require only the weak version; the continuous-frame and spatial-product lifts require the strong version together with sobriety. In all point-set-to-locale arguments, require the locally compact sober/spectral representation and agreement of the localic product with the ordinary product. Closed sublocales of spatial frames of opens are determined by their underlying closed subsets, but this must **not** be extended to arbitrary sublocales.

## I. Definitions and categorical backbone (arbitrary locales)

For a locale X, L=O(X), Q=L⊗L=O(X×X), basic rectangles a⊠b, and frame pseudocomplement a*=a→0, define:

    d_I= ⋁_{a∧b=0} a⊠b;
    d_U= ⋁_{a*∨b*=1} a⊠b;
    d_F= ⋁_{f:X→R} (f×f)^*( (R×R)\Δ_R ).

Write I=C(d_I), U=C(d_U), F=C(d_F), where C(d) is the closed sublocale complementary to the open d. For a localic real-valued map f, its kernel pair is K_f.

**Theorem A1 (working, arbitrary locales).**

    Δ_X ⊆ I(X) ⊆ U(X) ⊆ F(X)=⋂_f K_f.

I is the closure of the diagonal; F is a localic equivalence relation. For any locale map h:X→Y and R∈{I,U,F},

    R(X) ⊆ (h×h)^(-1) R(Y).

**Theorem A2 (working, arbitrary locales).** Let a≺b mean a*∨b=1, and a≺≺b the Picado–Pultr completely-below relation. Then

    d_F = ⋁_{a≺≺b*} a⊠b.

Let C(L)⊆L be the subframe generated by images of all real-map frame homomorphisms O(R)→L. It is the largest completely regular subframe. The universal map r_X:X→Loc(C(L)) to the completely regular reflection satisfies

    F(X)=X×_{Loc(C(L))}X = kerpair(r_X),

and F=Δ iff C(L)→L is epi in Frm. A frame epi need not be surjective.

**Theorem A2½ (all locales, working):** For the relational composite in Loc, **I∘I⊆U**. On the pullback P=I₁₂∩I₂₃ in X³, every far pair a*∨b*=1 yields a₁∧b₃=a₁∧((a*)₂∨(b*)₂)∧b₃=0 because a₁∧(a*)₂=0=(b*)₂∧b₃. Therefore **I=U makes I an internal equivalence relation in every locale**, without continuity or spatiality. Full proof: [A-proper-inseparability-relations.md](A-proper-inseparability-relations.md), §0.

**Theorem A3 (working, arbitrary locales).** The equations I=U and U=F are equivalent to rectangular generator-saturation conditions, or to implications for all pairs of parallel frame maps h,k:L→M (see [A-equality-characterizations.md](A-equality-characterizations.md), §2–3). These are exact but partly restate the definitions.

**Baseline collapse:** regular frames satisfy I=U=Δ; normal frames satisfy U=F; completely regular frames satisfy I=U=F=Δ. Extremally disconnected frames satisfy I=U=F, not necessarily Δ. **Precise priority:** the normal-frame implication is the formal consequence of Dowker–Papert's (1967) point-free Urysohn lemma, and of the established identity ≺=≺≺ in normal frames (Gutiérrez García–Picado, *On the Parallel Between Normality and Extremal Disconnectedness*, Remark 2.2). Details and original sources in [A-literature-priority-review.md](A-literature-priority-review.md), §3a. See [A-hierarchy-first-results.md](A-hierarchy-first-results.md), [A-equality-characterizations.md](A-equality-characterizations.md).

**Literature/terminology caution:** Isbell strong Hausdorff (sH) means I=Δ, which is not simply the conservative Hausdorff condition. The label F here means functional equivalence and is **not** the established locale property (Fsep) of fitted diagonal in Arrieta–Picado–Pultr.

## II. Examples and strictness (arbitrary locales)

**Theorem B1 (working).** The seven-point upper-set Alexandrov zigzag

    a1<b1>a2<b2>a3<b3>a4

satisfies I⊊U⊊F; for the pair (a1,a3), U but not I, and for (a1,a4), F but not U.

**Theorem B2 (working).** If Y is any nontrivial pointless locale, then P×Y is pointless and still satisfies I⊊U⊊F. The proof tests the closed sublocales via generalized points s_x:Y→P×Y. A concrete Y has frame RO(R), a complete atomless Boolean algebra. This tests *sublocale* strictness rather than just ordinary points.

**Finite Alexandrov classification (working):** if G is the reflexive inseparability graph, then U=G² and F is the equivalence closure of G. I=U iff each G-component is a clique; U=F iff each has diameter at most two.

Details: [A-hierarchy-first-results.md](A-hierarchy-first-results.md), [A-equality-characterizations.md](A-equality-characterizations.md).

## III. Algebraic/coherent frames

Let K(L) denote order-compact elements of a frame; algebraic means compactly generated, coherent additionally requires 1 compact and K(L) closed under finite meets.

**Theorem C1 (working, algebraic frames).**

    I=U  ⇔  (k,l∈K(L), k∧l=0 ⇒ k*∨l*=1).

The nontrivial direction uses compactness of a rectangle k⊠l and a **finite far-rectangle lemma**. Its locale-general proof warrants close scrutiny in the audit.

**Theorem C2 (working, coherent frames).**

    I=U ⇔ K(L) is a conormal bounded distributive lattice.

Under the standard prime-ideal representation, conormality ⇔ each prime ideal of K(L) contains a unique minimal prime ideal. For a commutative unital ring R,

    I(Spec R)=U(Spec R) ⇔ each prime ideal of R contains a unique minimal prime ideal

(the established mp-ring property; the algebraic terminology/results are **not novel**).

**Theorem C3 (working, coherent frames).**

    U=F ⇔ for all compact k,l,
             k*∨l*=1 ⇒ k≺≺l*.

This uses coherent spectral spatiality and finite real-function gluing. Not asserted for arbitrary nonspatial frames.

**Corollary C4 (working).** Every coherent frame with I=U has I=U=F. This follows from the **compact-space quotient theorem**, not from the conormality calculations alone.

Details: [A-coherent-conormality.md](A-coherent-conormality.md), [A-coherent-implication.md](A-coherent-implication.md).

## IV. Continuous frames

Under classical choice principles, continuous frames have **strongly locally compact** sober spectra. **They are not a class of pointless locales in this setting.** For opens u≪a (way-below), relative compactness gives compact saturated K with u⊆K⊆a, and similarly v≪b.

**Theorem D1 (working).** For continuous L,

    I=U ⇔ [a∧b=0, u≪a, v≪b ⇒ u*∨v*=1];
    U=F ⇔ [a*∨b*=1, u≪a, v≪b ⇒ u≺≺v*].

Proof reduces covers of a×b to finite subcovers of u×v. The U=F direction uses finite function gluing in Euclidean cube. These are *exact criteria* requiring no global compactness.

**Theorem D2 (compact *locale* strengthening; working formal consequence, classical).** If X is **any compact locale**, including a pointless one, and I=clΔ is an **internal equivalence relation**, then I=U=F as closed sublocales. Indeed the projections I⇉X are proper (closed inclusion followed by compact-factor projection); Vermeulen 1994, Prop. 5.5 and Cor. 5.17, produce a proper effective quotient q with closed diagonal. The quotient is compact strongly Hausdorff, hence completely regular by the classical compact Hausdorff locale theorem (precise primary citation pending); thus I=kerpair(q)=F. The old compact-space statement is a special case. I=U suffices for transitivity point-freely.

**Theorem D3 (working, kω spaces).** For a kω-space, if E=cl(Δ) is transitive, then X/E is normal Hausdorff and E=F. Proof: q(K_n) are closed compact Hausdorff stages; the quotient carries their final topology; extend separating continuous functions compatibly by Tietze. In particular the point-set theorem covers **weakly** locally compact σ-compact spaces, while the **localic** corollary for continuous frames requires **strong** local compactness and sobriety.

**Theorem D4 (working).** A **weakly** locally compact paracompact space, with *paracompact defined by open locally finite refinements without assuming Hausdorffness*, decomposes into a clopen disjoint union of σ-compact open subspaces; thus transitive E implies E=F. A locally countable open cover by σ-compact subspaces also suffices. The point-set equalities lift to closed locale relations when X is **strongly locally compact and sober**.

**Theorem D5 (WORKING COUNTEREXAMPLE; central, substantially sharpened 19 October 2026).** The unrestricted implication

    continuous L and I=U  ⇒  U=F

is **FALSE**. More strongly, there exists a locally compact sober X with I=U⊊F and compact opens u={p_A}, v={p_B} satisfying

    u*∨v*=1, but u not≺≺v*.

Construction: for a locally compact Hausdorff nonnormal T with disjoint closed A,B without disjoint open neighborhoods, adjoin two *open singleton tags* p_A,p_B; require every open meeting A to contain p_A and every open meeting B to contain p_B. Then X is locally compact sober, I=U pointwise has classes A∪{p_A}, B∪{p_B}, and the other singletons; p_A and p_B have disjoint-closure singleton neighborhoods, yet no continuous real map distinguishes them. For explicit T use the deleted Tychonoff plank

    T=([0,ω1]×[0,ω])\{(ω1,ω)},
    A={ω1}×[0,ω),   B=[0,ω1)×{ω}.

Since X is locally compact sober, pointwise equality/strictness of these **closed** relations lifts to the localic product. This counters *the exact stable-buffer conjecture* for way-below compact opens.

**Theorem D6 (NEW WORKING STRENGTHENING, 19 October 2026).** There exists a **T1, strongly locally compact, sober** topological space X, whose frame of opens L is both **algebraic and subfit**, satisfying

    I(L)=U(L) ⊊ F(L).

It has compact opens u,v with u≪u, v≪v, u*∨v*=1, and **no c** such that u≺c≺v*. Replace each old generic tag by a countably infinite discrete cloud; neighborhoods meeting A (respectively B) must contain a cofinite tail of that cloud. The points of A become mutually inseparable, as do the points of B, while X becomes T1. Each point of A has a compact neighborhood K∪tail inside any given open (K compact in T, the tail together with a∈A compact); added points are clopen, giving sobriety. The pointwise relations agree I=U, but all real maps are constant on A and B, so nonnormality of the deleted plank forces F to merge these classes. Since X is strongly locally compact sober, the equality and strictness lift to closed localic relations. The plank has a compact-open basis, so X does too. **Theorem D6 and its compact-open witnesses were checked in two external adversarial reviews on 20 October 2026; priority remains unexamined.** **Crowded stabilization:** taking the product with the Cantor set gives a further **T1, algebraic, subfit, strongly locally compact sober counterexample with no isolated points**. For any topological X and completely regular Hausdorff Y, each pointwise R∈{I,U,F} satisfies `((x,y),(x',y'))∈R(X×Y) ⇔ (x,x')∈R(X) and y=y'`; the Cantor factor preserves compact-open witnesses and localic equality/strictness. Proof in the same note, §5¾. Complete proof: [A-subfit-continuous-counterexample.md](A-subfit-continuous-counterexample.md). It subsumes D5 for strength; D5 is retained as a simpler generic-tag predecessor.

Details: [A-continuous-frames.md](A-continuous-frames.md), [A-kw-compact-exhaustion.md](A-kw-compact-exhaustion.md), [A-beyond-countable-exhaustion.md](A-beyond-countable-exhaustion.md), [A-continuous-counterexample-plank.md](A-continuous-counterexample-plank.md).

**Closed compact-fiber quotient criterion (topological).** If a strongly locally compact sober X has transitive I and its ordinary quotient q:X→X/I is **closed with compact classes**, then the quotient is locally compact Hausdorff and I=U=F as closed localic relations. On *arbitrary* topological spaces, a closed equivalence relation with perfect projections has a Hausdorff quotient. See [A-obstruction-candidates.md](A-obstruction-candidates.md) and [A-proper-inseparability-relations.md](A-proper-inseparability-relations.md).

**Proper localic relation theorem (externally audited, conditional on Vermeulen and classical Hofmann–Lawson).** For a **continuous frame** L, if I is an internal equivalence relation and its projections I⇉X are **proper locale maps**, then I=U=F and X/I is the completely regular reflection. Vermeulen 1994, **Proposition 5.5** gives a proper quotient and **Corollary 5.17** gives effectiveness for *all* closed proper equivalence relations, regardless of compactness or spatiality. Properness here means Scott continuity of the right adjoint together with closed Frobenius; it is not just “proper subset.” The quotient has closed diagonal and continuous frame, hence is completely regular in classical mathematics. Independently, for **every locale**, I∘I⊆U, so I=U makes I an internal equivalence relation. See [A-vermeulen-effectiveness-correction.md](A-vermeulen-effectiveness-correction.md).

**Intrinsic saturation operator and correction.** For I=C(d_I), `r_I(u)=(p₂)_*p₁^*(u)=∨{v:1⊠v≤(u⊠1)∨d_I}` for arbitrary locales. Its fixed points are the invariant opens, but **idempotence and Scott continuity do not follow from transitivity or even I=U=F**. A **countable algebraic sober example with finite I-classes and compact Hausdorff quotient** has r_I²≠r_I; contrast the deleted-plank cofinite-cloud example, where q is closed but has noncompact fibers and Scott continuity fails. Properness of the relation projections suffices for idempotence and Scott continuity; neither is equivalent to I=F. See [A-saturation-operator-nonidempotence.md](A-saturation-operator-nonidempotence.md).

**SCOTT-CONTINUITY CONVERSE (new working theorem, 20 October 2026; direct proof, not yet externally audited).** If X is any **weakly locally compact ordinary space**, Y any topological space, and E⊆X×Y a **closed relation**, then the operator `r_E=(p_Y)_*p_X^*:O(X)→O(Y)` preserves directed joins **iff** the projection `p_Y:E→Y` is a **perfect topological map** (closed with compact fibers). Neither symmetry nor equivalence axioms are needed. The key proof uses a cover `V_i⊆K_i` with compact K_i: Scott continuity yields an open cover of Y on each member of which E is contained in a **finite compact support** K_F×W, making the projection closed by the tube lemma and its fibers compact. Conversely a closed compact-fiber map has Scott-continuous right adjoint. For **continuous frames**, classical spatiality and product comparison make this an exact equivalence `r_E Scott ⇔ p₂ proper as a locale map` for **any closed localic E↪X×X**; with symmetric I this characterizes properness of **both** projections. In particular, if I is transitive, Scott continuity implies **I=U=F**. This does **not** assert that I=F implies Scott continuity, nor that the converse holds for arbitrary noncontinuous pointless locales. Full proof: [A-scott-continuity-converse.md](A-scott-continuity-converse.md).

**Arithmetic counterexample.** The original *two-tag* deleted-plank example has an **arithmetic** frame (algebraic with finite-meet-stable compact opens). It has I=U⊊F, proving stable continuity alone insufficient. The combined condition **subfit + arithmetic** remains untested. See [A-continuous-counterexample-plank.md](A-continuous-counterexample-plank.md).

**NEW COUNTEREXAMPLE (20 October 2026, working, not externally audited): subfitness + arithmeticity ALSO fail together.** Replace the **discrete** clouds in the deleted-plank T1 precursor by infinite **cofinite-topology** clouds. Its opens have compact cloud portions, so compact opens form a finite-meet-stable basis, while its open frame is subfit because the precursor is T1. The precursor is not sober: the closed irreducible sets A∪S_A and B∪S_B acquire generic points in its sobrification. The frame is unchanged, and those new generic points stay inside their respective inseparability classes. On the strongly locally compact sober spectrum, **I=U⊊F as closed localic relations**, with the same compact-open witnesses failing one-step rather-below interpolation. Thus **even an algebraic, arithmetic, subfit frame with I=U need not have U=F**. Contrasting positive fact: a **sober T1** arithmetic space must be **zero-dimensional** (each compact open subspace is T1 spectral, hence Stone; arithmeticity makes its intersections with other compact opens closed), so its I=U=F=Δ. The counterexample's sober spectrum necessarily fails T1 even though its frame is subfit. Detailed proof: [A-subfit-arithmetic-counterexample.md](A-subfit-arithmetic-counterexample.md). No novelty/priority claim.

## V. Supporting obstructions, not alternative main theorems

- Openness of the inseparability quotient is sufficient in the strongly locally compact setting; closedness is sufficient only with additional global hypotheses such as σ-compactness. Neither is necessary for I=U=F. **Closedness alone is insufficient:** the deleted-plank two-tag example has a *closed* inseparability quotient map yet I=U⊊F (the saturation of any closed set adds at most two closed equivalence classes).
- If E is a **closed relation** and K compact, then E[K] is closed. For the stronger buffer statement, require **E=I=clΔ to be an equivalence relation**, and require a compact K with u⊆K⊆a (obtained from u≪a under **strong local compactness**, not for arbitrary spaces). Then cl(E[cl(u)])⊆E[K]⊆cl(a), possibly noncompact.
- The relation uρw iff ∃a (u≪a≺w) need not interpolate, even if I=U=F. Arbitrary far opens need not admit any interpolant; the specific way-below far pairs matter.
- The failure of stable interpolation in the deleted-plank example is **global nonnormality**: opening the closed saturated buffers would give disjoint neighborhoods of A,B in T.

## V½. Additional fitted-diagonal relation (recorded; analysis deferred)

The 2022 paper of Arrieta–Picado–Pultr studies (Fsep): the diagonal is a **fitted sublocale**. Define D(X)=fit(Δ_X), the intersection of all open sublocales containing Δ. Unlike I,U,F, D is **not generally closed**.

For every locale, **Δ⊆D⊆U**. In general D and I are **incomparable**; opposite three-point upper-set Alexandrov V-posets witness this. For **ordinary points** x,y of an ordinary space (even if non-sober), (x,y) lies in D iff **every open cover contains a member containing both x and y**, equivalently **cl{x}∩cl{y}≠∅**. Thus its point relation is precisely the **closure-meeting graph Cm** of Cameron–Kumar (2026); the relation on **original points** is diagonal **iff X is T1**. To identify all **locale points** with original points, assume X **sober**; non-sober spaces have additional points after sobrification. The pointwise diamond was already established by Cameron–Kumar; we are recording its realization by the fitting of Δ. This last statement must not be confused with the stronger localic condition (Fsep) that D=Δ as sublocales. Formal proofs: [A-fitted-diagonal.md](A-fitted-diagonal.md). **Source precedent:** Arrieta (2024), *Localic Separation and the Duality Between Closedness and Fittedness*, defines **anti-Urysohn locales** by the condition a*∨b*≠1 for all nonzero a,b; equivalently, our U is the total relation X×X. This is a known extreme case, not a prior definition of the full U sublocale. Further equality combinations are intentionally deferred.
