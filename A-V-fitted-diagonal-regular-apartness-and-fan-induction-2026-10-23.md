# Fitted diagonal from regular-apartness and fan induction
23 October 2026

Definitions: L frame, Q=L⊕L, d=join of disjoint rectangles a box b. I=C(d) has closed nucleus j_I(x)=d join x. Write c prec a if c* join a=1. Let d_sep be the join of all c box f for which c prec a, f prec b, a meet b=0.

THEOREM A (stronger than V2=V). If d_sep=d then
  V_2 = V = I
as sublocales, and their common nucleus is j_I(x)=d join x.

Proof. For each generator c box f of d_sep, the strip v=(c* box 1) join (1 box f*) has an explicit H-V depth2 bar tree and v meet(c box f)=0. Hence c box f<=o_v(0)=v*. Let j_2 be the join of open nuclei of all rank<=2 bars. Then j_2(0)>=d_sep=d. Since every rank2 bar v is an open neighborhood of I, j_2<=j_I; thus j_2(0)<=j_I(0)=d. Hence j_2(0)=d. By inflationarity and monotonicity, j_2(x)>=x join d for any x, while j_2<=j_I gives the reverse inequality. Therefore j_2=j_I. Since I<=V<=V_2 as sublocales, equality of all three follows.

THEOREM B (clopen localization of d_sep=d). Let {c_i} be a family of complemented elements of L, with join e. Assume each open sublocale ↓c_i satisfies its own d_sep=d. Assume the closed remainder Z=C(e) is irreducible (its open frame is hyperconnected, so disjoint nonzero opens do not exist). Then L also satisfies d_sep=d. The case e=1 needs no assumption on Z.

Proof. If c is complemented with complement c', the global disjointness open d restricted to c box c equals local disjointness for ↓c. The local rather-below relation lifts to the global one: for u,a<=c, if (c meet u*) join a=c, then u* join a=1 because u* contains c'. Thus if local d_sep=d on c, global d_sep contains (c box c) meet d. It also contains c box c' and c' box c, since c prec c, c' prec c', and c meet c'=0. Consequently d_sep contains (c box 1) meet d and (1 box c) meet d. Joining patches gives
   d meet ((e box 1) join (1 box e)) <= d_sep.
Irreducibility of the closed remainder implies d <=(e box1) join(1 box e): for disjoint a,b in L, their restrictions to Z are disjoint, so one is zero; therefore a<=e or b<=e, and a box b is in one strip. Combining inequalities gives d<=d_sep. The reverse inequality holds by definition.

COROLLARY C (all iterated K-fans have fitted diagonal). Base X1=Q with rational K-topology K={1/m} and special point 0. For every x!=0, choose a clopen rational interval with irrational endpoints disjoint from a neighborhood of 0; it has the usual regular rational topology. These regular clopen patches cover X1 minus {0}. Every regular frame has d_sep=d because each disjoint pair a,b is a join of c prec a and f prec b. The closed remainder singleton {0} is irreducible. By Theorem B, d_sep(X1)=d(X1).

Inductively, X_(m+1) adds one point t to countably many CLOPEN copies of X_m, and their union is X_(m+1) minus {t}; closed singleton {t} is irreducible. Therefore d_sep(X_m)=d(X_m) for every m. The disjoint union X_infty=disjoint-union_m X_m is covered by complemented components satisfying d_sep=d, so the equality also holds on X_infty.

By Theorem A:
  I(X_m)=V(X_m)=V_2(X_m) for every m;
  I(X_infty)=V(X_infty)=V_2(X_infty).

This is a major strengthening of previous fixed-rank fitted results: these locales have a FITTED CLOSED DIAGONAL despite admitting symmetric canonical bars of arbitrarily high exact finite separation-tree rank, and X_infty carries a symmetric two-rectangle bar of exact well-founded tree rank omega. Unbounded separation-tree rank is therefore compatible not just with V2=V but with V=I.

OTHER EXAMPLES. Arbitrary disjoint unions of hyperconnected locales satisfy d_sep=d, because disjointness lives entirely on cross-component clopen blocks. In particular two uncountable cocountable spaces produce a nonregular, noncontinuous, noncompact frame with no nonzero compact opens yet a fitted diagonal generated by rank2 neighborhoods.

CONTRAST. On the original rational bridge locale, g*(d_sep)=0 but g*(d)=1 for graph g(t)=(ell_t,r_t): if c box f met graph over a nonempty rational interval, a bridge attached to both sheets inside that interval would lie in closures of c,f, contradicting their rather-below containment in disjoint opens. Thus the regular-apartness hypothesis fails there, consistent with strict V2>V3. This does not by itself decide whether I is fitted on the rational-bridge space.

OPEN. General Question A (V_fin=V for every frame) remains unsettled; the fan mechanism cannot furnish a counterexample because its diagonal is already fitted at rank2.

## Explicit dual-far strip formula for the fitted diagonal

Assume d_sep=d. For each c prec a, f prec b with a meet b=0, let v_cf=(c* box 1) join (1 box f*), a bar of rank<=2. Let j_T be the join of just the open nuclei o_vcf. Since v_cf meet(c box f)=0, the bottom value j_T(0) dominates every c box f, hence d_sep=d. Since every v_cf is a neighborhood of I, j_T<=j_I. As in Theorem A, this forces j_T=j_I everywhere. Therefore the fitted closed diagonal has the explicit expression

  I=V= meet_{c prec a, f prec b, a meet b=0} O(v_cf).

Thus even the restricted family of specially constructed RANK-TWO DUAL-FAR STRIPS generates I and V. The more general strip-fitted relation W from the earlier failed unrestricted dual-far formula also satisfies W=V=I on all frames with d_sep=d. In particular, this exact intrinsic strip formula holds on ALL iterated K-fans and on their disjoint-union rank-omega example, despite the existence of two-rectangle bars of unbounded height.

This does not restore the general identity W=V for arbitrary frames: the rational-bridge counterexample has g*d_sep=0<g*d and a genuine strict W>V by its separate graph-density argument. It also does not prove the general finite-neighborhood Question A outside this class.

## Necessary support of any hypothetical counterexample to Question A

Let r<1 be a proper bar fixed by all finite-rank open nuclei, as in the fixed-point criterion for V_fin!=V. In particular r=v->r for every rank-two strip v_cf. Since v_cf meet(c box f)=0, each c box f is <=v_cf->r=r. Hence d_sep<=r. Also d join r=1 because r is a bar. Therefore its nonzero closed residual R=C(r) is contained simultaneously in O(d) and C(d_sep):

  0 != R <= O(d) meet C(d_sep).

Thus a NEGATIVE answer to Question A is possible only if the locally closed 'unregularized apartness defect' O(d)∧C(d_sep) is nonzero, and it must carry a closed sublocale on which every finite-rank bar is dense. In particular d_sep<d is necessary. On all iterated K-fans d_sep=d, so this defect is zero. On the rational-bridge graph g*d_sep=0 but g*d=1, so its graph image lives within the defect; its rank-three null bar removes that obstruction, showing the defect need not survive finite-rank nuclei.

This isolates the intrinsic point-free region where an attempted hereditary graph-density construction must live. It is stronger than requiring a bar of infinite separation-tree rank; the latter may occur even when the defect is zero and I is already fitted.
