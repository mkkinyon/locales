## 1. General symmetry reduction

Use diagrammatic composition \(R\circ S\) meaning R then S, always with parentheses. The converse of a binary localic composite reverses factors, even when composition is nonassociative:
\[
(R\circ S)^\smile=S^\smile\circ R^\smile.
\]
Put \(Q=A\circ A,\ L=Q\circ A,\ R=A\circ Q\). Since \(A=A^\smile\), \(Q=Q^\smile\) and **\(L^\smile=R\)**. Therefore
\[
\boxed{(A^2)\circ A=A\circ(A^2)\iff (A^2)\circ A\ \text{is symmetric}.}
\tag{1.1}
\]
On the frame tensor \(Q_2=\mathcal O(V)\oplus\mathcal O(V)\), write \(\tau\) for coordinate swap; if \(K_L,K_R\) denote endpoint frame congruences of L and R, then \(K_R=\tau(K_L)\). Thus a separator may be exhibited by a pair (u,v) of elements of \(Q_2\) with u≡v mod \(K_L\), but \(\tau(u)\not\equiv\tau(v)\) mod \(K_L\), or vice versa. It is NOT enough to prove nontransitivity \(A^2\nsubseteq A\).

## 2. The genuine four-step image

Write
\[
E_2=A_{12}\cap A_{23}\subseteq V^3,\quad e_2:E_2\twoheadrightarrow Q=A^2,
\]
where \(e_2\) is the canonical locale-surjection in the image factorization of the endpoint projection. Put
\[
E_4=A_{12}\cap A_{23}\cap A_{34}\subseteq V^4,\qquad
P_4=\mathrm{im}(E_4\to V^2).
\]
The left cubic source is \(S_L=Q_{12}\cap A_{23}\subseteq V^3\), with coordinates \((x_1,x_3,x_4)\); the right source \(S_R=A_{12}\cap Q_{23}\), coordinates \((x_1,x_2,x_4)\). The maps
\[
\ell:E_4\to S_L,\qquad r:E_4\to S_R
\]
are respectively **pullbacks of** \(e_2:E_2\to Q\) along \(S_L\to Q\) and along the reversed comparison \(S_R\to Q\). Therefore \(P_4\le L,R\).

Reversal of four coordinates is an isomorphism E_4→E_4 because A symmetric; it identifies \(\ell\) with r after swapping the labels. Consequently \(\ell\) is a locale-surjection iff r is. If this one particular base change of e₂ is surjective, both are and
\[
\boxed{L=R=P_4.}\tag{2.1}
\]
This **single-base-change criterion is sufficient but not necessary** for associativity. If \(\ell\) is not onto, the two bracketings could still agree above P₄.

There is a weaker single-sided sufficient criterion: if the **endpoint image** of \(\ell\) agrees with the endpoint image of \(S_L\), i.e. \(L=P_4\), symmetry implies \(R=P_4\). This “endpoint equation conservativity” is strictly weaker than requiring \(\ell\) surjective in general; its validity is not yet established for Arrieta A.

## 3. Exact four-variable congruence formulation

Set \(F=\mathcal O(V)\), \(Q_n=F^{\oplus n}\). Let \(G_{ij}\) be the frame congruence of \(A_{ij}\), generated by \(\gamma_{\mathcal C}^{ij}=1\) for every Θ-admissible cover \(\mathcal C\) of V. The full path E₄ has
\[
\mathcal O(E_4)=Q_4/(G_{12}\vee G_{23}\vee G_{34}).
\tag{3.1}
\]
The two-step congruence is
\[
K_2=\ker\left(Q_2\to Q_3/(G_{12}\vee G_{23})\right)
\tag{3.2}
\]
with endpoint coordinates (1,3).

On the three-variable frame for \(S_L\), let
\[
H_L=K_2^{12}\vee G_{23},\qquad \mathcal O(S_L)=Q_3/H_L.
\]
Let \(\alpha_{134}:Q_3\to Q_4\) insert the first, second and third variables in full-path positions 1,3,4, respectively. Put
\[
J_L=\ker\big(Q_3\xrightarrow{\alpha_{134}}Q_4/(G_{12}\vee G_{23}\vee G_{34})\big).
\tag{3.3}
\]
By construction \(H_L\subseteq J_L\), and \(\ell\) is a locale-surjection **iff**
\[
\boxed{H_L=J_L.}\tag{3.4}
\]
Here inclusion/equality are for frame congruences. To test merely the **endpoint equation** condition \(L=P_4\), restrict H_L and J_L to the outer two coordinates of the three-variable presentation, or equivalently compare their pullbacks along the corresponding \(Q_2\to Q_3\). This yields the *weaker* exact test
\[
\boxed{\alpha_{13}^{-1}(H_L)=\alpha_{13}^{-1}(J_L).}\tag{3.5}
\]
A proof of (3.5) is enough to settle CO-01 positively. A failure of (3.5) only proves L>P₄, **not** L≠R. For a negative CO-01 verdict one needs a swapped endpoint equation under (1.1), equivalently \(K_L\ne\tau(K_L)\).

This is a concrete reduction of one problematic localic pullback to **frame-congruence equalities in Q₃/Q₄**, and distinguishes the strong “all source equations” and weaker “endpoint equations” tests. It does not claim those equalities have been proved.

## 4. Spatial invisibility: a general lemma

**Lemma.** Let X be a sober spatial locale, and let \(S\hookrightarrow X^n\) be any localic subobject which contains the full diagonal \(\Delta_n\), and whose ordinary points all have equal coordinates. Then for every **spatial locale T** and map \(f:T\to S\), the map factors *uniquely* through \(\Delta_n\to S\).

**Proof.** The n coordinate maps \(T\to X\) are equal at every ordinary point of T, because points of S have equal coordinates. Since T is spatial, two locale maps T→X agreeing on ordinary points are equal: inverse images of every X-open agree at all points of T and thus in \(\mathcal O(T)\). Therefore the composite T→X^n factors through the diagonal \(\Delta_n\to X^n\). As S→X^n is monic and \(\Delta_n\subseteq S\), the factorization lifts uniquely through \(\Delta_n\to S\). QED.

From audit VIII, A and every finite localic power Q=A²,L,R have **exactly diagonal ordinary point-pairs**. The three-coordinate cubic sources S_L,S_R likewise have only diagonal ordinary triples (a point of S_L gives a point of Q on the first two and a point of A on the last two). All these relations/sources contain their diagonals because A is reflexive. Consequently:
- Every map from a spatial T into Q,L,R, or either cubic source factors through the corresponding diagonal.
- For **every spatial T→S_L**, the pullback of \(\ell:E_4\to S_L\) to T has an explicit section: factor T through \(\Delta_3\), then lift by \(\Delta_4\subseteq E_4\). Hence \(\ell\) is a **split surjection after every spatial base change**. The same holds for r.
- The previous non-pullback-stability of \(A\to V\) along the discrete refinement \(V_d\to V\) does **not** show failure of either special comparison \(\ell\) or r. Even their base changes along all spatial maps are benign.

**Interpretation:** A failure of (3.4), if one exists, is invisible to spatially parametrized generalized triples, not merely to ordinary points. Similarly, a counterexample to associativity cannot be detected by spatial test locales. This neither proves (3.4) nor rules out failure under a **nonspatial** base change. It explains why ordinary topology, finite discretizations and point-fibre computations cannot settle CO-01.

## 5. Even coordinate-permutation Boolean triples lift

Use the generalized points \(h_\pi=q\circ\widehat\pi_V^*:\mathcal O(V)\to\operatorname{RO}(V)\) from audit VIII, indexed by coordinate permutations \(\pi\in\operatorname{Sym}(\mathbb N)\). That audit proves two claims: (a) for every \(\pi,\kappa\) the pair \(h_\pi,h_\kappa\) lies in A²; (b) there exists \(\sigma\) with \(h_\pi A h_\sigma A h_\kappa\), by infinite agreements and Baire category.

Given an **actual Boolean-valued triple** \(h_\pi,h_\kappa,h_\lambda\) that factors through \(S_L=Q_{12}\cap A_{23}\), it satisfies \(h_\kappa A h_\lambda\). Pick \(\sigma\) as in (b). Then
\[
h_\pi A h_\sigma A h_\kappa A h_\lambda
\]
is an **actual four-step generalized path on the same Boolean test locale**. It lifts the given triple through \(\ell\). The symmetric statement holds for S_R. Thus the coordinate-permutation Boolean family does not witness **even failure of surjectivity of \(\ell\)**, which is stronger than saying only that it cannot separate the endpoint bracketings.

Do not infer liftability of arbitrary generalized maps from the permutation case. All actual maps here have a common fixed nonzero Boolean domain \(\operatorname{Loc}(\mathrm{RO}(V))\).
