## Repairs and attribution

- **I1 — B2 lower inclusion proof:** The packet ambiguously says that, “after barycentric subdivision,” vertices of both comparison maps lie in \(\Phi(\sigma_0)\). This is false if it means the literal subdivided map \(r\). The *original longer working note* [finite-poset crosscut](PointFree_finite_poset_crosscut_chain_witness_comparison_2026-11-10.md) explicitly uses the last-vertex map \(\operatorname{sd}\operatorname{Ord}(P)\to\operatorname{Ord}(P)\), \(\sigma\mapsto\max\sigma\), which **does** make the carrier argument valid. The blind packet omitted this crucial specification. Repair the canonical proof to state the last-vertex map; compare the two maps via common carrier simplices, not careless “contiguity” between different targets. Alternatively omit the separate B2 lower proof after using C3 plus B2 upper (see below).
- **I2 — C3:** Equivalence of the canonical arrows under \(\mathbf C\) directly implies either arrow is an equivalence iff the other is. Equivalent homotopy cofibres are a *further* consequence; contractible cofibre alone cannot certify an equivalence.
- **I3 — site:** Specify a sufficiently large size bound κ and a small site of κ-small Stone locales closed under required finite products/coprod/pullbacks, with finite jointly surjective families **including the empty cover of ∅**. Representables are sheaves and surjections satisfy effective Čech descent; sheafified Yoneda h preserves the required finite coproducts and initial object. Evaluation at * preserves colimits: a covering sieve of * is maximal since each nonempty Stone cover has a point/section. This also implies that the constant-object functor on spaces is fully faithful (evaluation at * is a left inverse), so a constant \(S^1\) is not terminal.
- **I4 — strictness:** G3's \(X_T\) shows SNC does not entail SZC, but **not** strictness of the *finite-cover criteria*: the G2 facet cover passes the old prime-endpoint test. H1 separates D2 from E3; H2 separates E3 from F2. The referee gives a stronger argument that **no** finite closed cover of H1 passes D2 and **no** finite closed cover of H2 passes E3. Independently checked the core combinatorics for H1 by enumerating all 279 downsets of a finite 12-point truncation: possible intersections of a downset containing p without q and a downset containing q without p all contain the core Z, and only Z, Z∪{e}, Z∪{f} admit ambient common upper bounds; none has a least point. The infinite argument follows by the same order obstruction and the absence of a finite zigzag on the dunce-hat face poset.
- **I5 — subdivision reference:** The bare edgewise geometric-realization homeomorphism does not automatically certify the two block maps. Use Brown–Chan–Galatius–Payne Prop. 3.8 and Cor. 3.9, or the direct proof: the ordinal-sum endofunctor ε induces a cofinal \(\epsilon^\mathrm{op}:\Delta^\mathrm{op}\to\Delta^\mathrm{op}\), and the two block inclusions \(\mathrm{id}\Rightarrow\epsilon\) induce equivalent colimit maps; both are equivalences in any ∞-category with geometric realizations. The explicit point-free simplicial homotopy C2 remains correct but is optional for C3.
- **I6:** For finite P, \(I_\bullet(P)\) and \(D_\bullet(P)\) are exactly the ordered-tuple simplicial models of the common-upper and common-lower witness complexes. The canonical chain maps correspond to ordinary inclusions of the order complex after the standard ordered-tuple/ordinary-complex comparison.
- **I7, I9, I10:** In image arguments, factorization through the proconstructible point image also uses spatiality of the source, not merely target spatiality. Specify SNC invariance under **spectral order-homotopy equivalences**, not arbitrary vaguely defined homotopy equivalences. Replace inappropriate “contiguous” with “carried by a common target simplex” where the maps are presented through different subdivisions.
- **I8:** Reduce overloaded names C, H, T, K, A, F; separate “closed sublocale,” “chain realization,” “homotopy topos,” “ambient upper hull,” “lattice coproduct,” and “finite simplicial complex.” Choose one phrase for upper-witness terminology.
- **I11 — provenance:** Cameron–Kumar, *Graphs on topological spaces* arXiv:2610.14783v1 **§9** (not §8), already establish the type-level homotopy equivalence of the witness complexes and the same contractible-intersection hypothesis leading to an abstract equivalence with the order complex. Their notation/order direction is opposite ours. **Do not claim B1 or the homotopy-type portion of B2 as new.** Their formulation does not claim the **canonical inclusions** are equivalences, and the unconditional **strong collapse** from the full lower-witness complex to maximal crosscut is not stated in that paper. ## Referee-origin additions: independent assessment and status

1. **Simpler derivation of B2 lower:** C3 identifies canonical arrows for every finite P. Hence B2-upper alone implies B2-lower. This avoids the Quillen-fibre proof and its carrier issue in the shortest canonical account; retain the repaired independent proof in a historical appendix if useful.
2. **All finite closed covers, without compact complements:** If \(u_i\in\mathcal O(X)\) have \(\bigwedge_i u_i=0\), \(Z_i=C(u_i)\) remain patch-closed downward-closed coherent sublocales of coherent X. The actual patched covers and intersection diagrams of D1/F1 still work. The compactness of each \(u_i\) is used only to identify \(K(Z_S)\) with an explicit interval \([v_S,1]_{K(X)}\). This is a valid generalization; keep distinction between a geometric codescent theorem and the interval-algebra certificates.
3. **UB is exact:** For coherent X and closed nonzero \(Z=C(v)\), UB (finite centeredness of compact opens of X meeting Z) is equivalent to the existence of an ambient common upper specialization point for **all** of Z, which is equivalent to \(H_n^X(Z)=Z^{n+1}\) for all n. Necessity follows already at n=1 (finite subsets via all n) by compact prime-filter extension; no point-set equality is promoted except in the proconstructible class. This is a genuine sharpening of E1.
4. **Strict chain of cover criteria:** D2 ⊊ E3 ⊊ F2 as *existential finite-closed-cover criteria*; H1 and H2 give the strictness. The referee-origin universal-cover argument should be preserved alongside the examples, not attributed retroactively to the original packet.
5. **Compactified infinite fence W — key new obstruction:** Let W be the one-point compactification of the countable zigzag \(x_n<y_n>x_{n+1}\), with the limit ∞ incomparable to the fence. The closed order is Priestley: finite clopen principal upsets separate isolated points, and appropriate clopen upper tails separate ∞. The finite bounded compact-open sublattices consisting of upsets that contain or miss a sufficiently late tail are cofinal; the dual finite posets are finite fences with the tail collapsed to a terminal endpoint t, hence have contractible order complexes. But the underlying abstract poset of points of W has *two* connected components (the infinite fence and isolated ∞), so \(\mathbf C(W)\not\simeq1\). This concretely demonstrates **degreewise finite inverse limits do not imply realization continuity**. The last stronger observation about the sheaf of components having two underlying points but no morphism to a discrete two-point object separating them is plausible: any such morphism would restrict to a continuous locally constant map W→2 invariant under comparability, impossible since the fence accumulates at ∞. **Treat that sheaf-component claim as a separate referee-origin working addendum, not as necessary for the SNC counterexample.** Do not identify the two-point component sheaf with the constant sheaf on two points.

## Mathematical significance and limitations

The coherent finite-complex examples are mathematically correct but the convergent sequence itself does not change the realizations in their construction; it only makes the locale genuinely infinite.
