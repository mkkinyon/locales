# Both orientation-specific separation ranks equal three

For the original rational-bridge graph-null bar B_q, the minimal H-V-H tree depth and minimal V-H-V tree depth both equal 3.

The earlier exact-rank-three construction gives H-V-H. Apply that construction to the transposed graph gamma^tau(t)=(r_t,ell_t), renaming the first rational copy R and second L, and swapping their bridge sequences B_n,A_n and limit coordinates beta_n,alpha_n. All assumptions of the construction (rational-basis cofinal bridges, distinct irrational limit coordinates, and the closed discrete boundary) are symmetric under this interchange. It supplies an H-V-H tree for the transposed bar B_q^tau. Transposing each rectangle and each cover step turns this into a V-H-V tree for B_q. The already established failure of depth two in both orientations gives exact orientation-specific rank three.

The same argument applies to the dense-bridge Cantor-shift bar, replacing shift C by -C under coordinate flip and swapping alpha,beta; the geometric hypotheses are invariant under reversal (with -K in place of K).

Consequence: gluing copies of the original rational-bridge bar with coordinate transposes cannot force rank four via an orientation-conflict argument. This does not imply every rank-three bar admits both orientations, nor any universal depth-three theorem.
