## 3. Concrete inconsistencies and repairs

### E1 — High impact: contradictory status banners obscured the live repository

The root README had **38,862 characters** of chronological announcements **before its first heading**, with additional old notices inside the body; the Start Here, literature, ledger, frontier and proof-atlas files each had another stack. Several still spoke of **MAT*/MUL* pending a technical audit**, **Plewe's complete article inaccessible**, or **Q/Q′ open**, all superseded by later work.

**Repair applied:** Saved the original prefatory notices from six files **verbatim**, plus the older notices inside README, in the [historical status archive](ProjectA_ENDGAME_HISTORICAL_STATUS_NOTICES_ARCHIVE_2026-11-21.md). Each live file now starts with its title and a short **current** route/scope statement. The 9–10 November old Project A/C indexes and older README results are explicitly marked as **historical**, not silently deleted.

No original blind reports or canonical proof files were modified. The archived notices may contain superseded mathematical statements; their preservation is a record of the research chronology, **not current endorsement**.

### E2 — Medium: reader-facing terminology was under-defined

The account invoked \(\operatorname{fit},\mathcal U,\mathcal F\), frame products and \(\mathcal D_2\) before an unfamiliar reader could tell them apart.

**Repair applied:** Added a short locale/frame/sublocale primer; defined closure/fitting, Urysohn and functional relations using the same notation as the [precise assumptions chart](ProjectA_ENDGAME_NOTATION_AND_ASSUMPTIONS_2026-11-21.md); and defined finite-cover vs all-cover diagonal relations at their first example. This adds context without duplicating the full frame proof.

### E3 — Medium: worked constructions and source links lacked small but critical details

The two-ray description omitted its actual alternating order inequalities, and the rational bridge mentioned the countable atlas without writing it down. Link text such as “calculation and audit” sometimes targeted only the calculation.

**Repair applied:** Added the zigzag inequalities, the \(\mathscr A=\{S,T\}\cup\{N_q:q\in\mathbb Q\}\) atlas, the localic interpretation of the patch-closed two-ray squares, and separate calculation/referee references for Examples 1 and 3. The five examples are unchanged; their **standalone reproducibility** is improved.

### E4 — Medium: literature crosswalk lagged the latest primary readings and MAT* result

The Session 10 inventory L01–L16 lacked dedicated Isbell 1981, Johnstone–Sun 1988, and complete-journal Plewe 1996 entries and still sometimes assigned MAT/MUL only compact Hausdorff parameters.

**Repair applied:** Preserved original L01–L16 source labels and added **L17–L19** as explicitly later source reconciliations. Updated L09 and L11 with the separately blind-audited **arbitrary locale P / locally compact scattered K** MAT*/MUL*/FINMAT* theorem and separate post-report additions. Added a short precise primary-source backbone to the adopted mathematical account.
