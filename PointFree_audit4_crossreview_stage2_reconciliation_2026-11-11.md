- A new strengthening, \(\mathbf C(X)\to|h(M_\bullet)|\) is **2-connective** (iso on \(\pi_0,\pi_1\), epi on \(\pi_2\), locally at all basepoints), was also accepted independently by A/B. The proof uses the doubly indexed Čech lower-witness resolution \(E_j\), the j=0 extra degeneracy and 1-connective maps \(\mathbf C(E_j)\to\mathbf I(E_j)\) for all j, then an \(\infty\)-topos realization-connectivity lemma. The latter was proved in the follow-up using skeletal pushouts and iterated codiagonals; semisimplicial cofinality is recorded in [Kerodon §10.1.2](https://kerodon.net/tag/04R8), and the space-level realization lemma in Ebert–Randal-Williams, *Semi-simplicial spaces*, Lemma 2.4. The bound is sharp on the six-point 3-layer 2-antichain example, where the chain realization is \(S^2\) and the meet realization contracts. **Do not misstate 2-connective as 2-equivalent.**

**Referee A/B's added constancy criterion, independently examined by referee C:**
- For a nonempty finite simplicial pair \(K\subseteq T\), a nonempty Stone space S and \(E=\underline{|K|}\amalg_{h(S)\times\underline{|K|}}h(S)\times\underline{|T|}\), if \(K\to T\) is a homotopy epimorphism then the fold \(E\to\underline{|T|}\) is an equivalence for *all* nonempty S. If S is infinite, the converse holds assuming E is merely equivalent to **some** constant object. Thus for infinite S, constancy, fold equivalence, homotopy epimorphism and acyclicity of every homotopy fibre coincide. For finite S every E is constant. Referee C checked the ∞-categorical epi argument, finite mapping-object lemma, finite-image section argument and K-relative two-copy fold. No connectedness restriction is necessary if acyclic fibres are by definition nonempty. Classical epi/acyclic antecedent: Raptis, *Some characterizations of acyclic maps*, Theorem 2.1(a), arXiv:1711.08898. The four small editorial repairs C2–C6 and swapped proof-heading directions should be integrated.

## 2. Corrected functoriality boundary

**Do not assert that arbitrary continuous maps preserving just the binary I and D relations induce a map of reconstructed chain groupoids.** A/B supplies a finite counterexample preserving \(I_1,D_1\) but failing to carry an \(M_2\)-simplex to an \(M_2\)-simplex. What is correct is functoriality for continuous maps carrying \(M_1\) and \(M_2\) into their targets, including monotone maps. Binary *isomorphisms* of coherent patched structures recover all \(I_n,D_n\) using simplicial-vertex neighborhoods, hence do preserve M_2 and induce an isomorphism of chain 1-types and both comparison diagrams. Order and 2-type remain undetermined.
