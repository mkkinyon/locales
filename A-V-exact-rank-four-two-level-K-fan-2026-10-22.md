## Topological space

Let K={1/m:m≥1}⊂Q, and let Y=Q_K be the classical rational K-topology: generated by the ordinary rational intervals and their differences with K. Write p=0. This is a countable, Hausdorff, first-countable space; K is closed, p∉K, the open A=Y\K contains p, but EVERY neighborhood b∋p has closure intersecting K. Proof: some ((-ε,ε)∩Q)\K⊆b, and for each 1/m∈(0,ε), every neighborhood of 1/m contains an ordinary interval that intersects that set.

Take pairwise disjoint copies Y_n of Y, with p_n=0_n and K_n the copy of K. Adjoin one new point ∞. Define X={∞}⊔(⊔_{n≥1}Y_n), each Y_n OPEN with its original topology, and at ∞ a neighborhood base
  U_N={∞} ∪ ⋃_{n≥N}(Y_n\{p_n})   (N≥1).
This is a valid topology: U_N∩U_M=U_max(N,M), and intersections with every Y_n are open. Each Y_n is also CLOSED (its complement contains U_{n+1} and all other copies). Thus X is COUNTABLE, FIRST-COUNTABLE, HAUSDORFF: to separate ∞ from point in Y_n use U_{n+1} and Y_n.

Define
  F={p_n:n≥1},  e=X\F,
  C={∞} ∪ ⋃_{n≥1}K_n,  a=X\C = ⋃_n(Y_n\K_n).
Both e,a are OPEN; F⊆a, C⊆e, and a∨e=1. The closed F is infinite discrete, hence locally compact, noncompact and non-countably-compact. The nonregular point locus of X is N=F∪{∞}, which is itself CLOSED DISCRETE: p_n are nonregular for the local copy of Y, and ∞ is nonregular because e∋∞ while cl U_N contains p_n for all n≥N; every other point is regular because Q_K\{0} is locally Euclidean. Thus the previously proved locally compact nonregularity-locus theorem gives rank≤4 for EVERY rectangular bar on X, and the new bar below attains the bound.

## Two rectangle bars

Let L=O(X), Q=L⊕L, d_I=∨_{u∧v=0}u⊠v. Define
  w=(a⊠1)∨(e⊠e),

Both are proper opens. Let B_w={(u,v):u⊠v≤w} and S_w=D∪B_w, and similarly for s.

CLAIM: w is a rectangular bar of exact rank THREE, with successful orientation V→H→V and FAILURE of H→V→H. Consequently s is a symmetric rectangular bar of exact rank FOUR. This refutes conjecture (*) and gives first strict rank≥4 in this research project.

## w HAS V-H-V depth-three tree, so it IS A BAR

Because a∨e=1 and (a,e),(e,e) are w-leaves,
  (1,e)∈H(S_w)⊆H(V(S_w)).
Fix n. For every point x∈C=X\a (including ∞), x∈e, x≠p_n, so Hausdorffness yields a first open u_x∋x with u_x≤e and p_n∉cl_X u_x, i.e. p_n≤u_x*. Then
 (u_x,e)∈B_w, (u_x,u_x*)∈D.
Their vertical join gives (u_x,e∨u_x*)∈V(S_w). As Y_n∩F={p_n} and p_n∈u_x*, we have Y_n≤e∨u_x*, hence (u_x,Y_n)∈V(S_w).
Also (a,Y_n)∈B_w. The opens a and u_x (x∈C) cover X, so (1,Y_n)∈H(V(S_w)).
Since e∨∨_nY_n=1, final vertical root cover gives
  (1,1)∈V(H(V(S_w))).
Thus d_I∨w=1 in the FULL LOCALIC tensor product. The exact upper bound 3 does not depend on pointwise spatiality of the localic square.

## Exact horizontal fiber for w

For an open b∈O(X):
  if b⊆e, then h_w(b)=1, because both (a,b) and (e,b) are B_w leaves and a∨e=1.
  if b meets F, then h_w(b)=a∨b*.
Explanation: (a,b)∈B_w for all b. If b contains some p_n∈F and (u,b)∈B_w, every ordinary point (x,p_n) with x∈u must lie in the pointwise image of w=(a×X)∪(e×e), which at the second-coordinate p_n reduces to a×{p_n}. Therefore u≤a. Disjointness leaves with second b have first coordinate ≤b*, and (b*,b)∈D. So h_w(b)=a∨b*.

Moreover for EVERY open b∋p_n, cl_X b meets K_n⊆X\a, by the K-topology fact at p_n. Hence h_w(b)≠1. Therefore no second-coordinate H(S_w)-good open contains p_n, so
  (1,1)∉V(H(S_w)).
This rules out the V→H depth-two orientation.

## NO H-V-H depth-three tree for w

Let c∈O(X) contain ∞. Because (U_N) is a local base, c⊇U_N for some N. In particular K_n⊆c for ALL n≥N.

Fix n≥N and ANY open b∋p_n. From the previous calculation,
  h_w(b)=a∨b*.
The open b has closure hitting some k∈K_n; k∈c and k∉a, while k∉b*=X\cl b. Therefore c≰h_w(b).

Define Φ_w(c)=∨{b:c≤h_w(b)}, so (c,1)∈V(H(S_w)) iff Φ_w(c)=1. Since NO eligible b contains p_n, we have p_n∉Φ_w(c), and Φ_w(c)≠1. Thus NO V(H(S_w))-good first open c contains ∞. A horizontal cover of first top by such good opens is impossible:
  (1,1)∉H(V(H(S_w))).
In particular H→V depth TWO also fails, while the V→H→V proof above shows rank(w)=3 and w is ORIENTATION-RIGID at rank3 (only V-first).

## s is a symmetric bar of EXACT rank FOUR

Thus B_s is a genuine symmetric canonical rectangular bar.
