## A. Diagonal and separation relations

Write \(X^2=X\times_{\mathbf{Loc}}X\), \(\Delta\hookrightarrow X^2\), with sublocale order = inclusion, closure \(c\), fitting \(f\). Set
\[
\mathcal I=c(\Delta),\quad \mathcal D=f(\Delta),\quad
\mathcal V=f(\mathcal I),\quad \mathcal W=c(\mathcal D),\quad
\mathcal M=\mathcal I\wedge\mathcal D,\quad
\mathcal J=\mathcal I\vee\mathcal D,\quad
\mathcal T=\mathcal W\wedge\mathcal V.
\]
\(\mathcal H\) = **least simultaneously closed-and-fitted** super-sublocale of Δ, obtained by alternating closure and fitting. \(\mathcal U\) = closed Urysohn relation; \(\mathcal F\) = functional kernel-pair relation. \(\mathcal A\) = oriented specialization localic subrelation defined on generalized points by \(f^*(u)\le g^*(u)\) for all opens u, \(\mathcal S=\mathcal A\vee\mathcal A^\smile\). Do not confuse \(\mathcal H,\mathcal F,\mathcal A,\mathcal W\) with Arrieta's uncalligraphic axioms (H),(F),(A),(W).
