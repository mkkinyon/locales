# Sharpness of three-stage normalization for arbitrary rectangle generators on a regular continuous frame
Date: 22 October 2026.

Let I=Q×N_{≥1} be discrete and X=R ⊔ I the topological disjoint sum. The frame L=O(X)=O(R)×P(I) is regular, spatial and continuous (since X is locally compact Hausdorff). For each i=(q,n), set
 U_i=(-∞,q+1/n),  V_i=(q-1/n,∞)
as opens of the R component. Both cover R, but for any simultaneous choices W_i∈{U_i,V_i}, the frame meet ∧_{i∈I}W_i=0: every nonempty real interval J contains q and is wide enough around q relative to some 1/n that neither U_i nor V_i contains J.

Define S⊆L×L as downward closure of:
 (R,R), (I,I),
 (U_i,{i}), (V_i,{i}),
 ({i},U_i), ({i},V_i) for i∈I,
where R and I also denote the two clopen components of X.

THEOREM. The rectangles from S cover 1 in the localic square L⊕L, but (1,1)∉H(V(S))∪V(H(S)). Nevertheless (1,1)∈H(V(H(S))), so this generator family has exact minimum separation-tree edge depth 3.

Tensor cover: X×locX decomposes into 4 clopen products R×R, R×I, I×R, I×I. R×R and I×I have dedicated (R,R),(I,I) leaves. At each column i of R×I, U_i∨V_i=R covers the column, and symmetric transposed leaves cover I×R. Therefore join of S rectangles is top.

No H→V proof: restricting labels of any such proof to the R×I block gives an H→V proof for the matrix family S_RI generated by (U_i,{i}), (V_i,{i}). Its existence would imply
  1_{O(R)} ≤ ∨_{f∈{0,1}^I} ∧_{i∈I}W_{i,f(i)}=0,
impossible, by the complete-distributivity matrix criterion. The other types of leaves restrict to zero on R×I, and zero-restrictions create no new relevant rectangles. Hence (1,1)∉H(V(S)).

No V→H proof: by symmetry of S, the transposed I×R block yields the same obstruction, so (1,1)∉V(H(S)). Adjacent equal-orientation joins flatten; all depth-two trees fall into these two classes.

A constructive H→V→H proof: at horizontal root, cover X by first open R and first singletons {i}. For first R, split the second coordinate vertically into R and singletons {i}; below (R,R) use leaf (R,R), and below (R,{i}) cover R horizontally by the two leaves (U_i,{i}) and (V_i,{i}). For first singleton {i}, split second coordinate vertically into I, U_i,V_i; below ({i},I) restrict leaf (I,I), and below ({i},U_i),({i},V_i) use transposed leaves. Thus exact depth three.

SIGNIFICANCE. The previously established continuous-frame identity C(S)=H(V(H(S))) is sharp even for a regular, locally compact Hausdorff space, IF terminal relations S are ARBITRARY rectangle families. It does NOT contradict our point-free regular-frame depth-two BAR theorem: that theorem requires disjointness leaves D and bar leaves B; our sparse S omits those leaves. Indeed the tensor join of S is top, so the canonical bar B_w for w=1 contains (1,1) and has rank0, even though the particular sparse generator S needs height3.

Related theorem: A completely distributive first frame factor A gives C(S)=H(V(S)) for every S; and this property for ALL B,S characterizes complete distributivity of A. See A-V-completely-distributive-frame-universal-depth-two-2026-10-22.md.
