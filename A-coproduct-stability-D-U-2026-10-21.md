# Coproduct stability of D=U

21 October 2026. Working proofs, no novelty claim.

**Coherent theorem:** Arbitrary set-indexed coproducts of coherent frames with D=U again have D=U. For a coherent L, D=U iff K(L) is normal as a bounded distributive lattice. Coherent coproducts correspond to bounded distributive lattice coproducts on compact elements. The spectra of these coproducts are topological products. In classical spectral duality, normality means that each point closure contains precisely one closed point; this property is preserved by arbitrary topological products because singleton closures are products and closed points are coordinatewise closed. Apply the coherent criterion to the coproduct. Uses classical choice for infinite products.

**Continuous theorem:** Finite frame coproducts of continuous frames with D=U preserve D=U. For locally compact sober X,Y, closure-meeting pairs in X times Y are exactly products of closure-meeting pairs. Urysohn-related pairs in X times Y project to Urysohn-related pairs in each factor. If Cm=Ury in each factor, then Cm=Ury in their product. Local compact sobriety and spatiality of the localic product lift equality to sublocales.

**OPEN:** arbitrary frame coproducts of frames with D=U. The finite-support disjointness proof for I=U does not automatically transport the infinitely quantified square-cover criterion for D=U. Infinite coproducts of general continuous frames are not covered by the finite-product locally compact argument.
