# Claude follow-up: alternative intrinsic dual formulas for V (22 Oct 2026)

Source: user-attached alternatives.txt, Claude's reply to "Did you have any suggestions for an alternative intrinsic dual formula that might be valid in arbitrary locales?" Analysis below separates Claude's conjectures from proved facts and external verification.

## 1. Separation trees
Replace d_I∨w=1 in T=L⊕L by a well-founded C-ideal generation witness with coordinatewise arbitrary joins and leaves lying either in the rectangles generating w or in disjoint-sided rectangles generating d_I. This is a universally valid route *provided the C-ideal-to-well-founded-tree correspondence is stated and proved precisely*. It is essentially the fitted-hull definition expanded in coproduct generators: a benchmark, not an independent conceptual dual.

## 2. Graph-far primitives
Candidate enlarge strips with complements of closed graph-images of partial locale maps disjoint from I. The rational graph S=C(q) is exactly what strip formula missed. Restricted partial maps between open sublocales (e.g. L→R graph) may be informative; arbitrary maps from independent locales risk trivializing the problem. No completeness theorem presently known. Need stress test on non-graph-shaped obstructions.

## 3. Spatial-square positive class
**Proposition:** If X is Urysohn and X×loc X is spatial, then V=W. Since X sober, the canonical comparison between locale and ordinary topological square is an isomorphism; the pointwise Urysohn argument for one-stage P(w)=1 becomes a valid frame argument.

**Verified literature consequence:** Till Plewe, *Localic Products of Spaces*, Proc. London Math. Soc. 73 (1996), 642–678, abstract states: localic product of a *complete metrizable* space and a *strongly Baire metrizable* space is spatial. Completely metrizable X is strongly Baire, hence its localic square spatial. Metric X is Urysohn. Therefore **V=W for completely metrizable X**, no local compactness necessary. Source https://londmathsoc.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1112/plms/s3-73.3.642 .

**Conjecture (Claude):** For arbitrary Hausdorff X, failure of V=W implies localic square nonspatial. Not proven; absent Urysohn separation, spatiality alone does not allow the current local pointwise proof.
