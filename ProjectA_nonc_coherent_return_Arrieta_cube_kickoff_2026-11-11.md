Let V denote Arrieta's regular noncompact subspace of Cantor space, A the exotic fitted symmetric reflexive relation in V² which is the nontrivial V×V block of the fitted diagonal of Y. Prior internally checked calculation: \(A\circ A\not\le A\), but nontransitivity does **not** imply nonassociativity. Every finite parenthesization of A has only diagonal ordinary point pairs, so ordinary-point tests cannot decide the question. Boolean-valued generalized points arising from coordinate permutations are all A²-related in the sense of the previous audit's §12, hence cannot distinguish the bracketings, because both cubes contain A² (unit law plus Δ≤A).

**Exact algebraic target.** Write L=O(V), \(Q_n=\bigoplus_{i=1}^n L\), and \(G_{ij}\) for the frame congruence on Q_n generated by \(\gamma_C^{ij}=1\) for every admissible cover C, where \(\gamma_C^{ij}=\bigvee_{a\in C}a_i\wedge a_j\). The twofold composite has endpoint congruence
\[
K_2=\ker\left(Q_2\longrightarrow Q_3/(G_{12}\vee G_{23})\right),
\]
where Q_2 injects coordinates 1,3. Then the two cubic endpoint congruences are
\[
K_{\rm left}=\ker\left(Q_2\longrightarrow Q_3/(K_2^{12}\vee G_{23})\right),\quad
K_{\rm right}=\ker\left(Q_2\longrightarrow Q_3/(G_{12}\vee K_2^{23})\right).
\]
The equality \((A^2)A=A(A^2)\) is exactly \(K_{\rm left}=K_{\rm right}\). One must produce an **actual congruence separator** (a pair of Q_2 elements congruent under one quotient but not the other), or prove equality by a valid image-stability/distributivity argument. Ordinary four-point chains always map into both bracketings and cannot separate them.

**First technical objective:** look for a discriminating *nonpermutation generalized point/frame quotient* or a restricted image-stability lemma strong enough for these **particular** kernel congruences despite the failure of global surjection-conservativity, openness and properness of A's projections.
