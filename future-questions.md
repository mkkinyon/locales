## Newly parked (15 November 2026): an oriented dual below inseparability

- **A-DUAL (user's question).** Given \(\Delta\subseteq\mathcal A\subseteq\mathcal D\), is there a *conceptually dual*, natural localic relation between \(\Delta\) and \(\mathcal I=\operatorname{cl}(\Delta)\)? The internal calculation actually proves \(\Delta\le\mathcal A,\mathcal A^{op}\le\mathcal M:=\mathcal I\wedge\mathcal D\), but simple coordinate transposition is not yet a dual of the closure versus fitting mechanisms. Keep the conceptual question **OPEN/deferred**. Full formulation: [A-DUAL note](ProjectA_LCA_FOLLOWUP_AND_A_DUAL_QUESTIONS_2026-11-15.md).

- **LC-PT (current after negative LC-A).** For locally compact sober T1 X, does point-fitting imply total unorderedness \((\mathcal A=\Delta)\)? See [LC-A canonical theorem](ProjectA_LCA_COUNTEREXAMPLE_CANONICAL_2026-11-15.md); the counterexample fails both properties and does not decide the implication.
