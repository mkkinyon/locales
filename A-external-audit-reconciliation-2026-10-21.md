## 3. Scott-continuity record contradiction (corrected)

The 20 October [A-scott-continuity-converse.md](A-scott-continuity-converse.md) contains a direct proof: if A is weakly locally compact, E⊆A×B closed, then r_E=p_{B*}p_A* Scott-continuous iff p_B is topologically perfect. For the forward direction a finite-open-cover exhaustion yields neighborhoods over which E has uniform compact support, permitting the tube lemma for closedness and compactness of fibers. For a **classically continuous locale X**, spatial locally compact sober representation and product comparison identify closed localic E⊆X×X with a topologically closed relation; topological perfectness is equivalent to localic properness. Therefore

    r_E Scott-continuous ⇔ p₂:E→X proper

for any closed localic E on continuous X, without transitivity. Symmetry gives both projections proper for I. **This is a working theorem with a substantial proof, not an unresolved converse.** The October 21 ledger wrongly reopened it. The unrestricted arbitrary-frame (or independent choice-free internal) converse remains open.
