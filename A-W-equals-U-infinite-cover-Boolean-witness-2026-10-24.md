# Project A — Explicit infinite-cover Boolean witness

Working theorem, 24 October 2026. The example distinguishes D2 from D but **does not** distinguish W2 from W. No literature novelty claim.

## Construction

Let X=P(N)\{empty}, the nonempty subsets of N with the Scott topology inherited from the powerset domain. For n∈N let c_n={A∈X:n∈A}. These compact opens cover X and their finite intersections form a compact-open basis. The top element is NOT compact (no finite c_n cover X). More precisely O(X) is the frame generated by {c_n} subject to join_n c_n=1 (and the frame axioms). X is sober, algebraic, locally compact, and noncompact.

Let K=2^N, and M=RO(K), the complete atomless Boolean algebra of regular opens of Cantor space. Define A_n={z:z_n=0} and B_n={z:z_n=1}. These are complementary clopens in K and, for every infinite S⊆N,

    join_{n∈S} A_n = join_{n∈S} B_n = 1_M,

because the ordinary unions are dense open, leaving a closed nowhere-dense set of sequences constant on S.

The assignment c_n↦A_n and c_n↦B_n determines frame maps h,k:L=O(X)→M. Equivalently h(U) is the join over all nonempty finite F with c_F⊆U of meet_{n∈F}A_n; this formula exhibits arbitrary-join and finite-meet preservation directly. The analogous formula holds for k.

## Finite-cover compatibility and failure for a countable cover

Let {u_1,...,u_m} cover X. For each n the point {n} belongs to some u_j. Every Scott-open containing {n} contains c_n, so c_n≤u_j. Some u_j contains c_n for infinitely many n; therefore h(u_j)=k(u_j)=1_M. Thus h,k respect EVERY finite cover.

But {c_n:n∈N} is a cover and

    join_n (h(c_n) meet k(c_n)) = join_n (A_n meet B_n) = 0.

The induced Q=L⊕L→M factors through D2 but not through D. Hence D2≠D: finite-cover compatibility is strictly weaker than arbitrary-cover compatibility, even for an algebraic frame L with a compact-open basis.

**However this does not separate their closures.** Every nonempty Scott open contains the largest point N. Thus L is hyperconnected, d_I=d_U=0, and I=U=Q. Since I≤W≤W2≤U,

    W=W2=U=Q.

So D2>D but W2=W=U. This proves that a nonzero pointless residual in D2 need not affect the closed hull. A topological disjoint sum X⊔{isolated point} makes U a proper relation while retaining D2>D and W2=W=U blockwise, but still does not settle the original W2=U⇒W=U question.

## Published antecedent for the full-filter operator

The full diagonal-neighborhood pre-nucleus Phi(q)=join_{δ*w=1}(w→q) is exactly the operator κ_L appearing in Arrieta–Picado–Pultr (2022), Lemma 5.4 (preprint 21-17, p.14). It must NOT be claimed as a new operator. The new relationship found in our previous segment is Phi(0)=d_U, with resulting W=U iff Phi(d_U)=d_U; check the equality's priority separately. Reference: https://www.mat.uc.pt/preprints/ps/p2117.pdf .

## Next step

A witness to D2>D can be non-dense as a sublocale of U. The genuinely stronger problem is to find a witness to W2>W, i.e. an infinite cover annihilating an OPEN part of U while every binary diagonal neighborhood remains dense in U. The current example does not do this.
