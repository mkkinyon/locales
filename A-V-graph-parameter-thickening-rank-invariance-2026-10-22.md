# Parameterized graph thickening preserves every separation-tree rank
Date: 22 October 2026.

Let X,Y,K be locales, let γ:Y→X×locX be a locale map, and let q_γ be the LARGEST open of X×locX annihilated by γ*. This exists as join of all γ*-null opens. Put L=O(X), B_γ={(u,v)∈L×L:u⊠v≤q_γ}; the D leaves are (u,v) with u∧v=0. Let X_K=X×locK and γ_K:Y×locK→X_K×locX_K be given by γ_K(y,k)=((γ_1(y),k),(γ_2(y),k)). Let q_{γ_K} be largest γ_K-null open and B_{γ_K} its canonical rectangle family.

THEOREM. If K has at least one locale point k:1→K, then B_γ and B_{γ_K} have identical sets of realizable well-founded separation-tree heights; in particular the minimal rank is exactly preserved. This is true for any γ, regardless of whether its barhood is already known, and even for nonspatial X,Y.

Proof (lifting). Projection π:X_K→X is a locale map, inducing a frame hom π*:O(X)→O(X_K). On squares write π_2*:O(X×locX)→O(X_K×locX_K). The commuting diagram γ_K followed by π×π equals γ followed by projection Y×K→Y. Therefore π_2*(q_γ) is γ_K-null, so π_2*(q_γ)≤q_{γ_K}. Consequently u⊠v≤q_γ implies π*u⊠π*v≤q_{γ_K}. Disjointness is preserved since π* preserves finite meets. Mapping all labels of an original proof tree under π* yields a tree of the same height upstairs.

Proof (restriction). Fix a point k of K. The section σ_k:X→X_K, x↦(x,k), induces σ_k*:O(X_K)→O(X). The commuting square γ followed by σ_k×σ_k equals γ_K after the section Y→Y×K, y↦(y,k). Thus (σ_k×σ_k)^*(q_{γ_K}) is γ-null and hence ≤q_γ. Every B_{γ_K} rectangle leaf restricts to a B_γ rectangle leaf; disjointness preserved. Mapping labels of any upstairs proof tree by σ_k* yields a same-height proof downstairs. Together these constructions preserve every attainable well-founded tree height (at most: adding redundant unary nodes can pad height; the min ordinal rank equality is the safe statement). Therefore rank equal.

BARHOOD ASCENDS AND DESCENDS: q_γ∨d_X=1 iff q_{γ_K}∨d_{X_K}=1. Ascending: π_2*(d_X)≤d_{X_K}, and π_2*(q_γ)≤q_{γ_K}, so equation ascends. Descending: apply σ_k×σ_k to upstairs bar equation; disjointness leaf images are below d_X, q_K image below q_γ, hence downstairs bar equation. Thus rank equality holds as bar rank where applicable.

CONCRETE NONDISCRETE BOUNDARY. Take original countable Hausdorff rational-bridge X with open rational copies L,R and closed discrete bridge set Z, and K=ω+1 with the convergent-sequence topology. Since K is compact Hausdorff/locally compact, X×locK is spatial, the ordinary product. Let e=(L∨R)×K. Its closed complement is Z×K: a topological sum of compact convergent sequences, hence locally compact and NONDISCRETE. The new graph γ_K(t,k)=((ell_t,k),(r_t,k)) has null canonical bar of exact rank THREE by the theorem. In ordinary products the graph image is F×Δ_K (up to permutation), closed if the original rational graph F was closed. This is a verified nondiscrete-boundary rank-three example, showing that simply adding a compact accumulation parameter cannot amplify rank.

SECOND NO-GO (cofinite convergence of dense original bridges). Suppose the original bridge points z_n include a bridge for each rational basic interval J_n, with left and right attaching sequences whose indices lie in J_n and meet every open neighborhood of z_n. Suppose one adds a point z_∞ with z_n→z_∞, meaning each neighborhood of z_∞ contains all but finitely many z_n. For ANY neighborhood U of z_∞ and ANY nonempty rational interval J, choose n sufficiently large with J_n⊂J (infinitely many rational basis intervals are contained in J). Then U contains z_n, so openness forces U∩ℓ[J]≠∅ and U∩r[J]≠∅. Hence traces U∩L and U∩R are both DENSE in their respective rational copies. In particular U meets every neighborhood of every rational point. Therefore z_∞ cannot be Hausdorff-separated from ANY rational point; such a naive iteration cannot be Hausdorff. This is stronger than a generic warning about unexpected graph closure.

These two results motivate a rank-four search via genuine coupled nested attachments, not Cartesian thickening or cofinite accumulation of a dense global bridge family. No higher-rank example and no universal rank-three proof is claimed.
