## 1. The adopted mathematical answer

The research began with Cameron–Kumar's graph relations on topological spaces and asked what survives **point-free**, as **genuine sublocales of a localic square** rather than sets of point pairs. A point graph, the sublocale generated by its points, and a genuine localic relation can disagree.

The resulting mathematical account has **two studies**:

**Study I — Genuine binary localic relations.** Three complementary *certified regimes*, not one universal graph algebra:
1. **Coherent Priestley/patch:** proconstructible spectral sublocale relations are reconstructed from patch-closed point-order data; **actual Loc** composition associates inside this specified class and computes the coherent diagonal-hull words.
2. **Isbell Booleanization / compact closed support:** nonzero sublocales invisible to point pairs generate new genuine composability witnesses; compact Hausdorff mixed weak/full \(p/q\) rectangles obey exact **actual localic composition**, forming an associative finite-join algebra. Johnstone–Sun's weak/full distinction and Plewe's support/density method are classical. The **exact full support generation formula (G′)** is *referee-origin, post-verdict* and internally reconciled; mixed RC received a later separate structural blind assessment. No general novelty claim.
3. **Scattered-index decomposition:** for **arbitrary locale \(P\)** and **locally compact scattered Hausdorff \(K\)**, the sublocale lattice of \(P\times K\) splits into closed singleton sectors (**MAT***), and arbitrary genuine **binary** relation composites satisfy exact matrix multiplication (**MUL***). Row-and-column-finite matrices with associative coefficients form a genuine **FINMAT*** algebra. These precise theorems received their own independent blind technical review. This is a **sublocale-lattice splitting**, not a statement that \(P\times K\) is a discrete coproduct or that all unbounded matrices associate.

**Study II — Higher simultaneous witnesses.** Patched \(I_n,D_n,M_n\), chains, common witnesses, Dowker and Stone-sheaf comparisons. Binary \(I,D\) graphs **with patch topology and isomorphism hypotheses** recover the patched higher witness hulls and associated **1-type**, but do **not** determine the chain **2-type**. The general chain-to-meet comparison is **2-connective** in a **specified sufficiently large Stone-sheaf \(\infty\)-topos**, contingent on named effective Čech/realization and earlier under-\(\mathbf C\) imports. It originated **after** the fourth audit's original blind verdict and received an independent opposite-referee **cross-check**; this is **X+C**, not original E or intrinsic localic shape.

The historical **A/B/C** theorem identifiers and chronological files remain intact as source/provenance indices, rather than being forced into the new two-study exposition.

## 4. Anti-overclaim checklist (apply before reusing any theorem)

- **Actual localic relation composition is not globally associative.** Coherent proconstructible, compact rectangular and finite-support scattered matrix algebras are **restricted** positive cases.
- **MUL* is binary**. Its matrix formula holds for arbitrary binary inputs; associativity in FINMAT* needs a **restricted associative coefficient algebra** and specified support finiteness.
- **Finite** sublocale-pullback joins are preserved; **arbitrary infinite** joins need not be. Keep finite/open-cover localization and true image-preserved joins separate.
- **Point sets ≠ sublocales** in general; a nonzero pointless sublocale may determine the localic composition even when there is no ordinary middle pair.
- **Plewe/Isbell/Johnstone–Sun supply classical antecedents**. Do not present product-spatiality or the closed-support **method** as newly invented here; priority of the exact formula (G′) remains unresolved.
- **Proof ranks have distinct definitions:** \(\mathcal V\)-separation-tree depth, \(\mathcal H\)-hull iteration, and genuine relation-composition length are not interchangeable.
- **Stone-sheaf higher comparisons require their chosen site and imported Čech/realization theorems**; there is no established site-independent intrinsic locale shape theorem.
- **E is not a proxy for publication readiness** and **X is not an originally blind-audited theorem**. Grades A18 (coproduct vs other preservation) and B15 (Vermeulen L vs referee-origin nonidempotence I) were explicitly corrected in the [second provenance pass](ProjectA_ENDGAME_SECOND_PASS_47_GROUP_PROVENANCE_2026-11-21.md).
- Historical files dated *before* a refutation may call Q/Q′, universal \(\mathcal H\)-transitivity, or finite-chart necessity open; the **current ledger** supersedes these status statements.
