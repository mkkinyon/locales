## 2. Background calculus: what was actually checked

1. **(R): full localic rectangles.** The genuine pullback of full rectangles contains the middle sublocale \(W=Y_1\wedge X_2\); if \(W\ne0\), \(W\to1\) is classically an open surjection, stable under pullback, so the actual image is the **full outer product**. If \(W=0\), it is zero. Not an ordinary-point composition argument (§2.1).
2. **Finite-join distributivity (new explicit proof).** The packet assumed \(f^{-1}\) preserves 0 and binary sublocale joins. The referee proves this for an **arbitrary locale map \(f\)** in §2.2 using complemented open/closed sublocales, the nucleus decomposition by \(c(ja)\vee o(a)\), and the assembly \(N(h)\). Together with the coframe law and the image adjunction, this establishes **binary-join distributivity of genuine localic relation composition for arbitrary locales**. The proof is an added verification of a prerequisite, **not** a blanket associativity theorem or an assertion about infinite joins.
3. **Compact support (G′).** The referee independently checks **both inclusions** in the generation formula \(\displaystyle [A]_X\times_{\rm Loc}[D]_Z=\bigvee_{K\in\mathscr K(A,D)}\mathcal B(K)\) for **compact Hausdorff** \(X,Z\), including the essential *fitness and complementary open/closed* step (report §2.4) and localic support-density lemma (§2.3). The fact that (G′) **originated in an earlier post-verdict addendum** remains historically accurate. It now also has this **distinct independent focused external check**. Historical novelty/priority is not established.
4. **Dense-middle (B*) and mixed p/q (RC).** Verified via density of localic composability pullbacks and Booleanization of compact supports, with **nonzero pointless middle intersections**; ordinary middle-point intersection is not substituted. The openness of one projection cited by the candidate is stronger than is needed there: **surjectivity is sufficient** (§2.5).
5. **Associative finite-join p/q algebra, (DW) and (PA).** Referee checks the zero/empty-support cases, generator triples, finite expansion and all parenthesizations in the **restricted associative algebra**. The directed walks are of **exact** length \(k-1\); for cliques, each nonempty support gives a loop and paths can be padded, hence the distance cutoff. The localic **(PA)** formula is stronger than mere equality of point sets of words. **Global Loc relation composition remains nonassociative.** No infinite-join interchange is licensed (§2.6).
6. **Necessary pointwise test discipline.** For finite joins of sublocales, a point is join-prime; **not for infinite joins**. The referee checks this explicitly and uses spatiality of the compact-Hausdorff ambient square **only to pass from all points to all of the ambient square**, not to conclude arbitrary intermediate sublocales are spatial (§§2.0, 3.1–3.3).
7. **B unitality and finite support.** The proof tests a putative finite join \(E=\bigvee_i r_i\) against each closed singleton diagonal \(e_x\), forcing every nonzero generator to be an \(e_{x_i}\) and hence finitely many points exhaust \(G\). This excludes a unit hidden in a **finite** join of nonclosed point-generated rectangles (§4).

**Imported, not separately proved from first principles by the referee:** sublocale coframe algebra; Isbell density; regular ⇒ fit; Joyal–Tierney open-surjection pullback stability; nucleus/assembly formula; spatiality of localic products with a locally compact factor; Hausdorff sobriety (§6). This remains a **classical, not constructive**, result.
